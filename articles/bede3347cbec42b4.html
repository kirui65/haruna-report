Prime Cabinet Secretary Musalia Mudavadi has issued a pointed warning to voters in Western Kenya, urging them not to slip into political miscalculation as the 2027 General Election approaches.

Speaking in Bungoma on Monday, November 23, Mudavadi invoked two earlier political calls he made that subsequently materialised, framing them as proof that his current guidance deserves serious consideration.

He added that he had also predicted the broad-based arrangement that brought Ruto and opposition leader Raila Odinga into alignment, a development he said had since come to pass.

Building on those two predictions, Mudavadi told the gathering that Ruto would win re-election through a free and fair ballot in 2027, and he called on the region to position itself accordingly.

The PCS framed his remarks not as a political directive but as candid counsel rooted in respect for the community, stressing that he and his allies had nothing to gain by misleading the electorate.
