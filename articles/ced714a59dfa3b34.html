Gulf Energy Announces Job Vacancies in Turkana, Nairobi as Turkana Oil Project Takes Shape

Gulf Energy E&P BV, the upstream petroleum firm spearheading Kenya&#039;s first commercial oil development in the South Lokichar Basin, has announced 34 job vacancies across 21 categories as it scales up operations ahead of a defining production milestone.

The company, which operates within the broader Gulf Energy Group, published the openings on its careers portal and is seeking professionals across technical, operations and leadership disciplines.

Roles are available in both Nairobi and Turkana, with some positions rotating between the two locations.

Nairobi-based staff should expect regular field travel.

Several of the advertised positions are re-advertisements, meaning candidates who applied in the earlier recruitment round are not required to submit fresh applications.

Gulf Energy has stated that all applicants must hold Kenyan citizenship, possess relevant academic qualifications and professional certifications, and demonstrate a willingness to work in remote locations where the role demands it.

The company specifically encouraged qualified applicants from the Turkana host community to apply.

Interested candidates can access full role descriptions and submit their applications through the careers portal at gulf.energy/careers .

The deadline for all applications is December 10, 2026, at 5:00pm East African Time.

The company cautioned that only shortlisted candidates would be contacted and warned that any form of canvassing would result in automatic disqualification.
