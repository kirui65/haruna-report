Three More UDA Lawmakers Bolt to Gachagua's DCP as Transfer Window Nears Close

WhatsAppFacebookX

Three sitting Members of Parliament elected on the ruling United Democratic Alliance ticket have aligned with former Deputy President Rigathi Gachagua's Democracy for the Citizens Party, bringing the outfit's claimed tally to 25 lawmakers.

Gachagua unveiled Gatundu South MP Gabriel Kagombe, Laikipia West MP Wachira Karani, and Nyeri Woman Representative Rahab Mukami at his Wamunyoro residence in Nyeri County on Tuesday morning, terming the move a defining moment for Mt Kenya politics.

"All politics is local. A defining moment for Mlima politics. Today, at my Wamunyoro residence, I welcomed three Members of Parliament, GG Kagombe, Wachira Karani, and Rahab Mukami, who have left UDA and joined the party of the moment and the future, DCP," Gachagua stated on his official X account.

The former deputy president urged other lawmakers still allied to President William Ruto's administration to abandon the ruling party and return to his political camp unconditionally before the end of the year.

"I welcome all leaders who wish to join our party and come back home unconditionally. Our transfer window is open to everyone and will close on 31st December 2026," Gachagua declared.

He expressed confidence that the wave of defections sweeping across the Mt Kenya region will dramatically expand his party's numerical strength in the National Assembly ahead of next year's general election.

"The DCP team has grown from 19 members to 25. I have no doubt that we will have 60 MPs by next year. The future is green. Green is wealth," he added.

Although the lawmakers have not yet formally written to the Registrar of Political Parties or the Speaker of the National Assembly to resign from UDA, they are expected to formalise their exit after February 10, 2027.

Under Kenyan electoral law, resigning from a sponsoring political party within six months of the August 10, 2027 general election no longer triggers costly parliamentary by-elections, giving defectors a safe legal window ahead of the March 16, 2027 party membership submission deadline.

For Kagombe, Tuesday's formal reception at Wamunyoro caps a turbulent political transition that began in August 2026, when the Gatundu South legislator first announced his exit from UDA during a tense public rally in his constituency.

At the time, hostile residents heckled Kagombe over his previous voting record in Parliament, prompting Gachagua to declare that the lawmaker would have to undergo strict party vetting and seek forgiveness from voters before being officially admitted into DCP.

The induction of Mukami, a close ally who previously backed President Ruto in Gachagua's home county of Nyeri, alongside Karani and Kagombe deals another blow to UDA's grassroots machinery in central Kenya.

The defections come barely four days after Runyenjes MP Eric Muchangi Karemba officially joined DCP at Wamunyoro last Friday, accelerating Gachagua's aggressive bid to consolidate Mt Kenya East and West ahead of 2027.
