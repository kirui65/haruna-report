First Lady Rachel Ruto turned heads on Friday, November 21, when she showed up at the Turbo Pedaling Edition 2.0 cycling event in Maili Tisa, Uasin Gishu County , and got on a bike herself.

A video from the event captured her pedalling along with her bodyguards keeping pace close beside her, a sight that quickly caught the internet&#039;s attention.

The event brought together cyclists, sports enthusiasts, local leaders and residents to celebrate fitness, wellness and emerging sporting talent in the region.
