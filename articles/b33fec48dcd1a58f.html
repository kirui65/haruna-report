OPINION: Bayern vs Ajax: A grand spectacle for Kenya, but are our priorities right?

WhatsAppFacebookX

NAIROBI, Kenya, Nov 18 &#8211; The announcement that German giants Bayern Munich and Dutch powerhouse Ajax will face off in Nairobi on January 5, 2027, to inaugurate the newly built Raila Odinga International Stadium is undoubtedly exciting news for Kenyan football.

It is an opportunity to showcase our sporting infrastructure, attract international attention and build momentum ahead of the Africa Cup of Nations (AFCON) 2027.

However, beyond the prestige of hosting two European football giants, I believe there are bigger questions that need to be asked.

Are we investing in the right priorities, and who is this historic occasion really being organised for?

Bringing clubs of Bayern and Ajax’s stature to Kenya comes at a significant cost. While the government has indicated that corporate partners would help facilitate the event, there is still a need for greater clarity on who these partners are, their financial contributions and how the entire arrangement is structured.
