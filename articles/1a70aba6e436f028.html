"KSh 3m per year": High Commission of Canada in Kenya is hiring for trade job

The High Commission of Canada in Kenya has opened applications for a Trade Programme Coordinator, offering a gross annual salary of KSh 3,078,605 plus benefits on a full-time, indeterminate contract.

The position, classified as LE-A3 under the Locally Engaged Staff (LES) framework, is based at the High Commission in Nairobi. Only one vacancy is available, and the application window closes on November 18, 2026, at 23:59 local time.

The selection process is open to all individuals residing in Kenya who hold valid authorisation to work in the country.

Applications must be submitted in either English or French, the two official languages of Canada, and must be completed through the VidCruiter online portal .

Documents submitted through any other channel will not be accepted.

Candidates must hold a bachelor&#039;s degree from a recognised university and demonstrate advanced proficiency in English across reading, writing, comprehension, and speaking.

A minimum of three years of recent administrative support experience, gained within the last five years, is required, covering areas such as organising meetings, events, and stakeholder engagements.

At least two years of experience conducting research and building relationships with clients or partners is also mandatory.

Proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite is essential, alongside knowledge of Kenyan political, economic, and business environments. The role demands strong judgement, organisational ability, and the capacity to work with diverse teams.

Day-to-day responsibilities include coordinating administrative and clerical functions for the Trade programme, arranging travel and accommodation, scheduling meetings, supporting hospitality events, and gathering and verifying information across a range of trade-related files.

Candidates with basic French proficiency, experience using Microsoft 365, Teams, or AI-enabled tools, and backgrounds in government, diplomacy, trade promotion, or policy work will receive preferential consideration.

Before appointment, successful candidates must obtain a Reliability Status security clearance from the Government of Canada, which involves criminal and credit background checks. Medical evaluations may also be required.

The standard working week is 37.5 hours, with occasional overtime, including evenings and weekends, as well as limited domestic and international travel.

The Government of Canada states it does not negotiate salaries and that remuneration is determined by an established multi-step pay scale aligned with local labour conditions in Kenya.

Competitive benefits consistent with local practices are included in the total compensation package.
