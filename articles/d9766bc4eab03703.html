Martha Karua: Kenya’s new dawn starts now

WhatsAppFacebookX

The PLP leader thanks her supporters after her presidential declaration, calling on Kenyans to build a country worthy of their children&#8217;s future.

Martha Karua, leader of the People&#8217;s Liberation Party, has thanked Kenyans for their warmth and encouragement following her presidential declaration on Friday, 27 November 2026, calling the outpouring of support a sign of how much the country shares in common despite its divisions.

Speaking after the event, Karua addressed those who had travelled from across Kenya to witness the declaration. &#8220;To everyone who came, who stood with us, who travelled from near and far, and who gave their time, energy and heart to make today possible, thank you,&#8221; she said.

Her message struck a deliberately unifying tone at the opening of what is expected to be a fiercely contested road to the August 2027 General Election. &#8220;I know that the road ahead will not be easy, but I also know that we have so much more in common than what divides us,&#8221; she added.

At the heart of her post-declaration remarks was a vision of Kenya defined by dignity, institutional integrity and opportunity. &#8220;I believe in a Kenya where every citizen has dignity, where hard work opens doors, and where our children can look to the future with confidence.&#8221;

That Kenya, she said, is worth building together.
