Muhoozi cancels $3bn Turkish SGR contract linking Uganda to Kenya

WhatsAppFacebookX

NAIROBI, Kenya Nov 21 &#8211; Uganda&#8217;s Chief of Defence Forces General Muhoozi Kainerugaba has announced the cancellation of a multi-billion-dollar contract awarded to Turkish construction firm Yapi Merkezi to build the country&#8217;s Standard Gauge Railway (SGR), throwing the future of the regional infrastructure project into uncertainty.

Muhoozi, the son of President Yoweri Museveni, said Uganda would seek another contractor to undertake the railway project, which is expected to connect Kampala to Malaba on the Kenyan border.

In a series of posts on X , the military chief declared that the government had terminated the agreement with the Turkish company, without providing details of the circumstances behind the decision.

&#8220;We have cancelled the contract with the Turkish company to build the SGR. We will get another one that is more worthy of our country!&#8221; he wrote.

He also called on supporters of the Patriotic League of Uganda (PLU), a political organisation he chairs, to organise demonstrations outside the Turkish Embassy in Kampala on June 19.
