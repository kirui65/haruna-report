Kenya: Ruto Tells Parliament Nairobi-Mombasa Highway Dualling Plan At Advanced Stage

WhatsAppFacebookX

News summary

President William Ruto says plans to dual the Nairobi-Mombasa Highway have reached an advanced stage, with the government targeting the expansion of the approximately 460-kilometre corridor as part of a wider programme to improve transport infrastructure and lower the cost of doing business.
