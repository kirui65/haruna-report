Bien Releases Long Awaited Alusa Continua Album

WhatsAppFacebookX

Kenyan music star Bien-Aimé Baraza has officially released the visuals for his highly anticipated sophomore solo album, Alusa Continua. The visuals of the album that features many beloved Kenyan Brands includes Tusker Brand as long-standing partner in the project, will be available for viewing across various outlets in Kenya from November 27.

The 17-track album features an impressive line-up of international and regional artists, including American rapper 2 Chainz, Nigerian singer Oxlade, Ugandan musician Joshua Baraka and Tanzanian Bongo Flava star Alikiba, alongside other Kenyan performers.

Commenting on the album release, Tusker Senior Brand Manager Zipporah Ndung&#8217;u said,

&#8220;Bien has been a valued partner in our journey of celebrating and championing Kenyan talent. Our longstanding relationship reflects a shared belief in the power of local creativity and the importance of creating opportunities for homegrown talent to grow and reach wider audiences. Through initiatives such as Base to Billboardz, we have seen this commitment come to life, and we are proud to celebrate Bien and his continued contribution to Kenya&#8217;s creative industry.&#8221;

The 50-minute project explores themes of resilience, identity and personal growth, drawing on diverse musical influences, including Afrobeats, soul, rhumba and traditional Kenyan sounds.

Through Base to Billboardz(B2B), Tusker partnered with Bien to create a platform that goes beyond music discovery, focusing on developing artists with demonstrated potential and momentum in the industry. The initiative brought together six Kenyan artists, including Muringi Matheri, Manasseh Shalom, Zawadi, Chris Barr, Njuguna and Ras Amor, who came together to form the Keteh Collective.

Tusker continues to celebrate Kenya&#8217;s rich cultural identity, recognising the power of shared experiences, collaboration and collective creativity in shaping the country&#8217;s story. By supporting artists, musicians and creative voices, the brand creates platforms that celebrate our shared stories, sounds and heritage while giving homegrown talent opportunities to take Kenyan creativity to wider audiences.

Kenya&#8217;s legacy is continually shaped by those creating new sounds, challenging conventions and taking local creativity to the world. Through Tusker, KBL remains committed to championing the talent and experiences that make Kenya distinctive.
