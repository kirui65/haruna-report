Kenya Hosts Government-Endorsed Africa’s Next Super Model As Nyaliep Puot Is Crowned

WhatsAppFacebookX

Nairobi hosted the grand finale of Africa&#8217;s Next Super Model 2026 at Broadwalk Mall on Saturday, 21 November, with the competition endorsed by the Ministry of Tourism and Wildlife. Model Nyaliep Puot was crowned winner, beating 19 other contestants from across the continent. She won a $5,000 prize, global exposure and the chance to work with luxury international fashion brands.

The finale opened with the Kenyan National Anthem and remarks from founder Joan Okorodudu, CEO of Isis Models International. She thanked the Government of Kenya for endorsing and supporting the competition. The Ministry&#8217;s endorsement shows that the government values the platform&#8217;s role in nurturing talent and lifting the creative economy.

Nyandech Changath was named first runner-up and Nyayiel James second runner-up. Nyaliep follows Dillah Acelle, who won the title in 2025.

Judges singled out the new champion&#8217;s confidence, poise and professionalism. &#8220;She showed immense confidence. She is destined for great stages,&#8221; said judge Matteo Puglisi, CEO of Select Models Management. He said the competition is opening international doors for African models and connecting them to global brands, which he described as life- changing.

For Nyaliep, the win was emotional. &#8220;I can&#8217;t believe it. It wasn&#8217;t an easy journey, so winning is fulfilling. I thank everyone who supported me in this and congratulate all the other finalists,&#8221; she said.
