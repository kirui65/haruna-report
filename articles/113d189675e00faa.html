Ruto’s Sh5tn transformation plan: What has Kenya achieved one year on?

WhatsAppFacebookX

NAIROBI, Kenya, Nov 18 —President William Ruto returns to Parliament on Wednesday for his 2026 State of the Nation Address facing scrutiny over the implementation of a Sh5 trillion transformation blueprint he unveiled nearly a year ago, promising to reposition Kenya as a competitive, industrialised and export-oriented economy.

The annual address is a constitutional requirement under Article 132(1)(c), which obligates the President to report to Parliament on measures taken and progress achieved in the realisation of national values set out under Article 10 of the Constitution.

In his November 20, 2025 State of the Nation Address, Ruto outlined four priorities: investing in people, transforming Kenya into a net exporter, expanding water storage and irrigation, and upgrading energy generation and transport infrastructure.

The President estimated that the programme would require at least Sh5 trillion over a decade, proposing a National Infrastructure Fund (NIF) and a Sovereign Wealth Fund (SWF) to mobilise capital without relying excessively on public borrowing.

The blueprint included targets to construct at least fifty mega dams, bring 2.5 million acres under irrigation, add 10,000 megawatts to electricity generation capacity and tarmac 28,000 kilometres of roads.
