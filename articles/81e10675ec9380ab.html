The Future Isn’t Being Imported. It’s Being Built Here.

For too long, the narrative around African innovation has been about leapfrogging with "lite" versions of Western apps. But Nairobi is no longer just the "Silicon Savannah", it is rapidly evolving into a global testbed for frontier technologies. The era of the simple "Uber for X" is over. The next wave of valuation, the next generation of unicorns, will be driven by Deep Tech: proprietary, hard-to-replicate science and engineering that solves systemic problems at scale.

Startup Grind Nairobi Week will take place from November 23 to 27, 2026, bringing together deep tech founders, investors, engineers and regulators to explore Africa's emerging frontier technology ecosystem.

A massive gap exists in the ecosystem. Deep Tech founders, those building in AI, Blockchain, and Hardware, struggle to communicate their complex value propositions to generalist investors. Meanwhile, regulators are racing to keep pace with the rapid adoption of Crypto and AI, and users are desperate for solutions that actually understand their context.
