Real Madrid vs Celta Vigo La Liga prediction: Match preview, team news, current form & h2h

WhatsAppFacebookX

Heavyweights Real Madrid return to La Liga action looking to maintain their excellent run of form when they host Celta Vigo on Saturday evening.

Madrid are second in the table with 30 points from 12 matches, three points behind leaders Barcelona.

They have won 10 of their 12 league matches, scoring 31 goals and conceding only 10 prior to this much anticipated encounter.

Celta, meanwhile, sit 11th with 13 points, having won only two of their 12 league matches.

Their biggest problem has been at the attacking end: they have scored just 10 goals so far, although they have also conceded only 10.

Jose Mourinho&#8217;s side come into the game on an impressive eight-game winning streak, scoring 24 goals in the process.

Their opponents have won just one of their last five games and all the pressure lies on them as they seek only a second win against Madrid in their last 26 attempts.

At the Bernabéu, their form has been even more impressive. They have won their last five home league matches and are yet to drop a league point at home this season.

Here is our predicted outcome for the match.

The match kicks off at 11PM E.A.T at the Santiago Bernabeau

The biggest news for the hosts is the return of Vinícius Junior, who has completed his domestic suspension.

His return is particularly significant because Madrid have still managed to win consistently without him, but his individual ability gives the team another elite attacking weapon.

The latest squad announcement confirms that Kylian Mbappé will lead the attacking line, supported by Jude Bellingham and Arda Güler.

Aurelien Tchouameni and Bernardo Silva are also in training and are fighting for a place in the starting XI.

Borja Iglesas is the latest on the Celta injury lost and joins Miguel Roman and Alvaro Nunez on the sidelines.

Other than that, the rest of the side is fit and will be led by Oscar Mingueza and Jonathan Bamba in the final third.

Madrid have dominated the overall rivalry. Across the most recent 10 meetings, Madrid have won nine, with Celta winning one.

The rare win came in a shock 2-0 victory last December at the Bernabeau, and they are hoping to replicate that success here.

Madrid are unbeaten at home in the league, have won their last five home league matches and have won eight straight games in all competitions.

Celta&#8217;s away record makes the task even more difficult.

Madrid have scored 31 goals in 12 league matches, while Celta have scored only 10. The home side have also scored at least twice in several of their recent victories.

Prediction &#8211; Real Madrid to win and/or Over 2.5 goals
