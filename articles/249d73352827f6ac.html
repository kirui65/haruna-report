The Public Procurement Regulatory Authority (PPRA) has advertised 35 job openings across a range of professional categories, inviting qualified Kenyans to apply for positions at different levels within the organisation.

The authority announced the vacancies through an advertisement published in MyGov on Tuesday, November 17, confirming that the available roles span entry, middle and senior levels.

Positions on offer include legal counsels, compliance officers and compliance assistants, among others.

PPRA confirmed that all successful candidates will be engaged on permanent and pensionable terms, subject to satisfactory performance and retirement age requirements.

As part of its standard staffing policy, employees will initially be assigned to one directorate, department or region for a minimum of three years before becoming eligible for job rotation.

Applications must be submitted online through the PPRA recruitment module at erp.ppra.go.ke. The authority has stated that incomplete applications will not be considered under any circumstances. Candidates who make the shortlist will be required to present original academic and professional certificates for verification at the interview stage.

All applications must reach the authority by the close of business on Thursday, December 10, 2026, at 5.00pm East Africa Time.

PPRA described itself as an equal opportunity employer and encouraged applications from women, youth, persons with disabilities, and candidates from marginalised and minority communities.

The authority noted that affirmative action principles, gender balance considerations, and national values under Articles 10 and 232 of the Constitution will guide the selection process.

The authority also warned that any form of canvassing would result in automatic disqualification, and confirmed that only shortlisted applicants would be contacted for interviews.

The recruitment covers various positions across different departments.

The available jobs include drivers, business development officers and other professional roles. Applicants are required to meet the academic, professional and experience requirements outlined for each position.
