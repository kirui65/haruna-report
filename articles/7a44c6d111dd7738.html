Belgium vs Italy UEFA Nations League prediction: Match preview, team news, current form & h2h

WhatsAppFacebookX

Belgium will take on Italy in a must win UEFA Nations League encounter on Sunday evening, if they are to stand a chance at qualification.

The red devils come into the game in third place in Group A1 with nine points, one less than their opponents Italy and France.

The fixture is particularly intriguing because the two nations have already met once in this campaign, with Belgium winning 2-0 in Rome in September.

However, Italy have responded impressively since that defeat and arrive in Brussels sitting at the top of the group after an outstanding 3-1 victory over France on Thursday.

Belgium, meanwhile, kept their qualification hopes alive with a 2-1 comeback win over Türkiye.

With only the top two teams progressing to the quarterfinals, this is effectively a qualification decider for Belgium, while Italy have the luxury of knowing that a draw is enough to take them through.
