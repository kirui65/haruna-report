Criminal offences: National Police Service warns Kenyans ahead of recruitment

The National Police Service (NPS) has issued a formal public notice ahead of its Police Constable recruitment drive on December 10, 2026.

The NPS has warned Kenyans that the exercise is free of charge and that any attempt to pay or receive money in connection with the process constitutes a criminal offence.

The advertisement, signed by Inspector General Douglas Kanja Kirocho and grounded in Articles 238, 243, 244, and 245 of the Constitution, covers 423 designated recruitment centres spread across all 47 counties. \

The notice makes clear that the NPS will not tolerate corruption, bribery, or the submission of false information at any stage of the process.

The NPS stated unequivocally that recruitment is open and free to all eligible candidates. It directed the public to report any demands for payment or other malpractice to the nearest police station or through the toll-free lines 999, 911, 112, or the DCI tip line 0800 722 203.

The service assured that all reports will be handled with strict confidentiality.

The pointed message is aimed at curbing the activities of fraudsters who have in the past posed as recruitment officials to extort money from applicants.

Candidates are required to download and complete the prescribed application form from the official websites: [www.nationalpolice.go.ke](http://www.nationalpolice.go.ke), [www.kenyapolice.go.ke](http://www.kenyapolice.go.ke), [www.administrationpolice.go.ke](http://www.administrationpolice.go.ke), or [www.dci.go.ke](http://www.dci.go.ke).

Forms are also available at Huduma Centres countrywide.

On the day of recruitment, applicants must arrive at their designated sub-county centre by 8:00 a.m. and carry originals and copies of their academic certificates and testimonials, a Kenya National Identity Card, and a birth certificate.

Those reporting to the NYS Headquarters in Ruaraka must additionally present an NYS discharge certificate reflecting a minimum conduct rating of "Good."

The recruitment exercise will run from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.

To qualify, candidates must be Kenyan citizens aged between 18 and 28 years, have attained at least a D+ mean grade in KCSE with a D+ in either English or Kiswahili, and be physically and medically fit with no criminal record or pending charges.

The minimum height requirement is 5 feet 8 inches for male candidates and 5 feet 3 inches for female candidates.

Female applicants must not be pregnant at the time of recruitment or during the full training period.

The NPS urged all interested applicants to prepare their documents early, verify their designated recruitment centre, and report any irregularities through the channels provided.
