Emergency Teams Contain Factory Fire in Mavoko as Damage Assessment Begins

WhatsAppFacebookX

A fire that broke out at a garments factory in Mavoko, Machakos County, along the Nairobi-Mombasa Highway, has been contained, with no injuries reported.

In an update on Monday, November 16, the Kenya Red Cross Society said its Machakos Action Team joined several emergency response agencies in containing the fire following a coordinated response involving county and national government teams.

The response brought together fire teams from Mavoko and Machakos County, Nairobi City County, the National Youth Service (NYS), the Kenya Police and the Kenya Red Cross Society.

The humanitarian organisation confirmed that the fire had been contained and no casualties had been reported. It added that an assessment would be conducted later in the day to establish the extent of the damage.

“The fire has been contained and no injuries were reported. An assessment will follow later today,” the organisation stated.

The incident comes days after three people were rescued and taken to hospital following the collapse of a building under construction in Syokimau, Machakos County, on Friday, November 13.

The Kenya Red Cross said search and rescue operations were ongoing for another person who had been reported missing.

“Three people have been rescued and taken to hospital after a building under construction collapsed in Syokimau. Search and rescue operations are ongoing for one person reported missing,” the organisation said.

Its response team worked alongside other emergency agencies to coordinate rescue efforts at the site.

In a separate incident, at least 11 people were rescued after a wall under construction collapsed in Riverside, Nairobi County.

In an update issued on Saturday, November 14, the Kenya Red Cross said it had deployed response teams and two E-Plus ambulances to the scene. Those rescued were taken to hospital for treatment.

Meanwhile, more than 300 households in Tana River County have been affected by flooding after heavy rainfall caused a seasonal river in Wardesa to burst its banks.

The flooding affected residents in Eldera, Kono Ramadha and Mata Wardesa, while the Kono Ramadha-Mlanjo road was cut off, disrupting transport in the area.

The Kenya Red Cross said its teams were on the ground assessing the situation and working with local administrators to alert communities downstream and guide families to safer locations.

The organisation urged residents in affected areas to heed safety advisories as emergency teams continued monitoring the situation.
