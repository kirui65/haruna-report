Nairobi Governor Johnson Sakaja has reflected on his administration&#039;s campaign pledges, identifying the school feeding programme as his most challenging promise to implement.

Speaking while reviewing his 2022 manifesto, Sakaja admitted that implementing Dishi na County, his flagship school feeding programme, was among the most difficult undertakings of his administration.

The governor said the initiative demanded substantial financial and political capital, with legal challenges and negotiations threatening to delay its implementation.

Launched on August 28, 2023, Dishi na County provides subsidised, freshly prepared meals to learners in public primary schools and early childhood development centres.

Parents contribute KSh5 per meal, with the county government and development partners supporting the remaining costs. The initiative operates through a network of central kitchens, which prepare and distribute meals to participating schools.

By August 2026, the programme had served more than 85 million meals. It currently feeds over 324,000 learners daily across 230 public schools in all 17 sub-counties.

Sakaja said the initiative had become an example of how government interventions could improve children&#039;s welfare while demonstrating that campaign commitments could be fulfilled.

Looking ahead, the governor said his administration was preparing to extend the feeding programme to public day secondary schools, with January 2027 set as the target.

The review of his manifesto comes as Sakaja positions himself for another term at City Hall.

In November 2026, the governor joined the Orange Democratic Movement (ODM), ditching the ruling United Democratic Alliance (UDA) party. He defended the move, describing it as a political transition that would not affect his relationship with the broad-based government.

Sakaja also launched the Let&#039;s Talk podcast, which provides a platform for engaging Nairobi residents on development, service delivery and his administration&#039;s record.

However, the 2027 Nairobi gubernatorial contest is already attracting interest from several politicians, including Embakasi East MP Babu Owino , Embakasi North MP James Gakuya and Kasarani MP Ronald Karauri.
