Government agencies announce over 80 vacancies in major public sector recruitment drive

WhatsAppFacebookX

News summary

Major Kenyan government agencies including the Public Procurement Regulatory Authority (PPRA), Kenya Industrial Estates (KIE), and KETRACO have announced a combined total of over 80 permanent and pensionable job vacancies across the energy, SME development, and public procurement sectors. Qualified professionals are invited to submit their applications online before deadlines falling between December 4 and December 10, 2026.
