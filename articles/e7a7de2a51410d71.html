Gachagua Promises Mt Kenya Political Direction Before Christmas Break

WhatsAppFacebookX

Former Deputy President Rigathi Gachagua says the Mt Kenya region will settle on its political direction for the 2027 General Election during the Christmas holidays.

Speaking at a church service at Wonders of God in Karurumo, Runyenjes, Embu County, on Sunday, November 22, 2026, Gachagua said families and community members would discuss candidates for various elective positions during the festive season.

He said the consultations would cover the presidency, governor, senator, woman representative, Members of Parliament, and Members of County Assembly. Gachagua added that people in the region traditionally make political decisions during Christmas gatherings.

“Tukienda uchaguzi mwaka ujao every year, every five years, maamuzi hapa mlimani tunafanya wakati wa Krismasi. Si Krismasi iko karibu? Si mnang’oa maneno? Kuanzia 23rd, 24th, 25th. By 26th Boxing Day, maneno imekwisha,” Gachagua said.

He said people returning to their rural homes from Nairobi and other towns would discuss the region’s political choices with their families before reaching a consensus.

“Watu ya Nairobi wamekuja, kila mtu watu wamekaa kwa familia wameongea, ‘Ehe, hii kareba inakaa namna gani? Tuangushe ama tupitishe?’ Wanaongea, wanakubaliana. ‘Hii gavana iko namna gani?’ Wanaongea, wanakubaliana. ‘Hii MCA nani, nani, nani, nani?’ ” he said.

The Democracy for the Citizens Party (DCP) leader urged Mt Kenya residents who still support President William Ruto’s political camp to return home and take part in the anticipated discussions.

Gachagua warned that residents who stayed away could end up on the losing side once the community chose its preferred candidates.

“Kwa hivyo wale mungali mko kwa Kasongo, decision ya mlima ya vile uchaguzi wa mwaka ujao utakaa, kuanzia kiti ya Rais, gavana, seneta, women rep, MP, MCA, itafanywa hii Krismas,” he said.

He also encouraged residents with political ambitions to attend the Christmas gatherings, saying the discussions would help shape the region’s electoral position.

Gachagua said he planned to issue political direction for Mt Kenya before the Christmas break, giving residents time to discuss his position with their families. He said he expected them to review the proposal and report back after the holiday.

“Before twende Christmas, 22nd, 23rd, lazima pia nitoe mwelekeo hapa mlimani ndio muende muongee wakati ya Christmas. Ikifika 27, 28 mnirudishie ripoti,” he said.

He said his political direction would reflect his assessment of sentiment on the ground, while leaving room for residents to propose changes.

“So I want to promise before we break for Christmas, we shall give political direction for this region so that tukikula Christmas tukubaliane vizuri,” Gachagua said.
