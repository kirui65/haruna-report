Matiang’i dismisses two-camp opposition claims

WhatsAppFacebookX

NAIROBI, Kenya, Nov 22 &#8211; Jubilee Party presidential hopeful Fred Matiang’i has dismissed claims of competing political camps within the opposition, insisting that the movement is united and will field one candidate against President William Ruto in the 2027 General Election.

Matiang’i, who is positioning himself for the opposition&#8217;s presidential ticket, said leaders should not be distracted by claims of internal divisions but instead focus on consolidating their political bases and building a united front ahead of the polls.

Speaking during a church service attended by Narc Kenya leader Martha Karua and Kiharu MP Ndindi Nyoro, Matiang’i explained his association with the two leaders, saying they shared a common approach to leadership and a desire to provide solutions to Kenya&#8217;s challenges.

He described Karua as a leader he admired for her straightforwardness and firm approach to issues, adding that their shared values informed their political relationship.

&#8220;First of all, my elder sister Mother Karua is someone I always look up to because we have a lot of things in common. I like her a lot because she is also no-nonsense. She&#8217;s also focused on the things we do; that&#8217;s why I keep her company,&#8221; Matiang’i said.
