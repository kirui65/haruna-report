Equity Bank Announces 18 Job Vacancies in Kenya – November 2026

WhatsAppFacebookX

Equity Bank has announced multiple career opportunities for professionals interested in joining its operations in Kenya and supporting programmes extending across different markets. The latest recruitment drive features 18 vacancies covering banking, financial crime compliance, data and analytics, project management, climate adaptation finance, monitoring and evaluation, gender and social inclusion, youth banking and the creative economy.

The opportunities provide openings for professionals at different management and specialist levels. Several positions are focused on strengthening the Bank’s regulatory compliance and financial crime management framework, including transaction monitoring, KYC advisory, AML policies, regulatory change and data-driven compliance testing.

Other vacancies support the Africa Rural Climate Adaptation Finance Mechanism (ARCAFIM), including positions concerned with programme coordination, climate adaptation banking, gender and social inclusion, and monitoring and evaluation. Equity Bank is also recruiting professionals to support its Youth Banking and Creative Economy portfolios.

Equity Bank Kenya Limited describes itself as a technology-driven financial services institution whose operations include banking, insurance, payments, technology, investment and social impact. Its broader strategy focuses on financial inclusion, economic empowerment, innovation and sustainable development.

Applicants should carefully review the requirements attached to their preferred position before submitting an application. Closing dates vary, with some of the earliest vacancies closing on November 24, 2026.

Below are the 18 Equity Bank job vacancies currently advertised.

Location: Kenya Closing Date: December 17, 2026

Equity Bank is recruiting a Regional Coordinator for its operations in Kenya. The position provides an opportunity for a professional interested in supporting regional-level coordination and implementation.

As a coordination position, the successful candidate will operate within the structures established by the Bank for the relevant programme or business area and support effective delivery across the assigned region.

Candidates interested in this position should refer to the official Equity Bank vacancy notice for the complete job description, responsibilities and eligibility requirements before applying. This is particularly important because the vacancy information supplied for this listing does not contain the full detailed job description.

Location: Kenya Closing Date: November 27, 2026

The Senior AML Manager – Transaction Monitoring will strengthen the effectiveness of Equity Bank’s risk-based Anti-Money Laundering, Counter Financing of Terrorism and Counter Proliferation Financing transaction-monitoring framework.

The position provides Second Line governance over transaction-monitoring coverage, scenario development and calibration, alert quality and data integrity. It also supports escalation of suspicious activity to the Money Laundering Reporting Officer.

The role also maintains the Bank’s documented transaction-monitoring framework and supports regulatory inspections, independent reviews and audits.

Location: Kenya Closing Date: November 27, 2026

The Manager – Transaction Monitoring will support the assessment and strengthening of Equity Bank’s risk-based AML/CFT/CPF transaction-monitoring controls.

The position focuses on monitoring coverage, detection scenarios, alert-management standards, data completeness and escalation of potentially suspicious activity.

The position also supports regulatory inspections, independent reviews and audits involving transaction-monitoring controls.

Location: Kenya Closing Date: November 27, 2026

The Manager – Testing (Data & Analytics) will plan and execute independent, data-driven Second Line testing of financial crime and regulatory controls.

The role uses analytics and data testing to assess controls, identify exceptions and document evidence-based findings and their root causes.

The role is particularly focused on producing testing that is documented, repeatable and capable of independent review.

Location: Kenya Closing Date: November 26, 2026

The Senior Manager – Testing (Data & Analytics) will independently evaluate the design and operating effectiveness of regulatory and financial crime controls using data-driven Second Line testing methodologies.

The position leads population-based testing, analytics and continuous monitoring while providing management and Board committees with information about control effectiveness.

Location: Kenya Closing Date: November 26, 2026

This position is responsible for proactive identification, interpretation, governance and oversight of regulatory changes, compliance policies, standards and regulatory obligations affecting the Bank.

The Senior Compliance Manager will help translate regulatory requirements into clear policies, accountable ownership and effective controls.

Location: Kenya Closing Date: November 26, 2026

The Senior Manager – KYC Advisory will provide specialist risk-based advice concerning Know Your Customer, Customer Due Diligence and Enhanced Due Diligence requirements.

The position provides independent challenge and oversight of financial-crime risks associated with customers and complex relationships.

Location: Kenya Closing Date: November 26, 2026

The Manager – Regulatory Change, AML Policies & Standards will support the identification and interpretation of AML/CFT/CPF regulatory developments.

The role is also responsible for maintaining relevant policies, standards and the Bank’s regulatory-obligations register.

Location: Kenya Closing Date: November 26, 2026

The Manager – Project Management Office will coordinate governance, planning, monitoring and reporting for complex regulatory-remediation and compliance-transformation programmes.

The position is intended to ensure that commitments made to regulators are properly tracked and delivered while risks and delays are escalated through appropriate governance structures.

Location: Kenya Closing Date: November 26, 2026

The Manager – AML & KYC Advisory will provide risk-based AML/CFT/CPF and KYC advisory opinions concerning customer onboarding, retention and higher-risk customer relationships.

Location: Kenya Closing Date: November 26, 2026

The Gender & Social Inclusion Coordinator will lead the technical-assistance and inclusion agenda under ARCAFIM.

The role focuses on enabling small-scale rural producers and small and medium-sized agribusinesses to access and sustainably benefit from climate-responsive financing, with particular attention to women, youth, low-income rural producers and underserved communities.

Applicants should note that the 18 vacancies do not share a single closing date.

Applicants are encouraged to submit their applications before the relevant closing date rather than waiting until the final day.

Interested candidates should access the official Equity Bank recruitment portal, select the position that matches their professional background and carefully read the complete vacancy notice before beginning an application.

Candidates should ensure that information submitted during the recruitment process is accurate and that all documents requested through the recruitment portal are provided in the required format.

Because each vacancy has its own responsibilities and eligibility requirements, applicants should rely on the requirements stated in the individual official job advertisement rather than assuming that requirements for one Equity Bank position apply to another.

Applications for these vacancies are being handled online through Equity Bank’s recruitment system.
