Absa Bank Kenya Reports Kshs. 15.3 Billion Profit After Tax for Nine Months to September 2026

WhatsAppFacebookX

Absa Bank Kenya PLC has reported a profit after tax of Kshs. 15.3 billion for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, supported by growth in customer lending, deposits and total assets, alongside strong capital and liquidity positions.

Absa Bank Kenya PLC recorded a return on equity of 19.9%, reflecting continued business resilience and the execution of its customer-focused growth strategy amid a lower interest rate environment.

According to its nine-month financial performance, customer assets grew by 11% to Kshs. 342.5 billion, while customer deposits increased by 6% to Kshs. 408 billion.

Total assets rose by 9% to Kshs. 604.1 billion, crossing the Kshs. 600 billion milestone for the first time and strengthening the bank&#8217;s capacity to finance businesses, households and key sectors of the Kenyan economy.

The growth in customer financing reflects increased support for individuals, entrepreneurs and businesses seeking capital to invest, expand operations and achieve their financial ambitions.

Customer deposits also increased, highlighting continued confidence in Absa Bank Kenya as a financial partner and the growing relevance of its banking solutions.

The bank said its balance sheet expansion was driven by deeper customer relationships and increased access to financial services.

Absa Bank Kenya generated total revenue of Kshs. 44.7 billion during the nine-month period, supported by balance sheet growth and disciplined management of funding costs.

Net interest income stood at Kshs. 32.3 billion, recording growth in each successive quarter, while non-interest income contributed Kshs. 12.4 billion.

The bank said its third quarter delivered its strongest revenue performance of 2026, reflecting sustained business momentum despite the lower interest rate environment.

Commenting on the results, Absa Bank Kenya Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer Yusuf Omari said the performance demonstrated customer confidence and the strength of relationships built across the bank&#8217;s franchise.

“Our nine-month performance reflects the trust that customers continue to place in Absa and the strength of the relationships we have built across our franchise. In a lower interest rate environment, we remained focused on supporting our customers’ ambitions through accessible financing, innovative solutions and seamless banking experiences,” said Omari.

He added that the customer-led approach supported growth in both lending and deposits, with momentum building throughout the year.

The bank continued to strengthen its portfolio quality while maintaining disciplined cost management and investing in technology, talent and business transformation.

Its cost-to-income ratio closed at 42%, remaining within the bank&#8217;s strategic plan.

Asset quality also improved, with the non-performing loans (NPL) ratio declining to 9.8%, which the bank said remained below industry levels.

The loan loss ratio improved to 2.0%, reflecting better portfolio performance and effective risk management.

Absa Bank Kenya&#8217;s capital adequacy ratio stood at 19.3%, while liquidity reserves were maintained at 44.7%, providing a strong financial position to support future lending and business growth.

The bank&#8217;s return on equity of 19.9% also demonstrated its continued ability to generate value for shareholders.

Digital transformation remained a key pillar of Absa Bank Kenya&#8217;s growth strategy during the reporting period.

One of the notable developments was the launch of Absa Next , described by the bank as Kenya&#8217;s first integrated digital financial ecosystem.

The platform brings together banking, savings, investments, credit and lifestyle services in one digital environment accessible to both Absa customers and non-customers.

The initiative is designed to simplify access to financial services while providing customers with a more integrated experience beyond traditional banking.

Absa also expanded its affluent banking proposition through the introduction of Kenya&#8217;s first co-branded travel and lifestyle credit card, extending customer benefits into travel and lifestyle services.

Absa Asset Management Limited strengthened its investment offering through the launch of two new Global Multi-Asset Special Funds.

The funds are denominated in Kenya shillings and US dollars, providing investors with additional investment options and opportunities to diversify their portfolios.

The launches form part of the wider group&#8217;s strategy to deepen customer engagement, strengthen its investment management business and diversify revenue streams.

Strategic partnerships continued to play an important role in expanding Absa&#8217;s financial and non-financial solutions.

The bank said its collaborations enabled it to provide tailored solutions that respond to the changing needs of customers while supporting business development and economic inclusion.

In Kenya, Absa facilitated financing for solar-powered cold storage solutions aimed at addressing gaps in the agricultural value chain and reducing post-harvest losses.

The initiative seeks to strengthen agricultural productivity by improving storage infrastructure and helping farmers and other value-chain players preserve their produce.

The bank also supported the third edition of a hackathon in Kenya through an established partnership, providing a platform for young women to develop technology-driven solutions to practical challenges.

These initiatives demonstrate the bank&#8217;s continued focus on using financial services and strategic partnerships to promote innovation, sustainability and inclusive economic growth.

Absa Bank Kenya said it would continue investing in digital platforms, strategic partnerships and customer relationships as it pursues its ambition of becoming the financial partner of choice.

The bank&#8217;s strategy is anchored on its purpose of Empowering Africa’s tomorrow, together… one story at a time , with a focus on delivering customer-led innovation and sustainable value for customers, communities and shareholders.

“As we execute the next phase of our strategy, our ambition remains clear on being the financial partner of choice by delivering customer-led innovation, market-leading solutions and sustainable growth across all our businesses,” said Omari.

He noted that the bank&#8217;s strong capital position, expanding digital ecosystem and diversified franchise provided a solid foundation for capturing emerging opportunities and creating long-term value.

Absa Bank Kenya&#8217;s nine-month results highlight the resilience of its banking operations, supported by balance sheet expansion, improving asset quality, digital innovation and continued investment in Kenya&#8217;s economic development.
