Content creator Mungai Eve has gone public with details of her hair restoration journey after a consultation at a clinic revealed more than she had bargained for.

In a video shared on November 17, Eve sat with a doctor who examined her scalp using a head-mounted dermatoscope, walking her through the results of an earlier scan in a candid session that she chose to bring her audience into.

Eve had initially come in dealing with traction alopecia, a form of hair loss caused by physical manipulation of the hair. However, the scan uncovered a separate and more complex condition called CCCA, Central Centrifugal Cicatricial Alopecia, an inflammatory condition of the scalp that the doctor described as very common among African men and women.
