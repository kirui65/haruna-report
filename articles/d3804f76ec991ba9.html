Governor Kihika commissions water, agricultural projects in Kuresoi North

WhatsAppFacebookX

The Nakuru County government is implementing various development projects in water, agriculture, healthcare, education and road infrastructure across Kuresoi North Sub-County.

The initiatives are aimed at improving residents&#8217; livelihoods, boosting agricultural production and expanding access to essential public services.

During a development tour of the sub-county, Governor Susan Kihika commissioned the Nyakinywa Water Project at Nyakinywa Centre and the Sirikwa Agricultural Aggregation Centre, while inspecting other ongoing development projects aimed at improving service delivery to residents.

Speaking during the commissioning of the projects, Kihika said her administration was prioritizing investments that directly address the needs of residents, particularly access to clean water, better agricultural markets, improved healthcare and education facilities.

She said the Nyakinywa Water Project, whose first phase involved drilling and solarising a borehole, installing pipelines and constructing a storage tank, was expected to serve more than 5,000 residents and local institutions.

“Access to reliable water is essential not only for domestic use, but also for supporting agricultural production and improving the livelihoods of our people,&#8221; she said.

The Governor highlighted that the county had also invested in agricultural infrastructure through the Sirikwa Agricultural Aggregation Centre, which seeks to improve produce handling, eliminate exploitative brokers and increase farmers&#8217; earnings.

She noted that the facility had helped raise potato prices from Sh1,200 to Sh7,000 per bag by improving aggregation and market access.

Kihika explained that her administration is constructing agricultural aggregation centres across the county to leverage value addition and increase farmers&#8217; incomes.

She observed that the Sirikwa facility demonstrates how organised marketing and improved produce handling can transform farmers&#8217; earnings.

&#8220;I have directed that part of the facility be utilised for the last-mile distribution of subsidised fertiliser to ensure farmers access essential farm inputs more conveniently. Our intention is to replicate this model across all 55 wards,&#8221; she said.

Kihika further highlighted investments in road infrastructure, saying the county had allocated resources towards grading and gravelling access roads and constructing Shalom Bridge to improve connectivity and facilitate the movement of agricultural produce to markets.

On healthcare, she said construction of a modern maternity wing at Sirikwa Health Centre will improve maternal healthcare services and provide mothers with a dignified environment for childbirth.

She added that Choronok and Gacharage dispensaries were undergoing renovations, while Kuresoi North Level Four Hospital was nearing completion.

In education, the Governor said the county had completed the Aerial, Karima and Kiletien Early Childhood Development Education (ECDE) centres and would introduce free uniforms for all ECDE learners starting January.

She said the county had also enrolled 1,061 learners in the Uji feeding programme and allocated Sh1.5 million in bursaries to support needy students.

The Governor also highlighted the distribution of avocado seedlings, sunflower seeds and maize seeds to farmers to promote crop diversification, improve food security and create additional income opportunities.

She said the county was also working to improve security and commercial activities through the installation of high-mast floodlights and streetlights in key trading centres.

Kihika further noted that the Gacharage Economic Stimulus Programme (ESP) Market was at the planning stage, while the county continued to prioritise the development of markets to provide traders with better working environments.

In Nyota Ward, she reported that six water projects had been completed, including the Chesirikwa Water Project, with four others ongoing.

The county had also completed seven road upgrades and constructed a footbridge at Kabaris-Chesirikwa.

Other achievements included the completion of 11 ECDE centres, construction of four sanitation blocks and the installation of 10 floodlights and streetlights across major commercial centres.

The Governor added that the county was fast-tracking title deeds for 306 residents in Sitoito and Langwenda while securing land for public utilities.

She also urged residents to support President William Ruto&#8217;s development agenda, citing the 26 ongoing Economic Stimulus Programme (ESP) markets across Nakuru County.

Kihika reaffirmed that the projects form part her administration’s wider development agenda aimed at strengthening agricultural productivity, improving access to essential services and expanding economic opportunities across Kuresoi North Sub-County.
