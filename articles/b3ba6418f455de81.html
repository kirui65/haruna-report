Bien Baraza has opened up about the deal he struck with his wife, Chiki Kuruka, to get her on board as his full-time manager, and it involved a very specific promise: land and a house in Karen.

The Sauti Sol band member was speaking during a candid CTA interview, shared on TikTok on November 17, 2026, where he reflected on his touring career, his management journey, and what it took to bring Chiki into his corner professionally.
