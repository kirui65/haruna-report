Media personality Oga Obinna has set Kenyan social media alight once more after posting a breezy car reel that brought DNA testing back into the conversation.

In the clip, shared on Friday, November 20, via his Instagram handles @ogaobinna and @theobinnaz, Obinna is behind the camera inside a moving luxury SUV with an open sunroof.

Seated beside him is a young man dressed in a dark polo shirt and a blue Titleist visor. The two laugh, gesture and chat in a mix of Swahili and English about food, fatherhood and responsibility. Throughout the footage, on-screen text poses the question:

The tone is playful rather than confrontational. No child is named, no test result is shared, and there is no formal announcement of a scheduled paternity test.

The reel reads more like a teaser for an upcoming YouTube episode of the Obinna Show Live than any real-life accusation.

The reason the video has generated so much buzz is that DNA and paternity are topics Obinna has made his own in recent months, both in his personal life and on his platform.

He has previously spoken publicly about demanding a DNA test for his daughter Ada after her mother&#039;s lawyer sent him a request for KSh 54,000 in monthly child support.

Obinna said the financial pressure, combined with noticing that Ada appeared significantly lighter-skinned than him, prompted the request. The test eventually confirmed Ada was his, and he later had an open conversation with her about it.

Beyond his own family matters, Obinna has emerged as one of Kenya&#039;s most vocal advocates for mandatory paternity testing. In a video posted in September, he called for DNA tests to be compulsory at birth and argued that women who knowingly mislead men about paternity should be required to repay every expense incurred in raising the child.

No such legislation currently exists in Kenya.

His show has also hosted some of the country&#039;s most talked-about DNA storylines, including the Stevo Simple Boy saga, in which a DNA test eventually excluded Stevo as the biological father of his son Dragon Junior.
