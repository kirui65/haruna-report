Gachagua gives Mt Kenya leaders Christmas deadline to dump Ruto, promises political direction

WhatsAppFacebookX

NAIROBI, Kenya, Nov 22 &#8211; Former Deputy President Rigathi Gachagua has warned Mt Kenya leaders still supporting President William Ruto that they risk being rejected by voters in the 2027 General Election, saying the region will settle its political direction during the Christmas festivities.

Gachagua said political decisions in the Mt Kenya region are traditionally made during family gatherings over Christmas, when relatives return home and deliberate on the leaders they want to represent them.

Speaking in Embu County, the Democracy for the Citizens Party (DCP) leader said discussions held between December 23 and December 26 would determine the political fate of leaders seeking elective positions in the region.

He warned politicians who have remained in Ruto&#8217;s camp despite growing political differences with the former Deputy President to reconsider their positions before the festive season.

“Here in the mountain, let me tell those who don&#8217;t know: when we go to the election next year, every five years, decisions here in the mountain are made during Christmas. Starting from December 23, 24, 25, by the 26th on Boxing Day, the decision is concluded,” Gachagua said.
