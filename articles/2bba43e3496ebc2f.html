Tanzania vs Nigeria AFCON qualifier prediction: Match preview, team news, current form & h2h

WhatsAppFacebookX

Nigeria travel to Tanzania for their 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifier second leg on Tuesday evening, needing a win to take charge of Group L.

The Super Eagles won the first leg 3-1 at home last Friday, leaving them in second place with six points, one less than leaders Guinea Bissau.

Tolu Arokodare was the difference-maker, scoring twice after coming off the bench, while Alhassan Yusuf had given Nigeria an early lead. Tanzania equalised through Alphonce Msanga before Nigeria eventually pulled away.

Tanzania have already secured qualification as one of the co-hosts of the tournament, but remain rooted to the bottom of the standings with one point.

A victory in Zanzibar would take Nigeria to nine points, temporarily moving them level with or potentially ahead of Guinea-Bissau depending on the latter&#8217;s result.

Tanzania , by contrast, have much less at stake in terms of qualification because their place at the finals is already guaranteed.

The East African side have never beaten Nigeria in the past and the visitors have plenty more at stake here, making it a difficult outing for Tanzania.

Will Nigeria make it back-to-back wins to take charge of the group and their qualification hopes? Here is our predicted outcome for the match.

The match kicks off at 4PM E.A.T at the Amaan Stadium in Zanzibar

Dickson Job and Clement Mzize were ruled out through injury before the November international window, while Feisal Salum is also unavailable.

There is also a fitness concern surrounding Ibrahim Hamad, who picked up a knock in the first match.

That could force coach Miguel Ángel Gamondi to make changes to his side, with Alphonce Msanga, who scored Tanzania&#8217;s equaliser set to break into the starting XI.

The Super Eagles remain without Victor Osimhen, who is recovering from knee surgery. His absence has placed greater responsibility on Arokodare, Akor Adams and Taiwo Awoniyi.

Calvin Bassey is expected to be available after recovering from the cramp that forced him off during the first match.

The rest of the side will remain unchanged from the first leg.

From the seven matches played between these nations, Nigeria have won five times with two resulting in a draw.

The Super Eagles have won their last three matches against their opponents, scoring six goals and conceding twice.

The last two matches between these two sides has seen both teams scoring, and with home advantage in their favor, Tanzania are expected to score at least once here.

There is more pressure on Nigeria to bag all three points so they are tipped to attack throughout the encounter and are favorites to avoid defeat here.
