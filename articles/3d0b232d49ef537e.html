SIBOH School and Sports Academy: Details of New Private School Launched Inside Ruto’s Koilel Farm

A new private institution combining academic learning with elite sports training has been officially opened on President William Ruto&#039;s Koilel Farm in Moiben, Uasin Gishu County .

First Lady Rachel Ruto presided over the launch of SIBOH School and Sports Academy Wednesday, November 18, at the campus along Naiberi Road.

The ceremony drew senior government officials including Basic Education Principal Secretary John Ololtuaa and Sports Principal Secretary Elijah Mwangi, alongside a number of political leaders.

Speaking at the launch, the First Lady described the president as the driving force behind the project, recalling how the site looked before construction began.

Rachel noted that SIBOH was conceived to "nurture dreams, discover talents, and prepare future generations for greatness," and that the school represents more than an investment in education and sports; it is an investment in the future of our children and our nation.

Rachel Ruto gives glimpse of new elite school and sports academy owned by William Ruto

The idea of the institution took shape in 2022, with the site selected in 2023 and construction and staff recruitment following in subsequent years.

SIBOH is an acronym for Servanthood, Integrity, Bravery, Optimism and Honour.

The Christian-based institution will teach under Kenya&#039;s Competency-Based Education framework across Pre-Primary, Primary, Junior School and Senior School levels, with the Senior School offering STEM, Social Sciences, and Arts and Sports Science pathways.

Drawing on Eldoret&#039;s status as a global distance-running hub, the academy will provide professional coaching in more than 15 disciplines, including athletics, football , rugby, swimming, basketball, volleyball, tennis, badminton, golf and cricket.

The campus features smart classrooms equipped with interactive touchscreen display boards, a purpose-built early-years hall with play structures and wooden shelving, a wellness centre staffed by qualified medical personnel, and boarding facilities operating a Farm-to-Fork catering approach.

The school also has safeguarding policies covering child protection, anti-bullying and digital safety.

Rachel revealed that the institution was in the process of hiring teachers and other staff.

The academy will admit its first learners in January 2027, with registration currently open.

Some sources indicate that termly tuition and meal fees range from KSh 90,000 at Pre-Primary level to KSh 370,000 depending on grade. Additional recurring charges include KSh 5,000 for medical cover and KSh 5,000 for enrichment programmes per term, while optional transport costs KSh 10,000 or KSh 15,000 return.

The school is led by Executive Director Victor Boiyo. Charlene Ruto was introduced at the launch as the institution&#039;s patron.

The corporate entity and shareholding structure behind SIBOH have not been publicly disclosed.
