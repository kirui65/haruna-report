Omar Abdihamid Shares Insights on the Future of Oncology at KESHO 2026

WhatsAppFacebookX

Omar Abdihamid , Clinical Oncologist at Shabaha Cancer Centre and Clinical Oncology Specialist at Garissa Regional Cancer Centre, shared on LinkedIn :

“Honoured to be part of the 9th Kenya Society of Haematology and Oncology (KESHO) KICC Conference , happening here in Mombasa, Kenya.

It was a privilege to contribute as a speaker and a scientific committe member and engage with colleagues from across region and beyond on the issues shaping the future of cancer care.

Conferences like KICC are more than just scientific meetings &#8211; they are spaces where we exchange ideas, challenge conventional thinking, build collaborations, and most importantly, ask how we can translate evidence into better outcomes for our patients.

As oncologists, our responsibility goes beyond knowing the latest evidence. We must also ask : How do we make that evidence work in the realities of our health systems and for the patients we serve?

Grateful for the opportunity to learn, share, and connect with so many inspiring colleagues.

KESHO (Kenya Society of Haematology and Oncology) is a professional organization dedicated to improving cancer and blood disorder care in Kenya through education, research and collaboration.

Its 9th Kenya International Cancer Conference (KICC 2026) took place on November 19–21 in Mombasa, bringing together oncologists, researchers and other healthcare professionals from across the region and beyond.

The conference focused on improving access to cancer care, advancing research, sharing clinical knowledge and strengthening healthcare systems, with an emphasis on turning scientific evidence into better outcomes for patients.
