Ruto takes veiled swipe at Gachagua over Mt Kenya voting bloc politics

WhatsAppFacebookX

NAIROBI, Kenya, Nov 18 — President William Ruto has taken a veiled swipe at former Deputy President Rigathi Gachagua over his political mobilisation in the Mt Kenya region, declaring that no community should be reduced to a voting bloc.

In his State of the Nation Address to Parliament on Wednesday, Ruto challenged political leaders against asking entire regions to surrender their political choices to individual personalities, insisting that voters should assess leaders on their proposals and performance.

“No community should be reduced to a voting bloc. No region should be asked to surrender its judgement to a personality,” he said.

Although he did not mention Gachagua by name, the remarks came amid the former Deputy President&#8217;s efforts to consolidate the Mt Kenya region behind his Democracy for the Citizens Party (DCP), which he has increasingly presented as an alternative political home for the region.

Gachagua has repeatedly described Mt Kenya as a DCP stronghold and urged residents to reject the ruling party and incumbent lawmakers allied to it, whom he has branded &#8220;traitors&#8221;.
