Fresh look: Popular Kenyan company takes on new identity after over 60 years

Kasuku, the stationery brand that has been a fixture in Kenyan classrooms for over six decades, has relaunched with a new brand identity and positioning line, signalling a strategic shift from product supplier to purpose-led education brand.

The relaunch took place on November 12, 2026, at the Mövenpick Hotel in Westlands, Nairobi, in the presence of media, education stakeholders, and partners.

The event was organised by Twiga Stationers & Printers Ltd, the company that owns the Kasuku brand.

Chief executive Asitha Samaraweera framed the rebrand as both a continuation of the brand&#039;s heritage and a deliberate expansion of its story.

The updated visual identity retains the brand&#039;s core product promise of quality stationery, but the more significant change is in how the brand presents itself.

The new tagline, "Dream. Learn. Become", is built around a three-stage narrative tied to childhood development.

As part of the rebrand, Kasuku announced an upcoming initiative called Share Your Dream, which will invite children across Kenya to share their aspirations and encourage one another.

The company described it as an early expression of its wider purpose, though no launch date, format, or delivery channel has been confirmed.

The pivot towards purpose-led engagement is a familiar strategy among consumer brands competing for the loyalty of parents, teachers, and learners.

For Kasuku, it also offers a way to differentiate itself from cheaper, unbranded stationery imports that have put pressure on margins across the sector.

Kenya&#039;s stationery market is competitive, with brands contending on price, quality, availability, and emotional relevance, particularly during the back-to-school season.

A heritage brand carries the advantage of recognition and trust, but those qualities must be actively converted into shelf presence and consumer preference in an environment where supermarkets, wholesalers, and online retailers all stock rival products.

The company has not disclosed the cost of the rebrand, any sales targets, or plans for expansion into new markets.

How effectively Kasuku translates its new positioning into consistent products and community programmes will ultimately determine whether the relaunch delivers lasting results.
