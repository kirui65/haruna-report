UK Embassy Kenya Announces Deadline for 2027 Fully Funded Commonwealth Scholarships Applications

Kenyan graduates seeking postgraduate education in the United Kingdom have until December 8, 2026, to apply for fully funded master&#039;s degrees under the 2027 Commonwealth Shared Scholarships programme.

The British High Commission in Kenya publicised the opening of applications on November 18, 2026, through its official X account.

The programme is jointly funded by the UK Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office (FCDO) and participating UK universities.

The award is designed specifically for talented candidates from low- and middle-income Commonwealth countries who could not otherwise afford postgraduate study in the UK. It typically runs for 12 months, covering an eligible one-year master&#039;s programme.

Scholars receive full tuition fee payment, return airfare between Kenya and the UK, a monthly living allowance, and a one-off warm clothing allowance. Based on published 2025/2026 rates, the monthly stipend stood at £1,452 for students outside London and £1,781 for those at London-based institutions, with a £522 clothing allowance.

These figures may be revised before the 2027 intake begins.

The FCDO also covers the Immigration Health Surcharge, giving scholars access to the National Health Service, though some dental, optical, and prescription costs may still apply.

Six development themes anchor the 2027 awards: science and technology, population health, innovation and entrepreneurship, peace and governance, crisis resilience, and access and inclusion.

Approved courses span institutions including King&#039;s College London, the London School of Economics, the University of Edinburgh, and Sheffield Hallam University, among others.

To be eligible, applicants must hold Kenyan citizenship or permanent residency, possess at least an upper second-class honours degree (or a lower second-class degree supplemented by a postgraduate qualification), and demonstrate that they cannot fund UK postgraduate studies independently.

Candidates must not have studied or worked in a high-income country for a year or more and must be ready to begin studies in September or October 2027. There is no age restriction.

Applications are submitted through the CSC Central online portal. Candidates must identify an approved course, apply directly to the relevant university, register on the portal, and upload supporting documents, including academic transcripts, a valid identity document, and at least two signed references.

The commission accepts no applications by email or post and charges no application fee.

After the December 8 deadline, participating universities will review candidates and forward nominations to the commission in March 2027.

Selection weighs academic merit, the quality of the study proposal, and the potential development impact on Kenya following the scholar&#039;s return home.

Scholars are required to return to Kenya within one month of completing their studies and cannot use the award as a pathway to remain in the UK for employment.

The scholarship does not automatically cover costs for accompanying dependants.

The CSC has cautioned applicants against fraudulent agents offering guaranteed selection in exchange for payment, noting that all applications are handled directly by the commission and its partner universities.
