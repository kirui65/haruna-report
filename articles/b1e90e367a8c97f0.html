JIF Circularity Call 2027: Funding and Business Partnership Opportunities for African Circular Economy Enterprises

WhatsAppFacebookX

African businesses working in plastic waste management, recycling, electronic waste recovery and sustainable manufacturing have an exciting opportunity to develop innovative commercial partnerships with European companies through the Joint Innovation Facility (JIF) Circularity Call 2027 .

The initiative connects African circular economy businesses with European partners to develop commercially viable joint ventures, introduce innovative technologies and scale sustainable business solutions across East and Southern Africa.

Implemented through a collaboration between Project SPARK and SWITCH-2-CE, the initiative aims to improve access to financing, technical expertise, international markets and strategic business partnerships for enterprises operating in the circular economy.

The programme focuses on two priority value chains: plastic packaging and plastic waste, as well as electronics and electronic waste.

Eligible African businesses are encouraged to submit Expressions of Interest before 29 January 2027 . Applicants who successfully pass the initial screening will be invited to submit full grant proposals by 28 February 2027 .

The Joint Innovation Facility Circularity Call is a regional initiative designed to strengthen circular economy businesses through strategic cooperation between African and European companies.

The programme operates under the SWITCH-2-CE in East and Southern Africa Financing Facility, implemented by Expertise France and the Deutsche Gesellschaft für Internationale Zusammenarbeit (GIZ) on behalf of the European Union.

It combines the complementary approaches of Project SPARK and SWITCH-2-CE to address financing and business development challenges affecting circular economy enterprises.

Many African businesses developing sustainable solutions face difficulties accessing appropriate financing, advanced technologies, international markets and experienced business partners.

The JIF Circularity Call seeks to address these barriers by bringing together African entrepreneurs and European companies capable of developing commercially sustainable partnerships.

Through catalytic funding, technical assistance, business connections and market access opportunities, the initiative aims to help promising circular economy solutions move from development and testing towards commercial expansion.

The programme seeks to accelerate the transition towards a more resource-efficient and sustainable economy by supporting commercially viable circular business models.

The initiative places particular emphasis on partnerships that demonstrate clear commercial potential and contribute to measurable environmental and economic benefits.

The JIF Circularity Call concentrates on two priority value chains.

The programme welcomes innovative businesses developing solutions that reduce plastic pollution, improve recycling systems and encourage sustainable packaging.

The second priority value chain focuses on improving the management, recovery and reuse of electronic products and waste.

The call welcomes upstream, midstream and downstream business solutions, with particular interest in upstream and midstream approaches.

This means partnerships addressing sustainable product design, material sourcing, waste prevention, processing and manufacturing are particularly relevant.

The JIF Circularity Call is open to partnerships involving eligible African businesses and European companies.

The lead applicant must be an African for-profit entity legally based in one of the designated priority countries.

Every consortium must include an EU-based for-profit company registered in one of the 27 European Union Member States.

Consequently, eligible African businesses must establish or demonstrate a partnership with a qualifying European company as part of their proposed joint venture.

The JIF Circularity Call follows a two-stage application process.

Interested African businesses and their prospective European partners must submit an Expression of Interest.

Expressions of Interest will be reviewed on a rolling basis. Early submission is encouraged.

Applicants who successfully pass the initial pre-screening process will be invited to submit a complete grant proposal.

Only applicants who successfully complete the initial screening can proceed to the final grant proposal stage.

Businesses should ensure that their proposed partnerships demonstrate commercial viability, technical feasibility and alignment with the priority circular economy value chains.

Interested businesses should visit the official Project SPARK website and access the JIF Circularity Call application section.

Applicants should review the eligibility requirements, partnership conditions, application instructions and supporting documentation before submitting an Expression of Interest.

Project SPARK is a regional initiative implemented by GIZ on behalf of the German Federal Ministry for Economic Cooperation and Development (BMZ).

It strengthens African innovation ecosystems by supporting research-based, climate-smart and gender-responsive innovation.

Its work includes improving access to finance, markets, technical expertise and strategic partnerships.

The SWITCH-2-CE programme promotes an inclusive transition towards a circular economy across East and Southern Africa.

The initiative supports green business models, improved resource efficiency, climate action and sustainable economic opportunities, with particular emphasis on plastic and electronic waste value chains.

The programme is co-funded by the European Union and BMZ and implemented by GIZ in partnership with Expertise France.

The JIF Circularity Call 2027 presents an important opportunity for African businesses seeking to expand sustainable operations through international partnerships, technical collaboration and improved access to financing.

Enterprises operating in plastic packaging, plastic waste management, electronics recycling and electronic waste recovery are particularly encouraged to explore the opportunity.

Eligible businesses in Ethiopia, Kenya, Malawi, Mozambique, Namibia, Rwanda, Tanzania, Uganda, South Africa and Zambia should identify suitable European partners and prepare their Expressions of Interest ahead of the January 2027 deadline.
