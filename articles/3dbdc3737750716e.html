A video circulating online has captured the moment a visibly furious woman took a blue spade to a parked car&#039;s windshield in Ruiru, sending shards of glass flying and leaving Kenyans with plenty to say.

The footage, which went viral on Friday, November 27, shows the woman dressed in a brown long-sleeve top and dark trousers, approaching a silver-grey Nissan Note.

The car was parked along a dirt road beside St. Michael & All Angels Church Ruiru Parish, with lush green foliage and church service signage visible in the background.
