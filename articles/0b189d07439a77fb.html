Kenya Police FC hold off Posta Rangers to secure 2-1 victory in FKF Premier League

WhatsAppFacebookX

Kenya Police FC secured a valuable 2-1 victory over Posta Rangers in an FKF Premier League encounter at Police Sacco Grounds on Sunday, November 15, 2026, with Nana Asante and Eric Zakayo scoring to hand the Law Enforcers maximum points in front of their home supporters.

The hosts made a positive start to the encounter, showing attacking ambition as they sought to impose themselves against a Posta Rangers side looking to collect points away from home. The opening exchanges saw both teams compete for control, with the visitors attempting to contain Kenya Police&#8217;s attacking threat.

The breakthrough eventually came through Nana Asante, who found the back of the net to put Kenya Police ahead and give the hosts an important advantage. The goal shifted the momentum in favour of the Law Enforcers, who continued pressing for another opportunity to extend their lead.

Their persistence paid off when Eric Zakayo scored the second goal, giving Kenya Police a 2-0 advantage and placing Posta Rangers under considerable pressure. The two goals gave the hosts a strong position as the match entered a crucial stage.

However, the contest became more complicated for Kenya Police following a red-card incident that left the Law Enforcers with a numerical disadvantage. The dismissal forced the hosts to contend with a difficult situation as Posta Rangers sought to capitalise on having more players on the pitch.

The Mailmen responded by increasing their attacking efforts, searching for a route back into the match. Their persistence produced a breakthrough through Mark Shaban, whose goal reduced the deficit and brought Posta Rangers back into contention. With the score at 2-1, the visitors had an opportunity to put further pressure on the hosts and search for an equaliser. Kenya Police, however, remained determined to protect their advantage and avoid surrendering the lead.

The closing stages required concentration from the Law Enforcers as they attempted to manage the visitors&#8217; attacking threat. Despite Posta Rangers&#8217; efforts to force a comeback, Kenya Police maintained their advantage until the final whistle.

The result represents an important boost for Kenya Police in their FKF Premier League campaign, particularly after navigating the challenge of playing with a numerical disadvantage. Their ability to preserve the lead ensured that the three points remained at Police Sacco Grounds.

For Posta Rangers, the defeat will be a disappointing outcome after scoring to revive their hopes but failing to find a second goal that could have earned them a share of the points.
