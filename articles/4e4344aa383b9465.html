Nairobi Court Sentences Man to 20 Years in Jail or KSh 50m Fine for Dealing in Narcotics

A man convicted of traffickin*g in narcotics has been handed a KSh 50 million fine or a 20-year prison sentence by a Nairobi court, in a ruling NACADA has welcomed as a major boost to the fight against illicit substances.

Stephen Ngii presiding over JKIA Law Court 1, delivered the judgment on Wednesday, November 25, 2026, finding Samson Gathuki Njeri guilty of the offence.

The case stemmed from an operation conducted nearly a year earlier, which led to the recovery of dozens of packages containing suspected narcotics.

According to the National Authority for the Campaign Against Alcohol and Drug Abuse (NACADA), its enforcement officers, working alongside officers from the National Anti-Narcotics Investigation Bureau (NANIB), arrested Njeri on December 20, 2025.

The suspect was apprehended in the Kimbo backstreet area of Juja, Kiambu County , during a joint operation.

Officers recovered 96 packages containing dry plant material suspected to be narcotics, alongside KSh 6,600 believed to be proceeds of crime.

Subsequent forensic analysis confirmed that the seized substance weighed 4,510.08 grammes and had an estimated street value of KSh 135,302.40.

The court further ordered that the KSh 6,600 be forfeited to the State. The narcotic exhibits are to be destroyed after 14 days, except for samples retained for record purposes.

NACADA Chief Executive Officer Dr Anthony Omerikwa welcomed the ruling, crediting the public for providing information that helped authorities arrest and prosecute the suspect.

He also emphasised the importance of community involvement in tackling drug traffickin*g, noting that information volunteered by members of the public had helped trigger the operation.

Omerikwa said NACADA is committed to pursuing and prosecuting individuals involved in the narcotics trade across the country.
