One year after the death of beloved Kenyan gospel singer Betty Bayo, her family gathered at Mugumo for what should have been a straightforward memorial, only to find themselves locked in a tense standoff over access to the late musician&#039;s home.

In a TikTok video filmed by MBC TV, Bayo&#039;s siblings and other family members spoke, describing a situation that had clearly left them shaken and frustrated.

His brother John Mbugua claimed that the family had made the trip specifically to honour Bayo on her first anniversary. He said they had been attending for her and expected to do so for many years to come.

Betty Bayo&#039;s sister, Irene Wanjiku, also appeared in the video and confirmed they had formally invited Bayo&#039;s children to the event, sending letters ahead of time.

She added that the family had gone a step further by notifying the local Officer Commanding Station, holding a letter permitting them to proceed.

However, the family alleged that Tash, whose full name is Hiram Gitau and who was Bayo&#039;s widower, had effectively blocked them from holding the ceremony at the singer&#039;s home.

According to the family, all communication had been routed through lawyers, and his response had done little to resolve matters on the ground.

Another family member, who identified himself as Wendo, described making the journey for the memorial and indicated the gathering was meant to be a modest occasion for family and friends.

He appeared visibly conflicted, noting that beyond the anniversary itself, there was little else to focus on.

The family&#039;s frustration centres on their inability to access their late sibling&#039;s property one year on, with questions lingering over Tash&#039;s motivations for the alleged ban.

None of the family members provided a direct explanation from Tash regarding his reasons, and the matter appears unresolved as of the date of filming.

Betty Bayo was a celebrated Kenyan gospel musician known for her powerful vocals and faith-driven music.

Her passing left a significant void in the local gospel scene, and her first anniversary drew not only family but also public attention given the circumstances surrounding the memorial.
