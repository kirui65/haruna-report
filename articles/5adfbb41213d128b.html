Nairobi - The Kenya Meteorological Department has released its daily weather forecast covering all 47 counties, effective from 9.00 pm on November 26, 2026, through to 9.00 pm on November 27, 2026.

Acting Director Edward Muriuki issued the bulletin on November 26, 2026, at 3.00 pm, warning that strong southerly to southeasterly winds exceeding 25 knots (12.5 m/s) are expected over parts of Marsabit and Turkana counties. Rainfall is otherwise expected to remain light and confined to isolated locations across the country.

Along the coast, Mombasa and Kilifi will experience partly cloudy skies tonight before giving way to sunny intervals through the morning. Kwale and Kilifi residents should, however, expect light showers to be possible during the afternoon.

Tana River and Lamu face a more active outlook, with thunderstorms and light showers forecast both tonight and during the afternoon of November 27. Taita Taveta is similarly flagged for light rains and thunderstorms at multiple points during the forecast period.

Further inland, Makueni stands out as one of the counties with the most widespread rainfall activity, with light showers and thunderstorms anticipated tonight, in the morning, and again in the afternoon.

Kitui and Machakos are also in line for scattered showers, particularly in the morning and afternoon hours. Marsabit, specifically around Moyale, is expected to record light showers and thunderstorms overnight.

Nairobi residents can expect partly cloudy skies tonight with a chance of light showers, though no significant rainfall is forecast for the city&#039;s morning or afternoon hours. Counties across the Rift Valley, western Kenya , and the Lake Victoria basin, including Turkana, Nakuru, Kakamega, Kisumu, and Narok, are largely forecast to remain dry with sunny intervals through the day.

Temperatures will peak highest in Turkana at 37°C and Mandera at 36°C, while cooler conditions prevail in highland areas. Laikipia is set to record a low of 12°C, with Uasin Gishu and Elgeyo Marakwet also dipping to 13°C overnight.

Garissa, Wajir, Siaya, Nyamira, and Kisii are among counties expected to remain largely dry, with partly cloudy skies and sunny intervals dominating through the forecast period.

The Kenya Meteorological Service Authority placed the probability of a strong El Niño developing this year at 81%, with a 97% likelihood that its effects will extend into early 2027.
