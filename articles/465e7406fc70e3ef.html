A Uasin Gishu County Government employee has been charged with allegedly defrauding a police officer of KSh 115,000 after promising to facilitate his promotion to the rank of sergeant.

The suspect, identified as Daniel Kemboi Kuyo, 40, works as a procurement officer at the county government and is accused of impersonating a Human Resource officer attached to the National Police Service Commission (NPSC).

Kuyo was arrested in the Outspan area of Kapseret following an intelligence-led operation conducted by detectives from the Directorate of Criminal Investigations (DCI).

He was subsequently taken to Eldoret Central Police Station for processing before being presented at the Eldoret Law Courts.

Appearing before Senior Resident Magistrate Keyne Gweno, Kuyo denied a charge of cheating contrary to Section 315 of the Penal Code.

The prosecution alleged that he obtained KSh 115,000 from police officer Stanley Kogo by falsely claiming that he could secure his promotion to the rank of sergeant.

According to the charge, Kuyo allegedly committed the offence on diverse dates between September 21 and November 3, 2026, in the Outspan area of Kapseret Sub-County, Uasin Gishu County.

During the court proceedings, the accused asked the magistrate to grant him lenient bond and cash bail terms, arguing that he was not a flight risk.

Magistrate Gweno ordered his release on a bond of KSh 200,000 with one surety of a similar amount. Alternatively, he was granted a cash bail option of KSh 150,000.

The case will be mentioned on November 30, 2026, for further directions.
