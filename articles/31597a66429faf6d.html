Kenyans pursuing postgraduate education abroad have seven fully funded scholarship opportunities closing between November 30 and December 1, 2026, covering programmes in China, Switzerland, Singapore, the United Kingdom and Hong Kong.

The awards target master&#039;s and doctoral students across a broad range of disciplines, with funding packages that in some cases cover tuition, accommodation, stipends, travel and research expenses.

The Yenching Academy of Peking University is accepting applications for its two-year master&#039;s programme in China studies until November 30, 2026, at 9am Beijing time.

Open to international graduates with a bachelor&#039;s degree in any field, the fellowship includes tuition, university accommodation, a monthly stipend and one return air ticket.

English proficiency is required, though graduates of English-taught programmes may qualify for an exemption.

Also closing on November 30 is the ETH Zurich Excellence Scholarship and Opportunity Programme (ESOP), which supports outstanding students pursuing master&#039;s degrees at the Swiss institution.

From the 2027/28 academic year, the award provides a tuition fee waiver and CHF 13,500 per semester, alongside mentorship and access to the ETH Foundation network.

Applicants must generally rank in the top 10% of their bachelor&#039;s class and submit a research proposal for their master&#039;s thesis. The deadline is 11:59am Central European Time.

The Swiss Government Excellence Scholarships also close on November 30, targeting early-career researchers who have completed a master&#039;s degree and wish to pursue research or doctoral studies at Swiss universities.

Successful research scholarship recipients receive a monthly allowance of approximately CHF 2,450. Applicants must secure the backing of an academic supervisor in Switzerland before applying.

The Graduate Ellison Scholars Programme offers fully funded doctoral places at the University of Oxford for the October 2027 intake.

Supported by the Ellison Institute of Technology, the programme funds research in artificial intelligence, engineering, economics, biology, plant sciences, chemistry and mathematics.

The package covers tuition, a living stipend, travel, conference participation, technology and arrival costs. The application deadline is December 1, 2026, at 5pm UK time.

The Hong Kong Research Grants Council is offering 400 fellowships through its Hong Kong PhD Fellowship Scheme for the 2027/28 academic year.

Each scholar receives an annual stipend of HK$344,400 and a yearly travel allowance of HK$14,400 for up to three years.

Applications close at noon Hong Kong time on December 1.

The University of Hong Kong&#039;s Presidential PhD Scholarship , also closing December 1, is open to outstanding international doctoral applicants.

The package is valued at up to HK$445,800 in the first year and includes a monthly postgraduate scholarship of HK$28,700, a tuition waiver, a cash award and a guaranteed hall place.

Applicants to full-time PhD programmes at HKU are automatically considered for the award.

Finally, the Singapore International Graduate Award (SINGA) supports doctoral research in science and engineering at leading Singaporean universities and research institutions, including those affiliated with A*STAR, the National University of Singapore and Nanyang Technological University.

The package includes full tuition, a monthly stipend of S$2,700 rising to S$3,200 after the qualifying examination, a one-time airfare grant and a settling-in allowance.

Prospective applicants are advised to prepare academic transcripts, recommendation letters and research proposals well ahead of the deadlines, as all awards are competitive and meeting minimum requirements does not guarantee selection.
