Kenya Power Announces Planned Outages in Nairobi, Kiambu, Nyeri, Kwale and Kilifi on November 26

WhatsAppFacebookX

News summary

Kenya Power has announced planned electricity interruptions in parts of Nairobi, Kiambu, Nyeri, Kwale and Kilifi counties on Thursday, November 26, 2026, to facilitate maintenance works. The scheduled outages will affect several residential areas, businesses, institutions and other customers, with interruptions expected to last between seven and ten hours depending on the location.
