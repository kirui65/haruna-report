Why Ministry of Sports need extra billions for Afcon 2027, World Athletics Championships

WhatsAppFacebookX

NAIROBI, Kenya, November 25, 2026 &#8211; The Ministry of Sports has requested an extra Ksh 8billion to enable Kenya effectively co-host next year’s Africa Cup of Nations (Afcon) as well as the World Athletics Championships in 2029.

While appearing before the National Assembly Committee on Sports and Culture, Principal Secretary Elijah Mwangi said just over Ksh 3.5 billion out of the requested amount will be directed towards Afcon – with the rest going towards the World Championships.

“The higher requirement in FY 2027/28 are for preparation to host AFCON 2027 which is Kshs 3,546,789,165 and the 2029 World Athletics Championships which is Kshs 3.9 billion. The requirements also provide for essential recurrent obligations necessary to sustain the operations and delivery of the State Departments core programs,&#8221; the PS said.

Mwangi noted that the ministry has been allocated just over Ksh 2.3 billion only, a stark contrast to the Ksh 10 billion for efficiently preparing for the two mega competitions.

“The resource requirement for the FY is Kshs 10,777,266,087 against an allocation of Kshs 2,393,028,584 resulting to the deficit,&#8221; he said.
