The Social Health Authority ( SHA ) replaced the National Health Insurance Fund (NHIF) and began operations on 1 October 2024. Missing out on SHA registration means you risk losing access to public healthcare at thousands of facilities across Kenya. There is no SHA registration fee charged by the government. However, monthly contributions are required to activate and maintain your cover.

Salaried employees: your employer deducts 2.75% of your gross pay (a minimum of KES 300) and remits it to SHA by the 9th of the following month.

When visiting a hospital, confirm the facility is empanelled with SHA for the service you need, present your ID for membership verification, and ask staff to confirm what is covered and whether any co-payment applies.
