Rotaract Club Malindi takes healthcare to the grassroots

WhatsAppFacebookX

The Rotaract Club of Malindi, popularly known as The Billionaires, is stepping up efforts to improve access to healthcare at the grassroots through an integrated medical outreach scheduled for December 5 at Muyeye Health Centre in Malindi.

Organized in partnership with the Kilifi County Department of Health and other stakeholders, the initiative seeks to bring essential medical services closer to residents, particularly those facing financial barriers to accessing healthcare, while promoting preventive care, early disease detection and community wellness.

Club President Peally Salim said the outreach reflects the club’s commitment to serving the community by reaching residents who may be unable to afford medical care despite the availability of services in health facilities.

The medical camp will offer general consultations, cancer awareness and screening, eye care, dental services, reproductive health services, as well as nutrition and disability assessments.

A key focus will be cancer awareness and early detection through the Mashinani War on Cancer campaign, which encourages residents to undergo regular screening, recognize warning signs and seek medical attention promptly.

The campaign also seeks to highlight the importance of early diagnosis in improving treatment outcomes and saving lives.

By bringing together a range of medical services under one initiative, the club and its partners aim to make healthcare more accessible and inclusive while responding to the diverse health needs of the community.

The outreach forms part of the club’s broader commitment to community transformation, guided by Rotary’s motto, Service Above Self, and the belief that meaningful change begins with identifying and addressing the needs of society.

Residents of Muyeye and neighbouring communities have been urged to turn up in large numbers on December 5 and take advantage of the services offered.
