"Gachagua aliangukia": Kenyans praise Pastor Dorcas after JKLive interview

Pastor Dorcas Gachagua turned heads across Kenya after her appearance on JKLive, where veteran journalist Jeff Koinange put her through her paces in what many viewers described as a masterclass in poise and articulation.

The mother of two sat down to speak about her husband, former Deputy President Rigathi Gachagua , and their family, fielding questions with a calm confidence that left many Kenyans reaching for their phones to share their reactions online.

Dorcas and Rigathi have been married for 37 years , a union that she touched on during the interview as she stood firmly by her husband and spoke openly about their family values. For many viewers, it was clear that the depth of that relationship came through in every answer she gave.
