"Anyone can use": Norah Muendo sparks reactions after sharing free nursery plans

Norah Muendo, the founder of Nila Baby Shop, has set Kenyan mums buzzing after unveiling plans for a completely free nursery inside her latest Nairobi CBD outlet.

In a viral Instagram reel posted on @nila_baby_shop on 12th November, Norah, dressed in a bright red two-piece, took followers on an excited tour of the unfinished space at the new CBD Archives branch.

White tiles, exposed wires, and construction materials were still visible throughout the walkthrough, but the entrepreneur&#039;s enthusiasm was impossible to miss.

Walking through the large open space, Norah pointed out where each section would sit, including beds for babies and toddlers to rest, a private nursing station for breastfeeding mothers, a dedicated daddies waiting area, and aluminium windows for ventilation.

Her pitch was simple: give mums shopping in the city centre a safe, clean space to change, feed, and settle their little ones, without the stress of navigating busy streets or paying for the privilege.

The nursery will form part of Nila Baby Shop&#039;s 8th branch, located opposite the Kenya National Archives on Moi Avenue, with the grand opening scheduled for 5th December 2026.

The launch promises even more for expectant mothers, including free pregnancy scans, maternity shoots, baby shoots, and refreshments on the day.

The announcement drew a flood of responses online. Many mothers praised the nursery concept as a much-needed solution for women who shop in town with infants and have no designated space to breastfeed or change nappies.

Others took the moment to celebrate Norah&#039;s journey, noting how far she has come since her hawker days.

A significant number of followers used the comments section to inquire about job openings at the new branch, asking about sales, cleaning, and customer care roles. The brand has been directing applicants to submit their details at the CBD branch directly.

Questions about how the nursery will be managed, including staffing, baby safety, and hygiene standards, were also raised by several netizens, though Norah is yet to address those specifics publicly.

Norah launched Nila Baby Shop in 2018 after struggling to find a convenient one-stop baby shop while pregnant with her firstborn daughter, Nila, after whom the business is named.

She started with KSh 14,000 worth of stock sourced from Gikomba Market and built the brand into one of Kenya&#039;s most recognised baby retail businesses, with locations in Nairobi CBD, Utawala, Mombasa, Kisumu, Eldoret , Meru, and Nakuru.

Her generosity has made headlines before, including paying a full year&#039;s rent for a mother of triplets who was struggling following childbirth.

The free nursery is her latest effort to give back to the mothers who, she says, have supported her every step of the way.
