Emirates introduces A350 aircraft and Premium Economy on Nairobi–Dubai route

WhatsAppFacebookX

Emirates has introduced its Airbus A350 aircraft on the Nairobi–Dubai route, bringing its Premium Economy cabin to the Kenyan market as the airline expands its premium travel offering on one of its three daily services.

The aircraft entered service on November 12, replacing the Boeing 777 on the morning EK717 flight from Dubai to Nairobi and the return EK718 service. The other two daily rotations between Nairobi and Dubai will continue operating with Boeing 777 aircraft.

The introduction gives passengers travelling from Kenya access to Emirates&#8217; Premium Economy product, which sits between Economy and Business Class and offers an additional cabin choice for travellers seeking greater comfort without paying Business Class fares.

The new offering is particularly relevant to passengers connecting through Dubai to long-haul destinations in Europe and the United States, where Emirates operates an extensive international network.

Speaking about the introduction, Emirates said Kenya had become its second-largest African market by capacity after South Africa, underscoring Nairobi&#8217;s importance to its continental operations.

The airline indicated that it would continue assessing opportunities to expand its services in Kenya, with future capacity decisions dependent on passenger demand.

Emirates has positioned Premium Economy as a mid-market product, with fares generally sitting approximately halfway between Economy and Business Class. The airline also indicated that the introduction of the A350 would not, in itself, result in a repricing of fares on the Nairobi route.

The aircraft deployment comes as airlines increasingly differentiate their products across cabin classes to attract passengers with varying travel budgets and preferences.

For travel agents, the introduction creates another option when packaging international travel, particularly for clients travelling to Europe and North America through Dubai. Premium Economy provides an alternative for passengers who want additional comfort on long-haul journeys but may not require the full range of services offered in Business Class.

The introduction further strengthens Emirates&#8217; Nairobi operations and expands the range of international travel products available to passengers departing from Kenya.
