Kenya Airways Announces Top Job Vacancies, Offers Permanent Employment Terms

Kenya Airways has advertised three senior permanent positions at its Nairobi headquarters as part of its ongoing strategic recovery and growth agenda, with applications closing in late November 2026.

The national carrier is recruiting for a Head of KQ Academy, a Head of Fleet Development, and a Head of Employee Relations, all of which carry direct reporting lines to the airline&#039;s most senior executives.

The Head of KQ Academy will serve as the principal architect of the airline&#039;s enterprise learning strategy and as the Accountable Manager of its Approved Training Organisation (ATO), which operates under the oversight of the Kenya Civil Aviation Authority (KCAA) and in accordance with ICAO standards.

The role reports to the Chief People Officer and carries responsibility for both regulatory and commercial training, including revenue-generating programmes offered to external aviation clients.

Candidates must hold a bachelor&#039;s degree in a relevant discipline and bring at least 10 years of learning and development experience, with a minimum of five years in a senior leadership role. The position closes on November 25, 2026.

The Head of Fleet Development is accountable for managing Kenya Airways&#039; aircraft fleet across the full lifecycle, from acquisition and entry into service through to disposal and redelivery.

The minimum requirement is 10 years of progressive industry experience, with at least seven years directly relevant to fleet management or aircraft transactions. A university degree in engineering, finance, or law is mandatory. The application deadline is November 24, 2026.

The Head of Employee Relations will oversee the employment relationship across a workforce of more than 4,500 staff operating in over 30 countries.

The role carries responsibility for collective bargaining with multiple trade unions, compliance with Kenyan employment legislation, and the management of employment litigation before the Employment and Labour Relations Court.

Candidates must hold a bachelor&#039;s degree in human resources, law, or a related field, along with a mandatory professional HR certification such as CHRP (K) from the Institute of Human Resource Management (IHRM) Kenya.

A minimum of 12 years of HR experience is required, with at least seven years in a senior employee relations leadership capacity. The position closes on November 25, 2026.

All three roles are based at Kenya Airways headquarters in Nairobi. The airline specifies that only candidates with the right to live and work in Kenya will be considered.

Interested applicants are required to submit their curriculum vitae through the Kenya Airways careers portal.

In other news, the Central Bank of Kenya (CBK) has invited applications for its one-year internship programme targeting Kenyan graduates aged 21 to 29 who hold at least a first degree from a recognised university.

Applicants must have graduated within the previous 24 months and must not have completed another internship or gained relevant work experience since graduating.

Opportunities are available across nine departments, including Finance, Research, Banking and Payment Services, Communications, Human Resources, Legal Services, Internal Audit, General Services and Information Technology.

Applications must be submitted online through the CBK recruitment portal by December 10, 2026, with candidates required to provide academic documents, an institutional reference letter and proof of valid medical insurance.
