Siaya digital hub to spur innovation, entrepreneurship and economic growth

WhatsAppFacebookX

The Siaya Artificial Intelligence Youth Agency (SAIYA), was today launched in a colourful event held at SAIYA House in Siaya County and witnessed by the Medical Services Principal Secretary (PS), Dr Ouma Oluga, and his National Treasury counterpart, Dr Chris Kiptoo.

The initiative is aimed at empowering young people through Artificial Intelligence (AI) and digital skills, opening new avenues for innovation, entrepreneurship, employment and economic transformation within the county and beyond.

Speaking during the occasion, Dr Oluga underscored the significance of the Hub in shaping the future of Siaya&#8217;s youth, noting that it will serve as a platform for equipping young people with skills that can enable them to become agents of transformation in their communities.

The PS, who also serves as the Patron of the Agency, urged the youth to embrace emerging technologies, particularly Artificial Intelligence, noting that technological advancement, will play a critical role in shaping opportunities and livelihoods in the years ahead.

&#8220;As we talk about Youth Agency, it means the power for you to deliver the future you want to live in,&#8221; Dr Oluga said.

He further reaffirmed his commitment to creating opportunities and platforms through which young people can turn their ideas into practical solutions, contributing to both personal growth and national development.

&#8220;As your patron, my work is to create the platform for you to bring your ideas into life,&#8221; Oluga said.

While acknowledging that the journey towards innovation and success may not be easy, Dr Oluga expressed confidence in the resilience of the youth, urging them to remain determined in the face of challenges, as they pursue their aspirations.

&#8220;I have zero promise that I know how long that journey will take you. I have zero promise that you won&#8217;t get tired along the way, that you will not face any challenges that will hinder you from achieving what you need,&#8221; he said.

Also present during the launch was, Nelly Maluka Oluoch, Acting Chief Executive Officer of the Kenya Film Classification Board (KFCB), who challenged the youth to view the Hub as more than just an AI facility, describing it as a broader platform for empowerment and opportunity.

&#8220;This Centre goes beyond AI because that is just an enabler, which has opportunities and risks, but a real form of Empowerment,&#8221; Maluka said.

She cautioned the youth about the potential risks associated with Artificial Intelligence, urging them to uphold professionalism, responsibility and ethical standards, as they engage with the technology.

Maluka further encouraged young people to utilise the skills and opportunities offered by the hub to drive economic growth, innovation and self-reliance, while actively contributing to the country&#8217;s development.

&#8220;This can be our Singapore. Don&#8217;t wait for any other Singapore. Use the skills that you will gain from this Hub to help you grow yourselves and the entire nation,&#8221; she emphasised.

The establishment of SAIYA is expected to provide a platform through which young people can develop solutions, to long-standing community challenges, leveraging digital technology and innovation, to address local needs.

The initiative seeks to harness the potential of Artificial Intelligence, equip young people with digital competencies and unlock opportunities in innovation, entrepreneurship and economic transformation across Siaya County.
