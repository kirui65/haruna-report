Jinko Solar hosts Jinko Academy in Kenya to build local solar expertise

WhatsAppFacebookX

Jinko Solar, a global leader in photovoltaic technology and Battery Energy Storage Systems, has hosted the Jinko Academy in Kenya, bringing together academia and industry to strengthen local technical expertise in solar energy. The programme was delivered in collaboration with two of the country’s leading institutions, JKUAT and the University of Nairobi.

The Jinko Academy is designed to close the gap between theoretical learning and practical application in the renewable energy sector. Through its partnership with the two universities, Jinko Solar aims to equip students, researchers and local engineers with the skills needed to support Kenya’s growing solar industry and its Vision 2030 renewable energy goals.

The initiative comes at an important moment for Kenya’s energy landscape. Jinko Solar has a well established presence in the region. It supplied modules for the 50 MW Garissa solar plant and remains a leading module exporter to the Kenyan market. More recently, the company supplied its Tiger Neo 3.0 modules for Kenya’s largest private healthcare solar project, which covers more than 780 private hospitals nationwide.

During the academy sessions, participants worked directly with Jinko Solar’s technical experts on module manufacturing, system design and smart grid operations. The training also stressed practical applications, particularly in the high temperature and high irradiance conditions typical of Kenya, where reliable performance is critical.

The Jinko Academy reflects the company’s wider commitment to building capacity in emerging markets. As Kenya pursues an ambitious renewable energy agenda, partnerships between global technology leaders and local academic institutions are seen as essential to creating a skilled, self reliant workforce able to maintain and expand the country’s solar infrastructure.
