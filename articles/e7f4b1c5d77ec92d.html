Rwanda approves Samuel Nganga as World Bank manager

WhatsAppFacebookX

Kenyan professional Samuel Kamau Nganga has landed a prestigious international appointment after Rwanda&#8217;s Cabinet approved him as the new World Bank Group Country Manager for Rwanda, placing him at the heart of the institution&#8217;s development and investment partnership with the East African nation.

The appointment, announced following a Cabinet meeting on Thursday, November 26, 2026, represents a significant career milestone for Nganga, whose professional journey at the World Bank Group spans nearly two decades.

He takes over from Sahr Kpundeh, who held the position from September 2023 until August 31, 2026.

Nganga&#8217;s appointment is particularly significant because he will become the first World Bank Group Country Manager in Rwanda to oversee all four institutions operating under the World Bank Group through a unified leadership structure.

The four institutions are the International Bank for Reconstruction and Development (IBRD), International Development Association (IDA), International Finance Corporation (IFC) and Multilateral Investment Guarantee Agency (MIGA).

This consolidated arrangement is expected to strengthen coordination between development financing, private-sector investment, technical support and investment-risk guarantees.

In his new position, Nganga will lead the World Bank Group&#8217;s engagement with the Rwandan government and private sector, while supervising the formulation and implementation of programmes aligned with the country&#8217;s development priorities.

His mandate will place particular emphasis on employment creation, poverty reduction, private-sector expansion and shared economic prosperity. Nganga&#8217;s rise to the prestigious position follows an impressive career trajectory within the World Bank Group.

The Kenyan national joined the institution in 2007 through its Sub-Saharan Africa Department and progressively assumed increasingly senior responsibilities across different geographical regions and economic sectors.

He previously served as Regional Head of Operations for the International Finance Corporation across Africa. Immediately before his latest appointment, he was Manager of the IFC&#8217;s Manufacturing, Agribusiness and Services Africa department. In that capacity, he supervised investment and advisory programmes designed to stimulate private-sector growth across East and Southern Africa.

His professional expertise extends across manufacturing, agribusiness, consumer services, renewable energy, digital financial services, healthcare, education and financing for small and medium-sized enterprises. The breadth of his experience places him in a strategic position to connect development financing with practical economic opportunities.

Nganga assumes office as Rwanda and the World Bank Group prepare a new Country Partnership Framework covering 2027 to 2035. The framework will provide the strategic blueprint for the institution&#8217;s investments, technical assistance and advisory services in Rwanda over the next eight years.

His leadership will therefore be central to determining how World Bank resources and expertise support Rwanda&#8217;s economic transformation, strengthen businesses, expand employment opportunities and improve the livelihoods of citizens.
