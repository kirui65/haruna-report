German President Frank-Walter Steinmeier Arrives in Kenya for an Official Visit

WhatsAppFacebookX

German President Frank-Walter Steinmeier has arrived in Kenya for an official visit expected to strengthen cooperation between the two countries and create new opportunities in trade, investment, education, and technology.

Steinmeier arrived in Nairobi on Tuesday night, November 24, and was received at Jomo Kenyatta International Airport by Prime Cabinet Secretary and Cabinet Secretary for Foreign and Diaspora Affairs Musalia Mudavadi.

He is accompanied by a German business delegation and is set to hold bilateral talks with President William Ruto at State House aimed at advancing their common goals and opening up new possibilities for mutually beneficial cooperation.

The discussions are expected to focus on trade and investment, clean energy, climate action, education, technology and skills development.

In a statement following his arrival, Mudavadi said the visit provides an opportunity for Kenya and Germany to strengthen their longstanding partnership and explore new areas of cooperation and share a vision for sustainable development.

&#8220;Kenya and Germany share a longstanding partnership built on mutual trust, economic cooperation and a common vision for sustainable development,&#8221; he said.

He pointed out that increased investment, job and training possibilities for young Kenyans, and the sharing of technology and information could all be facilitated by closer relations between the two nations.

According to Mudavadi, &#8220;strengthening these ties can unlock greater investment, expand employment and training opportunities for young Kenyans, and facilitate knowledge and technology exchange.&#8221;

The visit will also include engagements with Kenya-Germany cooperation projects in healthcare, digital services, skills development, arts and culture, as well as nature and biodiversity conservation.

The visit is expected to provide an opportunity for both countries to strengthen their relationship and identify new ways of working together for mutual benefit.
