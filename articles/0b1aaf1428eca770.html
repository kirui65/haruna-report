Kenya&#039;s former vice president Arthur Moody Awori is approaching a remarkable century, and his family made sure to mark the milestone in style.

Photos and videos of a heartwarming birthday gathering, shared online around November 22, quickly captured the attention of Kenyans across social media.

In the footage, Awori sat before a white birthday cake adorned with lit candles, made a wish, and blew them out as his family erupted in cheers.

Nieces, nephews and other loved ones gathered closely around him, belting out Happy Birthday in a moment that clearly moved many who watched it online.

Awori was born on December 5, 1927, meaning his official 99th birthday falls on December 5, 2026. The early celebrations, however, were more than enough to get Kenyans talking.

What struck many viewers was not just the milestone itself, but how Awori looked and carried himself throughout.

Comments flooded in praising his upright posture, sharp presence and energetic demeanour, with fans noting he walks without a walking stick.

Some joked that the former VP appeared younger than people in their thirties and sixties.

Awori served as Kenya&#039;s ninth vice president under the late President Mwai Kibaki , holding the position from September 2003 to January 2008.

He took on the role following the death of Michael Kijana Wamalwa and also served as Minister for Home Affairs during his time in government.

He is widely remembered for his calm, diplomatic style and for introducing meaningful reforms in the prison system, most notably replacing cramped metallic police trucks, commonly known as "mariamu," with buses for transporting remandees to court.

As he inches closer to 100, it is clear that Kenyans have not forgotten what he stood for.

After decades in public office, the former president marked the milestone away from political fanfare, embracing the quiet country life he has preferred since leaving State House.
