The Kenya Meteorological Department has released its daily weather forecast for November 26, 2026, warning of light to moderate rainfall across several counties and strong southerly to southeasterly winds exceeding 25 knots (12.5m/s) over parts of Marsabit and Turkana counties.

The forecast, issued on November 25, 2026 at 3.00 PM by Acting Director Edward Muriuki, covers conditions valid from 9.00 PM on November 25 through to 9.00 PM on November 26.

Along the coast, Mombasa, Kwale, and Kilifi will experience partly cloudy skies tonight with a chance of light showers, giving way to sunny intervals through the morning and afternoon. Temperatures in Mombasa will range between 26°C and 32°C.

Tana River and Lamu will also see partly cloudy conditions tonight, with light showers and thunderstorms possible over a few places in the afternoon. Taita Taveta faces a more active forecast, with light showers and thunderstorms expected tonight, in the morning, and again in the afternoon.

In the eastern region, Kitui is among the counties facing the most significant rainfall activity, with light showers and thunderstorms expected tonight, light rains in the morning, and light to moderate showers and thunderstorms in the afternoon. Makueni will similarly see light to moderate showers tonight through to the afternoon.

Embu, Machakos, Isiolo, Meru, and Tharaka-Nithi will all have chances of light showers or thunderstorms, primarily in the afternoon hours.

Nairobi residents can expect partly cloudy skies tonight with a chance of light showers, cloudy and sunny intervals in the morning, and light showers with thunderstorms in the afternoon. Temperatures will sit between 15°C and 26°C.

Across the Mt Kenya region, Nyeri, Kirinyaga, Murang&#039;a, and Kiambu all face possibilities of light showers and thunderstorms, particularly in the afternoon. Nyandarua will record the country&#039;s lowest minimum temperature at 10°C.

In the western counties, Busia, Siaya, and Kisumu will see light showers and thunderstorms tonight, while Kakamega, Bungoma, Kisii, and Nyamira face similar conditions tonight and into the afternoon. Homa Bay and Migori are also forecast to receive light showers and thunderstorms.

The Rift Valley counties of Nakuru , Laikipia, Nandi, Uasin Gishu, and Kericho are expected to see light showers and thunderstorms mainly in the afternoon. Kajiado will also receive light showers and thunderstorms in the afternoon.

The hottest conditions are expected in Mandera at 37°C, followed by Turkana and Garissa at 36°C. Northern counties including Wajir, Garissa, Mandera, and Turkana will largely remain dry with sunny intervals throughout the day.
