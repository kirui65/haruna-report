The Kenya Medical Training College (KMTC) has shifted its 95th Graduation Ceremony from Thursday, December 3, 2026 to Friday, December 4, 2026, following an official announcement by the institution&#039;s Chief Executive Officer.

KMTC CEO Dr. Kelly Oluoch issued the public notice on Monday, November 23, 2026, alerting students and other stakeholders to the revised schedule.

The ceremony will be held at Moi International Sports Centre, Kasarani , Nairobi, beginning at 8am under the theme "Mashujaa wa Afya."

Alongside the ceremony date change, the graduation rehearsal has also been rescheduled.

Students will now attend rehearsals on Thursday, December 3, 2026, at the same venue and at the same start time of 8am, a day ahead of the main event.

The deadline for gown collection has been extended to Monday, November 30, 2026. Students are required to collect their gowns from KMTC headquarters in Nairobi before that date.

Graduating students will be able to collect their certificates between December 7 and December 16, 2026, on weekdays between 9am and 4pm at KMTC headquarters. Collection remains subject to completion of all clearance requirements.

KMTC has urged students to take note of all the revised dates and make the necessary arrangements ahead of the ceremony.

Further information is available on the college&#039;s official website at www.kmtc.ac.ke or via its social media handle @KMTCOfficial.

In related news, TUKO.co.ke reported on Moi University’s decision to postpone its 49th graduation ceremony by one week as a nationwide lecturers’ strike disrupted preparations.

The university had moved the event from December 3 to December 10, 2026.

Some prospective graduates were still waiting for missing examination marks, while lecturers remained away from work.
