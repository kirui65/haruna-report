Disease Surveillance, Healthcare Staffing Top Public Health Budget Priorities

WhatsAppFacebookX

NAIROBI, Kenya Nov 24 &#8211; Strengthening disease surveillance systems, addressing healthcare staffing shortages and improving sanitation are among the key priorities identified by the State Department for Public Health and Professional Standards ahead of the 2027/28 financial year.

Principal Secretary Mary Muthoni outlined the priorities on Tuesday when she appeared before the National Assembly Departmental Committee on Health to present the department&#8217;s proposals for the 2027 Budget Policy Statement.

The presentation, chaired by Committee Chairperson James Nyikal, focused on critical areas requiring additional investment to strengthen public health service delivery.

Muthoni highlighted the need to enhance disease surveillance and response capacity to ensure the country is better equipped to detect, monitor and respond to public health threats.

She also called for investment in Primary Health Care, improved sanitation and expanded access to essential public health services.
