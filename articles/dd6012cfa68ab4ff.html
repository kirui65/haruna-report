Direct doors: KRA announces new channels for Kenyans to use for tax disputes

The Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA) has designated official channels through which taxpayers can submit applications for the review of tax decisions, covering both customs and domestic tax disputes.

The authority issued two separate public notices under the Commissioner for Legal and Board Services, citing Section 229 of the East African Community Customs Management Act, 2004, as well as Section 73 of the Tax Procedures Act (Cap. 469B), as the legal basis for the designations.

Taxpayers aggrieved by decisions of the Commissioner of Customs and Border Control, as well as those disputing rulings by the Commissioner of Large Taxpayers or the Commissioner of Micro and Small Taxpayers, may now serve their documents through two routes.

The first is electronic service, where objections and related documents can be submitted via email to LSBCIROStaff@kra.go.ke.

The second is physical delivery or post, addressed to the Deputy Commissioner, Tax Dispute Resolution Division, Legal and Board Services Department, Block B, 7th Floor, Ushuru Pension Towers (UPT), Elgon Road, Upper Hill, P.O. Box 48240 – 00100, Nairobi.

The customs appeals designation applies to persons aggrieved by a decision of the Commissioner of Customs and Border Control and covers applications for review under Gazette Notice No. 1036 of 2019, which delegates relevant powers to the Legal and Board Services Department.

The domestic taxes designation applies to those disputing decisions made by the Commissioner Large Taxpayers and the Commissioner Micro and Small Taxpayers, covering notices of objection filed under the Tax Procedures Act.

Taxpayers seeking clarification on either process can reach KRA through the same email address: LSBCIROStaff@kra.go.ke.

The authority also listed its Complaints and Information Centre hotlines as +254 709 017 700 and +254 709 017 800, with the email cic@kra.go.ke, and a separate corruption reporting line at +254 0726 984 668.

Applicants must hold qualifications from recognised institutions, be available for the full 12-month programme and have no previous internship experience, with opportunities available in more than 50 disciplines.

Successful degree holders will receive a monthly stipend of KSh 25,000, while diploma holders will earn KSh 18,000, subject to statutory deductions, although the programme does not guarantee permanent employment.
