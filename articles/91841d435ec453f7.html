Detectives in Meru County have arrested a man in connection with the assault of police officers during Mashujaa Day celebrations held at Githongo Stadium in Imenti Central on October 20, 2026.

The Directorate of Criminal Investigations (DCI) confirmed the arrest in a statement issued on Sunday, November 22, identifying the suspect as Latif Kinyua Mbaya.

According to the DCI, a group of youths armed with stones and clubs descended on the stadium and disrupted what had been an otherwise orderly national celebration.

Anti-riot police moved in to restore calm, but the group later mounted a second assault, returning on motorcycles and charging towards the venue.

The renewed violence left two police officers with serious injuries. Two government vehicles also sustained damage, with their windscreens smashed during the confrontations.

Following the incident, detectives from Meru launched a formal investigation into the violence. Once the inquiry was concluded, the case file was submitted to the Office of the Director of Public Prosecutions (ODPP) for consideration.

The ODPP directed that Latif Kinyua Mbaya be charged with grievous harm, contrary to Section 231 of the Penal Code, in addition to other offences. Acting on those directions, investigators tracked the suspect to Meru Town, where he was arrested.

The DCI confirmed that Mbaya is currently being processed ahead of his court arraignment.
