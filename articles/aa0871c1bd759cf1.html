Kenya Electricity Transmission Company Limited (KETRACO) has announced 10 job openings spanning senior management, technical, and administrative functions.

The advertisement was published through MyGov on Tuesday, November 17, 2026.

The positions are open exclusively to Kenyan citizens, and all applications must be submitted online through the JobLink portal on KETRACO&#039;s website.

The company has explicitly stated that hard copy submissions will not be considered.

The two most senior roles on offer are at the KET 2 grade and are offered on a contract basis: a general manager, transmission planning and project development, and a general manager, transmission infrastructure development, each representing one post.

At the KET 3 level, the company is seeking one manager, system control and market operation, offered on a permanent and pensionable basis.

Six assistant manager positions at KET 4 grade are also available, all on a permanent and pensionable basis. These cover finance and accounts (project finance), design-electrical, system control and market operations, system studies, and supply chain management and logistics.

Rounding out the advertised openings are two administrative roles: a principal office administrator at KET 5 grade and an assistant office administrator I at KET 7 grade, both permanent and pensionable.

Candidates are required to submit applications through the JobLink portal available on the KETRACO website, clearly indicating the position applied for and the relevant vacancy reference number as the subject heading. Full job descriptions, qualification requirements, and application instructions are available at www.ketraco.co.ke.

The deadline for all applications is December 4, 2026, at 5.00pm, addressed to the managing director at KETRACO&#039;s offices at KAWI Complex, 4th Floor, Popo Road, South C, Nairobi.

KETRACO emphasised its commitment to equal opportunity employment, in line with Chapter 232(1) of the Constitution, and has specifically encouraged applications from persons with disabilities, marginalised groups, minorities, and female candidates.

The company warned that any canvassing or provision of false information will result in automatic disqualification, and that only shortlisted candidates will be contacted.

In other news, Public Procurement Regulatory Authority (PPRA) advertised 35 job vacancies for qualified Kenyans seeking employment opportunities in the public sector.

The recruitment targets professionals with relevant skills and experience to support the authority&#039;s operations.

The available positions include compliance officers and other roles across different departments. Applicants are expected to meet the academic, professional and experience requirements outlined for each vacancy.
