Left out: National Police adds new recruitment venues for December exercise

The National Police Service has gazetted 10 extra recruitment centres for its Police Constable intake exercise, after the venues were accidentally left out of the original advertisement published on 20 November 2026.

The addendum, signed by Inspector General Douglas Kanja Kirocho, raises the total number of recruitment centres nationwide to 433, up from the 423 initially announced .

The recruitment exercise remains scheduled for Thursday, 10 December 2026, running from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. across the country.

The 10 newly added centres are spread across six counties, with Marsabit accounting for four of them: Uran, Korr, Sagante, and Illeret.

Isiolo gained two centres in Sericho and Oldonyiro. Single centres were added in Siaya West (Uranga), Ndhiwa East in Homa Bay (Ombo), Samburu West (Loosuk), and Magarini North in Kilifi (Kibaoni).

The additions are concentrated in the northern and western parts of the country, reducing the distance candidates in those sub-counties would otherwise need to travel.

All other details from the original advertisement, including eligibility requirements and the application process, remain unchanged.

As reported earlier, the recruitment exercise is free and open to all eligible Kenyan citizens. Candidates must be aged between 18 and 28 years, hold a valid national identity card, and have attained a minimum KCSE mean grade of D+, with at least a D+ in English or Kiswahili.

The minimum height requirements are 5 feet 8 inches for men and 5 feet 3 inches for women, and applicants must be medically fit with no criminal record or pending charges.

Female applicants must not be pregnant at the time of recruitment or during training.

On the day of the exercise, candidates are required to present originals and copies of their academic certificates, their Kenya National Identity Card, and a birth certificate.

Those reporting to the NYS Headquarters in Ruaraka must additionally bring an NYS discharge certificate showing a conduct rating of at least "Good."

The National Police Service reiterated that attempting to bribe officials or submitting false information during the recruitment process constitutes a criminal offence.

Members of the public who suspect any malpractice are encouraged to report it at the nearest police station or through the toll-free lines 999, 911, 112, or via the DCI hotline 0800 722 203. The service has committed to treating all such reports in strict confidence.
