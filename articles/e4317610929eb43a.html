Matiang’i calls out President Ruto over repeated project launches, unfinished projects

WhatsAppFacebookX

Jubilee Party Leader and Presidential aspirant Dr. Fred Matiang’i has criticised President William Ruto over what he described as repeated launches and relaunches of projects across counties.

In addition, Matiang’i accused the government of filling communities with unfinished projects and foundation stones instead of completed developments.

Speaking during his mega Gusii tour, where he engaged residents of Kisii and Nyamira counties, Dr. Matiang’i questioned the President’s repeated project launches, saying:

“Mheshimiwa Rais amekuwa hapa siku nne anaingia kufungua project ambayo amefungua tena. Kazi ya Rais huyu ni kwamba, amejaza county zetu na foundation stones. Uongo kila wakati.”

Dr. Matiang’i also accused the President of using money to influence residents in Kisii, insisting that the people of Kisii and Nyamira identify with the Opposition.

He further criticised the government’s record, citing rising insecurity and questioning its ability to guarantee the safety of Kenyans. He also raised concerns over alleged misuse of public funds.

Dr. Matiang’i was accompanied by PPK Party Leader and Kiharu MP Ndindi Nyoro, PLP Party Leader Martha Karua, Siaya Governor James Orengo, former Education Cabinet Secretary Ezekiel Machogu, Nyamira Senator Erick Okong’o Omogeni, Kitutu Chache South MP Anthony Kibagendi, Bomachoge Borabu MP Obadiah Barongo, Kitutu Masaba MP Clive Gisairo and Bobasi MP Innocent Obiri, among other leaders.
