80 JKUAT students showcase solutions from Samsung Innovation Campus

WhatsAppFacebookX

Students taking part in the Samsung Innovation Campus (SIC) programme have presented digital solutions to local problems at a showcase at Jomo Kenyatta University of Agriculture and Technology (JKUAT).

The event, held at JKUAT&#8217;s main campus in Juja, was hosted by the university&#8217;s technology incubation centre, JHUB Africa, with Samsung Electronics East Africa.

Samsung Innovation Campus is Samsung&#8217;s global education programme, which trains young people in industry-relevant digital skills, including artificial intelligence (AI), coding and programming. In Kenya, it runs through a partnership between Samsung Electronics East Africa, JKUAT and JHUB Africa. Samsung said JKUAT is the only university in East Africa running the programme.

This cohort had 80 students working in 17 teams. Each team had to identify a local problem and build a technology solution to address it.

The &#8220;Smart Nyuki&#8221; team took first place with a solution that helps local beekeepers manage their hives.

&#8220;One of the most valuable aspects of this programme has been the opportunity to move beyond learning concepts and actually build something,&#8221; said Moses Njau, a member of the winning team.

&#8220;Working on our projects has helped us understand how technology can be applied to solve problems, while also strengthening our technical and problem-solving skills.&#8221;

Nyawira Muraguri, Head of Corporate Marketing at Samsung Electronics East Africa, said equipping young people with digital skills is central to the company&#8217;s commitment to creating opportunities through technology.

&#8220;Through the Samsung Innovation Campus, we are investing in young people by equipping them with the skills and confidence to become creators of technology, not just consumers of it,&#8221; said Muraguri.

&#8220;The projects being presented today demonstrate what is possible when young talent is given access to the right knowledge, tools and opportunities to innovate.&#8221;

Beyond classroom learning, the programme gives participants practical exposure to technology and helps them build problem-solving, critical thinking and innovation skills. Samsung said it links academic knowledge with industry needs
