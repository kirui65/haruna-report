"We apologise": Nairobi Water Company announces 3-day major interruption

Nairobi Water has issued a public notice advising customers that its billing system will be taken offline for scheduled maintenance from Friday, November 20, 2026, through Monday, November 23, 2026.

The planned downtime, confirmed through an official notice from the company&#039;s Corporate Affairs and Liaison Department, will begin at 3:00 PM on November 20 and run until 8:00 AM on November 23, spanning approximately 65 hours.

Throughout the maintenance window, four core service functions will be unavailable: customer contracting, meter reading, billing, and revenue collection.

This means customers will not be able to pay water bills, register new accounts, or request meter readings during that period. Automated invoice generation will also be paused.

Nairobi Water said the shutdown is required to upgrade and improve its systems. "Kindly complete any necessary transactions before the planned maintenance," the notice stated.

The utility provider is strongly urging residents and businesses to settle outstanding balances and handle any pending water-related transactions before the 3:00 PM deadline on Friday, November 20.

Customers with overdue accounts are particularly advised to clear payments in advance to avoid potential service disruptions.

Nairobi Water acknowledged the disruption the downtime may cause and offered an apology in its statement.

Normal billing and revenue collection services are expected to resume at 8:00 AM on Monday, November 23, 2026.

Customers are advised to monitor the utility&#039;s official channels for updates or emergency contact information during the outage period.

Recently, Nairobi Water announced emergency shutdown of the Kabete Distribution Mains after a major pipeline was vandalised, disrupting supply across dozens of estates.

The 23-hour outage affected more than 40 areas, including Kibera, Karen and Westlands, as well as several major hospitals, leaving residents and essential services urged to ration stored water while repairs took place.
