Bamburi Residents to Enjoy Enhanced Family Share Connectivity Through Safaricom-Huawei Partnership

WhatsAppFacebookX

Residents of Bamburi in Mombasa County will now enjoy enhanced connectivity following the official launch of the Family Share Plan in the area by Safaricom, in partnership with Huawei.

The Family Share Plan brings together fixed and mobile connectivity under one proposition, enhancing the Home Fibre experience. The solution allows customers to connect up to five family members to a single plan, enabling them to share mobile data and voice minutes while enjoying seamless connectivity both at home and on the go. It also offers families greater convenience, flexibility and value from their Safaricom subscriptions.

Speaking during the rollout event at Zawadi Estate in Bamburi, Mombasa, Fawzia Ali, Chief Consumer Business Officer at Safaricom, said the expansion to the region is part of the company&#8217;s nationwide efforts to enhance the Home Fibre experience and deliver greater value to customers.

&#8220;The rollout of Family Share in this region is part of our nationwide expansion effort aimed at ensuring that our Home Fibre customers stay connected and enjoy more value from their subscriptions. We have already rolled out the solution in Nairobi and Kisumu, and are now bringing it to Mombasa, with plans to extend it to more parts of the country. Our goal is to ensure families enjoy seamless connectivity and stay connected wherever they are, whether at home or on the go,&#8221; said Fawzia.

The Bamburi rollout builds on Safaricom and Huawei&#8217;s ongoing collaboration to enhance home connectivity and expand access to integrated digital solutions across the country. It follows the initial rollout in Nairobi and Kisumu, as the two partners continue to extend the Family Share experience to more households across Kenya. Other launch partners include Xiaomi, LG, Ketepa, Farmers Choice, Vision Plus and Unilever.

The plan allows new and existing Safaricom Home Fibre customers to connect up to five family members and share mobile data or voice minutes. By combining Home Fibre with mobile voice, data and SMS benefits, Family Share provides households with a more integrated connectivity experience and the convenience of managing their services through a single bill.

The offering further strengthens Safaricom Home&#8217;s customer value proposition, building on recent speed upgrades at no additional cost while bringing fixed and mobile connectivity together. This gives households greater flexibility in managing their communication needs and makes connectivity expenses more predictable.

Starting at KES 3,749, customers can enjoy speeds of 40 Mbps and share either 750 voice minutes or 14GB of mobile data with up to five family members.

Customers can subscribe to Home Fibre or 4G and 5G Wi-Fi plans through My OneApp, by dialling *400#, or by visiting any Safaricom shop or authorised dealer outlet.
