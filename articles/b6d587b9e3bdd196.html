KeNHA Announces Traffic Disruption on Busy Section of Mombasa Road on November 29

The Kenya National Highways Authority (KeNHA) has warned motorists of an expected traffic disruption along a busy section of Mombasa Road (A8) on Sunday, 29 November 2026, to accommodate a half marathon marking World AIDS Day.

The notice, signed by Director General Eng. Luka Kimeli and dated 27 November 2026, states that the stretch between Nyayo National Stadium Roundabout and Museum Hill Interchange will be affected from 6.00 am to 10.30 am.

The road closure will allow a 21-kilometre half marathon to proceed as part of the World AIDS Day 2026 commemorations.

The event requires the use of a portion of the busy Nairobi highway, necessitating a temporary suspension of normal traffic flow along the affected section for approximately four and a half hours.

KeNHA has urged all motorists planning to use the route during that window to plan their travel accordingly and expect significant delays or diversions.

The authority advised road users to follow the traffic management plan that will be in place and to cooperate fully with police officers and traffic marshals deployed along the corridor.

KeNHA did not specify alternative routes in the notice but indicated that guidance would be provided on-site by the officers managing the closure.

Motorists with enquiries can contact KeNHA through its toll-free line on 0800-211-244, its customer care line at 0700 423 606, or via email at complaints@kenha.co.ke.

The authority can also be reached through its official social media handles and website.

In other news, KeNHA will close the Lower Kabete-Ndumbuini/Kapenguria Road-Uthiru Interchange from December 2, 2026, to January 31, 2027, to facilitate reconstruction of its ramps, loops and slip roads.

The closure will affect motorists using the Nairobi-Nakuru Highway corridor, with KeNHA Director General Eng. Luka Kimeli urging drivers to follow traffic signs and instructions from police officers and marshals.

Nairobi-bound motorists heading towards Ndumbuini can use alternative routes through Kabete Technical, Leadway Petrol Station or the 87/Muhuri Interchange, while drivers from Rironi and Nakuru can turn at Muhuri Interchange and use Muhuri-Ndumbuini Road.

Motorists travelling from Ndumbuini, Uthiru and Kawangware have also been directed to use Marigoini Cabro Road, Ndumbuini Road, Uthiru Underpass or Muhuri Interchange depending on their destinations.
