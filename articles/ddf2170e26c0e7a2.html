"It costs KSh 1,000": Huduma Kenya explains adding father's name to birth cert

Kenyans who need to add a father&#039;s name to a birth notification must travel to the county where the birth was registered, Huduma Kenya has stated, confirming that the amendment cannot be processed from any other location.

The clarification emerged from a public exchange on the official Huduma Kenya Facebook page, after a citizen named Jacqueline asked about the correct procedure for updating a birth notification that had been issued without the father&#039;s details.

Huduma Kenya instructed Jacqueline to present herself at Civil Registration offices or a Huduma Centre situated within the county of birth, adding that a registrar would provide further guidance once she arrived.

The response prompted a follow-up from another user who wanted to know whether the county-of-birth requirement was absolute, particularly for people now living in Nairobi.

Huduma Kenya stood by its earlier guidance, confirming that applicants must return to the county where the birth record originated.

The agency also disclosed that the amendment service costs KSh 1,000.

The requirement for county-specific processing reflects how civil registration records are maintained in Kenya, where birth records are held at the district or county level, and amendments must be verified and approved by the registrar responsible for that jurisdiction.

Civil registration matters in Kenya fall under the Births and Deaths Registration Act, which governs how birth records are created, stored, and updated across the country.

Recently, Huduma Kenya clarified whether a father’s name can be removed from a child’s birth certificate .

The agency said the document cannot be changed at a service desk and that Kenyan law permits removal only in limited circumstances.

A parent’s regret or a relationship breakdown is not enough to erase a name from the record.

Even when paternity is disputed, a court must weigh DNA evidence against the child’s right to know their origins and access parental support.
