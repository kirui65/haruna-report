A past interview featuring Ohangla star Onyango Jasuba, popularly known as Nyakwar Dayo or Mandela, has begun circulating widely online following news of his passing.

Jasuba died on Saturday, November 14, after a short illness, leaving the Ohangla music community in deep mourning.

The resurfaced clip has since drawn significant attention, with many fans revisiting the musician&#039;s own words about life in the entertainment industry.

In the interview, the exact date of which remains unclear, Jasuba addressed a song he recorded titled Mwanduni Emanegi, loosely translated as "your wealth is what is killing you."

The track featured lyrics about a musician returning home carrying many diseases, and his explanation revealed that many listeners had drawn the wrong conclusion about what he meant.

The clarification was clearly important to him, as he stressed that his lyrics carried a broader message about the pressures musicians face rather than a personal health disclosure.

The conversation then moved to the lifestyle typically associated with performers, with the interviewer raising the question of whether alcohol and promiscuity contributed to the struggles of musicians.

Jasuba pushed back confidently, drawing a distinction between opportunity and behaviour.

When the interviewer asked him to repeat the statement on camera, he did so without hesitation, adding further context to his position.

The resurfaced clip has reignited public conversation about the perceptions surrounding performers and the pressures they navigate.

It is worth noting that there is no confirmed information linking any of the remarks in the interview to the illness Jasuba suffered before his death, and the specific cause of his passing has not been publicly established.
