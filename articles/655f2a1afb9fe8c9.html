CAF Unveils Shield-Inspired PAMOJA 2027 Logo as Kenya, Tanzania and Uganda Prepare to Host Africa Cup of Nations

WhatsAppFacebookX

The Confederation of African Football (CAF), together with hosts Kenya, Tanzania and Uganda, has launched the official logo of the TotalEnergies CAF Africa Cup of Nations 2027, unveiling a shield-inspired emblem that will carry the branding PAMOJA 2027 for the first finals of the continent's flagship football tournament to be staged across three nations.

CAF said the design is inspired by the shield, which the continental football body described as "a timeless symbol deeply rooted in African heritage, identity and strength". The emblem becomes the public face of a tournament that will be shared by the three East African neighbours under the PAMOJA name, the Swahili word for together, and it was presented jointly by CAF and the three host nations at the launch.

Kenya, Tanzania and Uganda secured the 2027 hosting rights through a joint bid approved by the CAF Executive Committee in 2023, the same decision-making process that awarded the 2025 finals to Morocco. The award returned the Africa Cup of Nations to East Africa for the first time since Ethiopia staged the 1976 edition, and the bid was built around matches being played across all three countries under a single shared tournament identity.

While the competition has previously been co-hosted, with Ghana and Nigeria sharing the 2000 finals and Gabon and Equatorial Guinea staging the 2012 edition together, the 2027 tournament is the first Africa Cup of Nations to be shared by three countries. The arrangement follows football's broader shift toward joint hosting, after the 2026 FIFA World Cup was staged across the United States, Mexico and Canada.

The launch confirms the full official title of the event as the TotalEnergies CAF Africa Cup of Nations PAMOJA 2027, with French energy group TotalEnergies continuing in its long-running role as the tournament's title sponsor. First contested in 1957, the Africa Cup of Nations is held every two years and brings together 24 national teams to decide the continental champion, with the most recent edition hosted by Morocco.

The shield was chosen for its resonance far beyond the pitch. Shields feature on the national coats of arms of Kenya, Tanzania and Uganda, and the symbol recurs across African traditions of ceremony, identity and defence, aligning with the qualities of heritage and strength that CAF attached to the new design.

The unveiling opens the public-facing countdown to the finals, with the new identity set to anchor the tournament's marketing, broadcast presentation and merchandise in the build-up to 2027. Attention now turns to operational preparations across the three host countries, with organisers due to release details on venues, match schedules and ticketing arrangements in the coming months.

National teams across the continent are engaged in the qualification campaign for the 24-team finals, while Kenya, Tanzania and Uganda prepare to stage the competition as hosts. CAF is expected to announce further tournament details, including the match schedule and the draw for the finals, as the countdown to the PAMOJA 2027 edition continues.
