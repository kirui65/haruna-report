Cheruiyot boosts Kisumu West traders, says small businesses drive grassroots economy

WhatsAppFacebookX

Senate Majority Leader Aaron Cheruiyot joined traders in Kisumu West, Kisumu, at the invitation of Hon. Rozzah Buyu, to recognise and boost smallholder traders.Cheruiyot described traders, in all their diverse crafts, as the most effective drivers of the economy at the grassroots.

He said traders work tirelessly and devise innovative strategies to navigate the complexities and overcome the challenges of the business world, often with little support but with great determination.

To recognise their hard work, the leaders boosted the businesses of smallholder traders in the constituency, offering encouragement and support to help them grow.

Speaking at the event, Cheruiyot said the initiative was guided by the Usawa Agenda, which seeks to uplift every Kenyan to lead a decent life and build a nation where everyone is valued and appreciated.

He said the agenda is about fairness, dignity and equal opportunity, ensuring that no Kenyan, however small their business, is left behind.

Cheruiyot thanked the people of Kisumu for their overwhelming support for the Broad-Based Government and for their decision to grant President William Samoei Ruto another mandate to strengthen national unity and implement progressive policies for all Kenyans.

He said the support shown in Kisumu was a sign of growing trust in the government&#8217;s agenda of inclusion, economic empowerment and national cohesion.

The event brought together traders from different sectors, who shared their experiences and aspirations, with leaders pledging continued support to strengthen grassroots enterprises.
