Kenya, Zimbabwe strengthen bilateral ties through media cooperation

WhatsAppFacebookX

Kenya and Zimbabwe have formalised cooperation in the media and information sector following the signing of a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between Kenya News Agency (KNA) and Zimbabwe’s Inter-African News Agency (NEW ZIANA).

The MoU was signed at State House, Nairobi, by Kenya’s Principal Secretary for Broadcasting and Telecommunications, Stephen Isaboke, and Zimbabwe’s Permanent Secretary for Information, Publicity and Broadcasting Services, Nick Ndabaningi Mangwana.

The signing follows Zimbabwean President Dr Emmerson Dambudzo Mnangagwa’s state visit to Kenya, where he held bilateral talks with President William Ruto at State House.

Speaking during the engagement, PS Isaboke said the MoU seeks to strengthen media cooperation between the two countries through enhanced collaboration, information sharing and professional engagement.

&#8220;As part of this engagement, I signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between the Kenya News Agency (KNA) and the Zimbabwe Inter-African News Agency (NEW ZIANA), alongside my Zimbabwean counterpart, the Permanent Secretary for Information, Publicity and Broadcasting Services, Mr Nick Ndavaningi Mangwana,&#8221; the PS said.

He revealed that the signing formed part of the broader bilateral engagements aimed at deepening cooperation and formalising agreements of mutual interest between the two countries.

&#8220;Today, I participated in the Kenya–Zimbabwe bilateral meeting held during the State Visit of President Emmerson Dambudzo Mnangagwa of Zimbabwe, hosted by President Dr William Ruto,&#8221; he said.

PS Isaboke said the MoU will provide a framework for the exchange of news and multimedia content, professional capacity building, sharing of knowledge and technical expertise, as well as collaboration in journalism and Information and Communication Technology (ICT).

“The MoU will provide a framework for the exchange of news and multimedia content, professional capacity building, sharing of knowledge and technical expertise, as well as collaboration in journalism and Information and Communication Technology (ICT),” he explained.

The MoU will pave the way for a stronger partnership between the two national news agencies, further enhancing institutional collaboration, promoting information sharing and facilitating the exchange of professional knowledge and expertise.

Isaboke added that the MoU will also promote people-to-people relations and enhance the visibility of African perspectives on regional and international media platforms.

The PS also underscored the importance of intra-African media cooperation in empowering African countries to tell their own stories, shape their narratives and showcase the continent’s development, innovation, cultural diversity and economic opportunities.

Isaboke revealed that the partnership will provide the Kenya News Agency (KNA) with an opportunity to strengthen its regional media presence, expand content exchange with NEW ZIANA and enhance the coverage and sharing of African stories across regional and international media platforms.

The partnership will also enhance information exchange between KNA and NEW ZIANA, promote the sharing of accurate and credible information, and strengthen the dissemination of African stories across regional and international media platforms.

“This partnership is an important step towards strengthening media cooperation, enhancing the flow of credible information and deepening the longstanding friendship between Kenya and Zimbabwe,” he said.

Established in 1963, the Kenya News Agency (KNA) is the government&#8217;s flagship news agency under the Directorate of Information, State Department for Broadcasting and Telecommunications.

The Agency plays a vital role in gathering, processing and disseminating credible, accurate and timely news and information to support national development and enable the public to make informed decisions.

With a presence in all 47 counties and 25 sub-county offices, KNA is strategically positioned to leverage the partnership to expand its reach, strengthen its visibility and enhance the sharing of Kenyan stories with regional and international audiences.

The collaboration with NEW ZIANA is expected to complement KNA’s mandate by creating opportunities for enhanced content exchange and professional collaboration and, more so, promote dissemination of credible African perspectives and news on the global media stage.

It will further contribute to the growth of Africa’s information ecosystem and advance the broader aspirations of continental integration.
