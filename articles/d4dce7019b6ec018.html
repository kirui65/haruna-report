Mombasa - Mohamed Ahmed, a former journalist with Nation Media Group who now leads sales operations at a Mombasa-based real estate firm, has been named among Kenya&#039;s 100 Most Influential and Inspirational Muslims for 2026.

The recognition, organised by The Muslim Media, placed Ahmed in the Business & Management category, acknowledging his contributions following a career that spans journalism, communications, marketing and business development.

That grounding in public affairs and stakeholder communication later shaped his transition into corporate roles, eventually leading him to his current position as Head of Sales at Megna Homes.

The company is a Mombasa-based property developer active in residential development, project management, sales and property management. Its flagship project, Santana in Mtopanga, is a gated community comprising 816 residential units and 64 commercial spaces. In 2025, Santana became the first development by a Mombasa-based real estate firm to receive EDGE certification for sustainable design.

Speaking after receiving the award, Ahmed described the recognition as a prompt for continued responsibility rather than a personal accolade.
