UEFA Champions League round five predictions: Wednesday match previews, key stats, current form & h2h

PSG have the chance to remain atop the Champions League table when they host AS Roma in the headline fixture on Wednesday night.

The back-to-back Champions League winners are unbeaten and have 12 points from their opening four games.

Barcelona, Liverpool and Sporting Lisbon are also all in action on Wednesday, looking to record positive results to remain in the top 10 position in the league phase.

With another nine matches scheduled to be played, we have a look at the top games and offer our predicted outcomes.

The hosts are in 31st place with three points ahead of the crucial match, knowing that defeat will definitely relegate them from the competition.

They have an uphill task against fifth place Barcelona who are seeking a fifth consecutive victory in all competitions.

Barca are coming off an impressive 3-2 comeback victory against Villarreal over the weekend, while Sabah were 2-0 winners against Kapaz in the Azerbaijan league.

Raphinha has scored six goals in his last four Champions League appearances and has 23 goals in his last 26 appearances in the competition.

The Brazilian and Lamine Yamal will be the main attacking outlets for the visiting team, who are tipped as favorites to win convincingly.

Liverpool will be boosted by their emphatic 4-0 win against Manchester United over the weekend.
