Somalia vs Ghana AFCON Qualifier prediction: Match preview, team news, current form & h2h

WhatsAppFacebookX

Ghana and Somalia meet for the second time in four days in the ongoing 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifiers on Monday evening.

The Black Stars were 2-0 winners when the two sides met last Thursday, courtesy of goals from Felix Afena-Gyan and Jonas Adjetey.

Following the victory, a first in their quest to qualify for the 2027 AFCON, Ghana are in third place in Group C with three points. Ivory Coast lead the standings with nine points, three more than Gambia in second.

Although Somalia are officially the home team, the match will once again be played at the Essipong Sports Stadium in Ghana, with Somalia unable to host the fixture at a CAF-approved home venue.

Another three points would take the Black Stars to six points and put them firmly back into the qualification battle.

However, they cannot afford to drop many more points because they still have difficult fixtures against the group leaders later in the campaign.

Somalia come into the game rooted to the bottom of the group without a point after losing all three of their fixtures so far.

The first leg win ended Ghana&#8217;s 37-month wait for an AFCON qualifying win and gave the new interim technical team a winning start in competitive football.

The Black Stars will now be looking to build momentum by winning the second game of the double-header.

The match kicks off at 6PM E.A.T at the Essipong Sports Stadium

There are no changes expected from the Somalia side that lost last Thursday, with Faisal Othman and Mubashir Nour providing the attacking options up front.

Musa Bile had a couple of chances after coming on as a substitute in the first leg but he may have to settle for a place on the bench.

Ghana have received several major boosts during this international window as Mohammed Kudus returned after a lengthy absence and immediately produced an assist.

Jordan Ayew returned after recovering from a groin injury and is expected to feature in the starting XI.

Mohammed Salisu and Alidu Seidu have also returned to strengthen the defence.

These sides have played against each other three times in their history &#8211; with Ghana winning all of the matches.

They have scored nine goals against Somalia and have not conceded any, making them favorites heading into this game.

Ghana have superior squad quality, greater international experience and have already beaten Somalia 2-0 only four days ago. They also desperately need another three points.

An away win is therefore the most straightforward selection for this particular game. Winning to nil also provides and attractive market.
