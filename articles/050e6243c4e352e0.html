"We've adjusted": Mash Poa announces immediate travel schedule changes

Mash East Africa Ltd, the company behind the Mash Poa bus brand, has updated its departure schedules on two major routes serving Western Kenya , while simultaneously alerting travellers to a surge in online payment fraud targeting its customers.

The revised timetable, published through the company&#039;s official social media channels, covers services on the Nairobi-to-Busia and Nairobi-to-Port Victoria corridors.

Mash Poa said the changes are intended to improve operational efficiency and the overall travel experience for passengers headed to the Nyanza and Western regions.

Passengers travelling to Port Victoria will board at 8:00 PM from any of three Nairobi pick-up points: the Nairobi Railways office, the Accra Road office, or the Kangemi office.

The route heads westward through Nakuru and Kisumu before continuing through Kombewa, Akala, Ndori, Bondo, Siaya, and Boro, with the journey concluding at Nyadorera and Port Victoria.

The Busia route, which passes through Nakuru , Kisumu, Maseno, Luanda, Yala, and Bumala, will now run on a split schedule to handle higher passenger volumes.

Busia A departs at 8:30 PM, while Busia B follows at 9:15 PM. Both services operate from the same three Nairobi boarding locations.

Travellers looking to avoid queues at the terminal can book seats in advance through the company&#039;s website, mash-poa.com, or by calling customer service on 0730 889 000 or 0733 623 260.

Alongside the schedule announcement, Mash East Africa issued a firm warning over fraudulent payment requests being made in the company&#039;s name.

The operator stated clearly that it does not receive money through personal phone numbers, mobile money wallets such as Pochi la Biashara, or individual bank accounts.

The company directed all passengers to settle fares exclusively via its official M-Pesa Paybill number, 857 988, and cautioned travellers to verify payment details before sending any money.

The advisory closed with the Swahili phrase "Kaa chonjo usigongwe!", meaning "Stay alert so you don&#039;t get scammed."

The schedule revisions come as travel demand along the Nairobi-Western Kenya corridor remains high, with the route serving as a key link between the capital and communities near Lake Victoria and the Ugandan border

In other news, Easy Coach updated fares for Western Kenya and Nyanza routes , with tickets from Nairobi ranging from KSh 1,550 to KSh 1,950.

The operator has also launched an early booking campaign ahead of the December festive season.

In previous years, buses to Kisumu and Busia have been among the first to sell out, with bookings filling through the end of December.

Travellers who wait until the last minute may face queues and fewer seats on the journey home.
