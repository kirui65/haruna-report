FORTRESS-SSA Master's Scholarship 2026/2027 at Moi University, Kenya – Fully Funded Postgraduate Opportunity for African Students

WhatsAppFacebookX

The FORTRESS-SSA consortium is inviting applications from eligible African students and public health professionals for its 2026/2027 Master&#8217;s Scholarship Programme at Moi University, Kenya . The scholarship provides an opportunity for qualified candidates to pursue advanced postgraduate studies in public health, infectious diseases, and field epidemiology.

The programme is designed to strengthen Africa&#8217;s capacity to prevent, detect, investigate, and respond to infectious disease threats through advanced education, research, and professional training.

One fully funded scholarship is available for a period of up to two years at the Moi University School of Public Health. Successful applicants will receive financial support covering tuition fees, international return airfare, monthly living expenses, medical insurance, and selected research and learning materials.

The scholarship is particularly suitable for African public health professionals, healthcare practitioners, researchers, and graduates interested in strengthening disease surveillance, epidemiological research, and public health systems across the continent.

The FORTRESS-SSA scholarship aims to develop skilled African professionals capable of addressing infectious disease challenges through specialised postgraduate training and evidence-based public health interventions.

Hosted by the Moi University School of Public Health in Eldoret, Kenya, the programme offers specialised academic training in three key areas:

The scholarship supports the development of expertise required to strengthen national and regional health systems, improve infectious disease preparedness, and promote effective public health responses across African countries.

The selected scholar will undertake a relevant Master&#8217;s degree programme during the 2026/2027 academic year.

Applicants can apply for scholarship support to pursue one of the following postgraduate programmes at Moi University:

The Master of Public Health programme focuses on developing advanced knowledge and professional competencies in public health practice, population health, disease prevention, health promotion, and public health policy.

The programme is suitable for professionals seeking to contribute to healthcare planning, health systems strengthening, disease prevention, and public health research.

2. Master of Science in Field Epidemiology and Laboratory Training (FELTP)

This programme focuses on strengthening practical expertise in field epidemiology, laboratory surveillance, disease investigation, and outbreak response.

It is relevant to professionals interested in disease surveillance, epidemiological investigations, laboratory systems, and the prevention and control of infectious diseases.

3. Master of Science in Infectious Diseases and Global Health (IDGH)

The programme provides specialised training in infectious diseases, global health challenges, disease prevention, and research approaches for improving health outcomes.

It is suitable for candidates interested in infectious disease research, public health interventions, and strengthening healthcare responses to emerging and existing disease threats.

The scholarship offers an important opportunity for African professionals to acquire advanced postgraduate qualifications while contributing to the development of stronger public health systems across the continent.

Through specialised training in public health, field epidemiology, and infectious diseases, the programme supports the development of professionals equipped to address emerging health threats, strengthen disease surveillance, and improve public health decision-making.

The post-study commitment to National Ministries of Health and Public Health Institutes also promotes the application of acquired knowledge within African health institutions.
