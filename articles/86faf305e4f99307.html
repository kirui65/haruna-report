Amnesty International Kenya Call for Submissions 2026: East Africa Data Governance Conference 2027

WhatsAppFacebookX

Organisation: Amnesty International Kenya Opportunity: Call for Submissions Conference: East Africa Data Governance Conference 2027 Theme: Designed for Whom? Trust, Accountability, and the Citizen at the Centre of Data Governance Sector: Technology, Human Rights, Digital Rights, Data Governance and Public Policy Submission Deadline: 11 December 2026 Deadline Time: 5:00 PM East Africa Time (EAT) Conference Year: 2027 Submission Formats: Presentations, Discussions, Panels, Roundtables and Workshops

Amnesty International Kenya is part of the global Amnesty International movement, working to promote and protect human rights, freedom, dignity and justice.

Through research, advocacy, public engagement and strategic partnerships, the organisation works to address human rights violations and strengthen accountability among governments, institutions and other powerful actors.

Its work on technology and human rights focuses on emerging challenges associated with digital transformation, data protection, online freedoms, privacy and responsible technology governance.

As part of its commitment to advancing digital rights and citizen-centred policies, Amnesty International Kenya is inviting submissions for the East Africa Data Governance Conference 2027.

The East Africa Data Governance Conference 2027 will bring together researchers, practitioners, policymakers, innovators, journalists, civil society organisations and community leaders to explore critical issues surrounding data governance in the region.

Under the theme Designed for Whom? Trust, Accountability, and the Citizen at the Centre of Data Governance , the conference aims to promote discussions and solutions that place citizens&#8217; rights, interests and lived experiences at the centre of data governance systems.

The conference provides a platform for participants to exchange research findings, share practical experiences, present innovative solutions and develop recommendations that strengthen transparency, accountability and responsible data management.

Contributors from different professional backgrounds are encouraged to submit their ideas, research and experiences.

The call for submissions is open to individuals and organisations working in areas related to technology, data governance, human rights, policy development and digital transformation.

Both established professionals and individuals with practical community-level experience are encouraged to contribute.

Amnesty International Kenya is particularly interested in submissions that demonstrate practical relevance, original thinking and opportunities for improving data governance across East Africa.

The roundtable format provides a moderated environment for collaborative discussion and participant engagement.

The workshop format combines short presentations with practical, interactive group activities.

Workshops are particularly appropriate for practical learning, collaborative exercises, skills development and exploring innovative approaches to data governance.
