Kenya will not relent on its bid to ensure clinker self-sufficiency for cement production – Ruto

WhatsAppFacebookX

…as domestic cement consumption exceeded one million tonnes in a single month for the first time in July

President William Ruto has confirmed that the country will not back down from its commitment to ensure domestic clinker self-sufficiency by locally manufacturing the principal cement-making ingredient.

Speaking at a groundbreaking ceremony for the new US$250 million (KSh 32 billion) Bamburi Cement Plc Clinker Plant in Matuga, Kwale County, President Ruto dismissed sceptics, saying the national objective is to produce more of what we consume and retain more value within our economy.

He noted that clinker, the principal ingredient in cement, accounts for a high share of cement production costs. “When we import what we can competitively produce at home, we surrender opportunities to create jobs, develop skills and build industrial capacity. By producing clinker locally, we strengthen our manufacturers, preserve foreign exchange and create wealth in Kenya,” President Ruto stressed.

While commending Pan-African Business Conglomerate Amsons Group, led by Group Managing Director Mr Edha Nahdi, for the US$250 million investment, President Ruto noted that the investment comes at a time of strong growth in our construction industry. In 2025, the sector expanded by 6.8%, he said, while cement production increased by 18%, from 8.85 million tonnes in 2024 to 10.44 million tonnes in 2025.

Said President Ruto, “Our ambition must also extend beyond this market. In 2025, Kenya exported 2.21 million tonnes of clinker, up from million tonnes in 2024, to Uganda and Rwanda. We must improve our competitiveness, reduce transport costs, ensure fair access to clinker and make Kenyan cement a preferred choice at home and across the region.”

By doubling its clinker and cement production, Bamburi Cement, a member of the Amsons Group, will significantly contribute to President Ruto’s 10-year national development roadmap. The bold and ambitious KSh 5 trillion roadmap will be financed by the National Infrastructure Fund (NIF), which aims to transform Kenya into a first-world economy by developing and expanding strategic infrastructure such as roads, rail, ports, airports, and oil pipeline infrastructure that rely heavily on quality cement and concrete products.

In the first eight months of 2026, cement production grew by 14% to 7.62 million tonnes, up from 6.68 million tonnes produced last year, as domestic consumption exceeded one million tonnes in July.

President Ruto noted that, once complete, the plant will produce 5,000 tonnes of clinker daily, or about 1.6 million tonnes annually. The plant near Ngombeni will also increase Bamburi’s clinker production capacity from about one million to 2.6 million tonnes a year, and its cement production capacity from 1.8 million to four million tonnes.

“For families building homes, contractors constructing schools and counties developing roads, a reliable supply of quality cement at competitive prices is essential,” he noted.

He added, “Our Affordable Housing Programme demonstrates how these opportunities reinforce one another. Across all 47 counties, more than 313,000 homes are at various stages of construction, with some already completed, and the programme has generated over 1.1 million job opportunities.”

Through the turnkey clinkerisation plant, Kwale County, he said, has now taken its place on Kenya’s industrial map, joining West Pokot and Kitui in our national drive to turn our mineral resources into productive industries, create jobs, and expand economic opportunities across the country.

Speaking at the groundbreaking ceremony, Amsons Group Managing Director Mr Edha Nahdi reiterated that the new Matuga clinker plant, with best-in-class features including a 6-stage precalciner system, represents one of the firm’s most significant industrial investments through Bamburi Cement in recent years.

“For Amsons Group and Bamburi Cement, this US$ 250 million investment is a statement of our long-term confidence in Kenya and its economic potential. It is also part of our commitment to advance the value of local manufacturing as the engine of East Africa’s economic growth,” said Mr Nahdi.

The plant aligns Bamburi’s production with Amsons Group’s regional growth strategy, strengthening cross-border collaboration, supply-chain efficiency and market resilience across East Africa.

Bamburi Cement CEO Eng. Geoffrey Ndugwa, speaking at the ceremony, attended by Kwale County Governor H.E. Fatuma Mohamed Achani and Bamburi Cement Board Chairman Dr John Simba, among other dignitaries, said the clinkerisation plant construction works will be undertaken by Sinoma CBMI Construction Co., Ltd, one of the leading globally recognised engineering and construction firms in the cement industry.

“Clinker is the core ingredient at the heart of cement manufacturing, and policymakers have been exploring avenues to guarantee domestic self-sufficiency. With this groundbreaking, the new Bamburi Matuga Clinker Plant brings that heart home. This clinkerisation plant will shield Kenyan manufacturers from global supply shocks and currency swings, while lowering the cost of building in Kenya,” said Eng. Ndugwa.

By reducing reliance on imported clinker, the plant will save foreign exchange, help stabilise cement prices and secure supply for the domestic market.

As construction begins on the new Bamburi Matuga Clinker Plant, communities in Kwale and the wider coastal region also stand to benefit from over 10,000 direct and indirect jobs. Beyond jobs, the project will create opportunities for micro, small and medium enterprises (MSMEs) and local businesses in logistics, quarrying, equipment maintenance and other services across the region.

The project will also facilitate skills transfer, giving local people industrial experience that will remain at the Coast long after construction is complete.

Sinoma CBMI Construction Co., Ltd., part of the world-leading China National Building Material Group (CNBM), brings extensive expertise in cement plant engineering, procurement, and construction.
