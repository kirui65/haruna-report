Australian High Commission Nairobi Announces Job Vacancies at KSh 244,216 Monthly

The Australian High Commission in Nairobi has opened applications for multiple Senior Visa Processing Officer (SVPO) positions at the Level 4 (LE4) grade within its Department of Home Affairs immigration section.

The vacancies are available on both a permanent and fixed-term basis, with successful applicants expected to work 37.5 hours per week from the High Commission&#039;s offices along Limuru Road, Rosslyn, Nairobi.

Officers in these positions will assess, review, and finalise visa applications, working under a manager&#039;s general direction while handling moderately complex casework independently.

Duties include applying relevant immigration legislation and policy, investigating the integrity of documents submitted alongside applications, preparing correspondence for visa applicants, liaising with internal and external stakeholders, and supporting risk management and integrity standards across visa processing operations.

The roles also require officers to assist with training new staff and contribute to team tasks as part of a collaborative working environment.

Applicants must demonstrate strong written and oral English communication skills, solid analytical ability, proficiency in Microsoft Word and Excel, and effective time management.

Tertiary qualifications in a relevant field and prior experience in visa decision-making are listed as desirable but not mandatory.

The commencement salary is KSh 244,216 per month, with a three-month probationary period upon appointment.

The start date will be confirmed after the completion of satisfactory medical and police or security clearances conducted in Kenya.

To be considered, all applications must be submitted in English and include a curriculum vitae of no more than two A4 pages combined with a one-page Statement of Claims in a single PDF document.

The statement must address three selection criteria: an example of applying legislation or policy to a decision; a demonstration of high-level English communication with diverse clients; and a situation where the applicant showed initiative to improve workplace practices.

Applications should be sent by email to nair.admin@dfat.gov.au with the subject line formatted as "Application for LE4 – [First Name Last Name]." Shortlisted candidates may be required to sit a language assessment and a work sample test before final selection.

The deadline for submissions is Wednesday, December 9, 2026, at 4:30 pm Nairobi time.

Only eligible applications submitted in the prescribed format will be reviewed, and only shortlisted candidates will be contacted.

Prospective applicants are advised to confirm that they hold the right to work in Kenya before applying, as compliance with local immigration regulations is the responsibility of each employee.
