Zimbabwean President Surprised Kenya's Defence Minister Is Female: "Defence?"

Zimbabwean President Emmerson Mnangagwa appeared caught off guard during a meeting with President William Ruto after being introduced to Kenya&#039;s female Defence Cabinet Secretary, Soipan Tuya.

Mnangagwa was in Kenya for a two-day State Visit that culminated in high-level bilateral engagements with his Kenyan counterpart at State House, Nairobi, on Friday, November 27.

During the engagement, Ruto introduced members of his Cabinet and senior military officials to the visiting Head of State.

However, an unexpected moment unfolded when the Kenyan president introduced Tuya, who heads the country&#039;s Ministry of Defence.

In a video capturing the exchange, Mnangagwa appeared surprised when Ruto introduced the Cabinet Secretary.

The Zimbabwean leader looked towards Ruto and seemingly sought clarification, asking, "Defence?"

The brief exchange prompted laughter from the two presidents and other leaders present. Tuya&#039;s presence in the Defence docket is particularly notable given the traditionally male-dominated nature of military leadership.

She is the third woman to serve as Kenya&#039;s Defence Cabinet Secretary, following Raychelle Omamo and Monica Juma.

President Ruto appointed Tuya to the Defence docket on August 7, 2024, following the reconstitution of his Cabinet after the dissolution of his previous Cabinet.

She was sworn into office on August 8, 2024, replacing Aden Duale , who was moved to the Ministry of Environment, Climate Change and Forestry.

Tuya officially took over the Defence Ministry on August 12, 2024.

Beyond the light-hearted exchange, the two presidents co-chaired the inaugural session of the Kenya-Zimbabwe Bi-National Commission (BNC), marking an elevation of diplomatic relations between the two countries.

They also witnessed the signing of seven memoranda of understanding covering affordable housing and appropriate building technologies, immigration, diplomatic training, customs administration, arts, culture and heritage.

Other agreements involved trade promotion through Kenya&#039;s Export Promotion and Branding Agency and Zimbabwe&#039;s ZimTrade, as well as media collaboration between the Kenya News Agency and Zimbabwe&#039;s Inter-African News Agency.
