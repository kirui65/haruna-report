Canada to Offer 5,500 Slots to Foreign Workers by Making Rural Programme Permanent

Canada has permanently established a rural immigration pathway designed to help smaller communities address long-standing workforce shortages, the federal government announced on November 9, 2026.

Immigration Minister Lena Metlege Diab made the announcement in Pictou County, Nova Scotia, confirming that the Rural Community Immigration Pilot has been converted into a permanent programme under the country&#039;s 2027–2029 Immigration Levels Plan.

The employer- and community-led initiative is structured around local partnerships between businesses and community organisations, with the aim of connecting newcomers and their families to the support networks necessary for long-term settlement.

The government plans to retain the 14 communities currently participating in the pilot, with further expansion to additional communities planned at a later stage.

For the 2027 admissions cycle, the government has set a target of 5,500 spaces under the now-permanent Rural Community Immigration Programme.

The initiative traces its roots to the Rural and Northern Immigration Pilot, which ran from 2019 to 2024 and saw more than 2,800 employers participate.

Over 12,500 candidates and their families were admitted to rural and smaller communities through that pilot.

The current Rural Community Immigration Pilot, launched in 2025, has already shown early impact.

Between January 2025 and August 2026, participating communities vetted and designated more than 1,950 trusted employers facing persistent labour shortages.

Those employers issued over 4,700 job offers and community recommendations, with newcomers helping to fill vacancies in sectors including health care, education, and sales and services.

Jason Guidry, Chief Executive Officer of Pictou County Partnership, said the permanent status of the programme would give rural regions greater certainty when planning for workforce needs.

Amber Manning, Human Resources Manager at WearWell Garments Limited, a Pictou County manufacturer established in 1978, highlighted the practical value of immigration pathways for businesses struggling to find skilled workers locally.

The programme sits alongside other permanent residence pathways, including the Provincial Nominee Programme and federal economic programmes managed through Express Entry, as well as various temporary immigration routes available to employers across Canada.

In other news, the US Department of State clarified the purposes of its three main passport application forms , warning that submitting the wrong one could delay processing.

Form DS-11 is for first-time applicants, children under 16 and individuals who do not qualify for renewal, while DS-82 is reserved for eligible passport renewals by mail or through the online service.

Form DS-5504 is used to correct or update passport information, change a name or replace a passport originally issued with limited validity.

Applicants can use the State Department&#039;s online form guide and fee calculator to identify the correct application, with those applying from outside the US advised to check their nearest American embassy or consulate for country-specific instructions.
