KeNHA Announces 2-Month Road Closure on Busy Nairobi-Nakuru Highway, Lists Alternative Routes

The Kenya National Highways Authority (KeNHA) has announced a two-month closure of the Lower Kabete-Ndumbuini/Kapenguria Road-Uthiru Interchange to allow contractors to carry out reconstruction of the interchange&#039;s ramps, loops, and slip roads.

In a public notice dated Wednesday, November 25, KeNHA confirmed the closure will take effect on Wednesday, December 2, 2026, and remain in force until Sunday, January 31, 2027.

Motorists on the Nairobi-Nakuru Highway corridor are advised to use designated alternative routes for the duration of the works.

KeNHA Director GeneralLuka Kimeli called on road users to adhere to the temporary traffic management plan, follow all posted road signs, and cooperate with police officers and traffic marshals deployed at the site.

Drivers heading from Nairobi along the A8 (Waiyaki Way) towards Ndumbuini have three options available to them.

The first requires exiting at Kabete Technical, proceeding to the Naivasha Road roundabout, using the Uthiru Underpass, and then rejoining the Nairobi-bound carriageway before taking Marigoini Cabro Road to Ndumbuini Shopping Centre.

The second option directs motorists to exit at Leadway Petrol Station, pass through the Uthiru Underpass, turn back onto the Nairobi-bound carriageway, and then access Ndumbuini via Marigoini Cabro Road.

The third route involves continuing to the 87/Muhuri Interchange, situated past Shell Kinoo, and using the 87-Ndumbuini Road near 87 Mall.

Motorists coming from Rironi or Nakuru who need to reach Ndumbuini will be required to turn at the Muhuri Interchange and proceed along the Muhuri-Ndumbuini Road.

Drivers leaving Ndumbuini heading towards the Nairobi-bound A8 carriageway have two alternative paths.

They can take Marigoini Cabro Road directly onto the carriageway or use Ndumbuini Road to access the 87/Muhuri Interchange.

The same interchange also connects those heading towards Rironi to the Nakuru-bound carriageway.

Motorists from Uthiru and Kawangware have been directed to conduct all turning manoeuvres to and from Naivasha Road through either the Uthiru Underpass or the Muhuri Interchange.

KeNHA has released a traffic management map detailing the affected sections, road closures, and alternative routes.

The authority urged motorists to study the alternative routes in advance of travel and to heed instructions from traffic officers and marshals stationed along the diversion corridors.

In other news, KeNHA has warned motorists of partial closures on the Thika Superhighway at Kihunguro as contractors install a pedestrian footbridge.

The hoisting operations were scheduled for Saturday and Sunday nights, November 21 and 22, 2026, between 11:00 p.m. and 4:00 a.m.

Motorists travelling between Nairobi and Thika were advised to use alternative routes via the Shell Petrol Station to rejoin the highway during the closures.

KeNHA urged drivers to exercise caution, follow the designated diversion routes and cooperate with police officers and traffic marshals, noting that the wider construction works would continue until December 20, 2026.
