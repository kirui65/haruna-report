Inside Oburu Oginga's Home with Plush Seats, Open-Air Balcony Overlooking Lush Greenery

ODM party leader Oburu Oginga offered Kenyans a rare look inside his private residence after welcoming youth leader and political mobiliser Oketch Salah for a lunch visit on Friday, November 20.

Salah marked the occasion by sharing two photos on social media, capturing the two men in relaxed, candid conversation at different spots within Oburu&#039;s home.

The first photo places the pair on a spacious open-air balcony, standing side by side, holding hands and smiling at the camera. Salah turned up in a long white kanzu paired with white sneakers, while Oburu kept to his signature style in a brown and orange diamond-patterned long-sleeved shirt, black trousers, grey slip-on shoes and a grey flat cap.

The balcony itself is quite the setting. Large glossy white tiles cover the floor, with faint grey marble veining running through them. A black wrought-iron railing with ornate scrollwork, curved swirls and interlocking circles frames the edge.

Behind the two men stretches a wall of dense greenery, with tall palm fronds and leafy trees rising under a bright blue sky dotted with a few white clouds. It is the kind of spot designed for unhurried conversation.

Inside, the second photo shows the two men seated in what looks like a classic family living room, far removed from the stiffness of a political office. Salah sits in a cream-coloured plush armchair to the left, animated as he makes a point, while Oburu is relaxed on a larger beige sofa with dark wooden armrests, listening attentively.

Between them sit two round coffee tables with white marble tops and striking gold metal bases, one holding a glass of water.

The room is finished with light blue and sheer white curtains letting in natural light, a large abstract wall painting in gold and brown tones, decorative gold vases on a side table, and a patterned throw draped over the sofa.

Accompanying the photos was a heartfelt message from Salah.

Oburu took over as ODM party leader following the death of his younger brother, Raila Odinga , on October 15.

Since stepping into the role, he has been receiving delegations, youth leaders and party allies at his home as he works to consolidate ODM and define its path forward.

Salah, known for his "Tunataka Pawa!" rallying call for youth empowerment, has been among his most prominent supporters.
