Mudavadi Rallies Western Kenya Behind Ruto as Govt Unveils Multi-Billion Projects

Prime Cabinet Secretary Musalia Mudavadi has called on residents of Western Kenya to throw their full support behind President William Ruto ahead of the next general election, arguing that political opposition has historically failed to deliver tangible development to the region.

Speaking in Butere on Friday, November 20, Mudavadi invoked the legacy of the late Martin Shikuku, widely regarded as a standard-bearer of opposition politics in Western Kenya , to make his case that opposition politics cannot be a permanent destination for the region&#039;s leaders.

The Prime Cabinet Secretary went further to predict that Ruto would secure a second term in a free and fair election, declaring his full commitment to the partnership with the President.

President Ruto, also speaking at rallies in the region, announced a sweeping package of government investments for Kakamega County under the affordable housing programme. He said the government had committed KSh 21 billion to construct housing units, hostels, and markets across the county.

Specifically, the President said 850 housing units are under construction in Kakamega, alongside a planned 14-storey building and an additional 2,000 units to be built in the area. He added that 20 markets are either complete or ongoing in the county.

Ruto also announced a KSh 3 billion allocation from the Housing Fund to construct hostels for 11,000 university students in Kakamega, with hostels to be built within every constituency.

Ruto linked the investment surge to the formation of the broad-based government with the late Raila Odinga , which he said was designed to end political divisions and distribute development equitably across Kenya.

He dismissed critics who questioned why high-rise construction was being extended to towns such as Butere, arguing that the government had already delivered similar projects in Nairobi, Eldoret, Kisumu, Mombasa, and Machakos.
