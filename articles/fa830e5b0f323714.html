County pledges staff, equipment for Saos Dispensary as it accelerates SHA drive

WhatsAppFacebookX

Uasin Gishu County Chief Officer for Preventive and Promotive Health Services Dr Billy Lubanga on Monday visited Saos Dispensary in Ziwa Ward, Soy Sub-County, assuring residents of stronger services through extra staff, laboratory equipment and expanded care, while urging full uptake of the Social Health Authority (SHA) cover.

Standing at the facility, Dr Lubanga told staff and community members:

“We are committed to improving healthcare delivery here. Additional staff will be deployed and laboratory equipment provided to enhance diagnostic services so that patients get better care closer to home.”

He stressed the critical role of SHA registration.

“Register with the Social Health Authority. This cover enables you to access healthcare services beyond dispensaries to higher-level health facilities when necessary. It is the pathway to quality care without the fear of catastrophic costs.”

Lubanga also highlighted the Facility Improvement Fund (FIF) as a key tool for expanding and upgrading facilities.

He noted that local graduates holding health-related qualifications would be considered for jobs as Saos Dispensary grows.

The official further called on Community Health Promoters (CHPs) to remain “the eyes of the Health Department in the community” by strengthening service delivery and linking residents to care.

The Chief Officer reaffirmed his commitment to the facility’s continued growth and paid tribute to Governor Dr Jonathan Bii Chelilim for sustained investment in the county’s health sector.

Saos Facility In-Charge Bethwel Kurgat welcomed the visit. “We appreciate the department’s commitment to addressing the facility’s needs,” he said.

“Additional staff, laboratory equipment and improved access to SHA services will go a long way in enhancing service delivery, reducing the challenges faced by patients and ensuring the community receives quality healthcare closer to home,&#8221; remarked Kurgat.

The visit comes as Uasin Gishu intensifies efforts to enrol residents under SHA, the national scheme designed to deliver universal health coverage.

The County latest data have shown registration figures rising steadily, with more than 515,000 residents enrolled at one recent count (around 44–45 per cent coverage), while over 200 health facilities across the county have been onboarded.

Nationally, enrolment has surpassed 26 million and continues to climb toward broader targets.

It is important to note that SHA enables free or heavily subsidized services at primary facilities and seamless referral to higher-level care, protecting households from the high cost of treatment.

The County leadership has repeatedly urged registration through facility desks, CHPs and the *147# USSD code, with special support for the elderly, persons with disabilities and vulnerable groups under Governor Bii’s administration.

Officials say the combination of stronger local facilities like Saos Dispensary and widespread SHA uptake is central to bringing reliable, affordable healthcare within reach of every household in Ziwa Ward and beyond.
