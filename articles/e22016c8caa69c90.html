Kenya's Cabinet Secretary Honoured With Global Alliance- APRA's Global Icon Award

Kenya&#x27;s Cabinet Secretary, Hon. William Kabogo, has been honoured with the prestigious Global Icon in Artificial Intelligence, Public Relations & Digital Economy Leadership Award by the Global Alliance and the Africa Public Relations Association in Abuja, Nigeria.

Kenya's Cabinet Secretary, Hon. William Kabogo, has been honoured with the prestigious Global Icon in Artificial Intelligence, Public Relations & Digital Economy Leadership Award by the Global Alliance and the Africa Public Relations Association in Abuja, Nigeria.

The award recognises his visionary leadership, transformative policy execution, and pioneering contributions to Artificial Intelligence, national connectivity, digital transformation, and the advancement of Kenya's digital economy.

The recognition celebrates Hon. Kabogo's commitment to positioning Kenya at the forefront of the Fourth Industrial Revolution through the promotion of responsible and ethical AI, expanded digital infrastructure, innovation, and an enabling environment for technology-driven enterprise, digital skills, and economic opportunity.

Beyond technology and infrastructure, the award places particular emphasis on his contribution to the evolving role of public relations and public communication in modern governance.

Hon. Kabogo has championed the understanding that government communication must go beyond announcements and information dissemination. It must be a deliberate process of engaging citizens, listening to their concerns, building understanding, strengthening institutional credibility, and ensuring that public policies and programmes are communicated in ways that are accessible, relevant, and meaningful to the people they serve.

His approach underscores the growing importance of strategic public relations in government—not merely as a publicity function, but as an essential pillar of governance, public participation, accountability, trust-building, and national development.

In an era defined by rapid technological change, information disorder, and increasingly discerning citizens, effective public communication has become critical to the success of public policy. Hon. Kabogo's leadership reflects a commitment to modernising government communication through digital platforms, strengthening stakeholder engagement, embracing innovation, and fostering a more responsive and citizen-centred approach to public information.

The recognition also celebrates his efforts to strengthen Kenya's digital ecosystem through expanded broadband connectivity, support for innovation and technology startups, digital inclusion, and international cooperation. These initiatives are helping create opportunities for citizens and businesses while supporting Kenya's ambitions as a competitive digital economy.

The award further acknowledges his role in advancing a progressive national narrative—one that communicates Kenya's ambitions, achievements, opportunities, and place in the global technology landscape with confidence and purpose.

By bringing together Artificial Intelligence, digital economy leadership, and Public Relations, the award recognises a broader vision of modern governance: one in which technological progress is matched by effective communication, innovation by inclusion, and policy ambition by public understanding and trust.

Hon. Kabogo's recognition in Abuja is not only a personal honour but also a moment of pride for Kenya, affirming the country's growing influence in digital transformation, strategic communication, innovation, and technology-driven development.

Distinguished African Leaders Among Award Recipients

The award ceremony also recognised an outstanding group of distinguished African leaders and public figures whose contributions to governance, leadership, public service, communication, and national development have earned them various honours.

Among the other recipients recognised at the ceremony were:

- Her Excellency Dr. Netumbo Nandi-Ndaitwah, President of the Republic of Namibia.

- Rt. Hon. Alban Sumana Bagbin, Speaker of Parliament, Republic of Ghana.

- Alhaji Idris Mohammed, FNIPR, FAPRA, Honourable Minister of Information and National Orientation, Federal Republic of Nigeria.

- Hon. William Kabogo Gitau, EGH, Cabinet Secretary for Information, Communications and the Digital Economy, Republic of Kenya.

- Hon. Dr. Omphile Marupi, Deputy Minister of Information, Publicity and Broadcasting Services

- Hon. Cornelius Mweetwa, former Minister of Information and Media, Republic of Zambia.

- Hon. Felix Kwakye Ofosu, Minister for Government Communications, Republic of Ghana.

- Dr. Shadrick Namalomba, Government Spokesperson and Minister of Information and Communications Technology, Republic of Malawi.

The presence and recognition of such eminent personalities underscored the continental significance of the occasion and celebrated the diverse contributions of African leaders and practitioners to governance, public communication, institutional leadership, and development.

The gathering also highlighted the growing importance of strategic communication, public relations, digital innovation, and leadership in shaping Africa's future and strengthening the continent's voice on the global stage.
