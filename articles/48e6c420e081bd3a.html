Maa festival chaos exposes the fault lines tearing Narok apart ahead of 2027

WhatsAppFacebookX

News summary

The Maa Cultural and Tourism Festival in Narok County descended into violence on Saturday when clashes between rival political factions erupted at Sekenani Gate, injuring at least ten people, scattering chairs across the dais and forcing security forces to form a protective cordon around President William Ruto as chaos swept through what was meant to be a celebration of Maasai heritage and cultural pride.
