Edna Karijo Wins Women In Tech Leadership Award At Africa Tech Festival 2026

WhatsAppFacebookX

Edna Karijo, Associate Director at eMobilis Technology Training Institute, has won the Women in Tech Leadership Award at the Africa Tech Festival Awards 2026. The winners were announced at a gala evening in Cape Town on 18 November, during the 2026 edition of Africa Tech Festival.

The award recognises women who have shown innovation, excellence and leadership in technology across the continent. Edna was shortlisted alongside Majda Lahlou Kassi of Ericsson and Shireen Powell of Project Isizwe.

Karijo leads digital skilling and inclusion work at eMobilis where she has led transformative programmes including Google&#8217;s Digital Skills for Africa, Ajira Digital Program and the Norwegian Refugee Council&#8217;s area-based livelihoods digital skills initiative. She is also a co-author of She Leads , a book of lessons from women leaders. She was also among the 2025 CIO Africa&#8217;s Top 45 Most Influential Women In Digital Transformation.

&#8220;This recognition represents far more than a personal achievement,&#8221; said Edna Karijo. &#8220;It has been a journey of dedicating many years to advocating for access to digital skills, digital inclusion, empowering communities, and creating opportunities for youth, young women, and underserved populations to benefit from technology. All this was made possible through the work at eMobilis Mobile Technology Institute and Internet Society Kenya.&#8221;

Edna also congratulated the other winners and thanked the Africa Tech Festival organisers and judges, as well as her family, colleagues, partners and networks. &#8220;This recognition belongs to all of us,&#8221; she said.

David Monaghan, Vice-President of Africa Tech Festival, said the quality of this year&#8217;s entries reflects the depth of Africa&#8217;s technology ecosystem, from multinationals to start-ups building impactful platforms.
