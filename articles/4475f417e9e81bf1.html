Kenyan Adtech firm Suss Ads brings ChatGPT advertising to seven African markets

WhatsAppFacebookX

A key element of the Kenyan launch is the integration of M-PESA for campaign funding.

Suss Ads, the Kenyan advertising technology firm, has launched its ChatGPT advertising solutions in seven African markets. This is a new conversational AI channel for brands and agencies who want to be able to reach consumers as they&#8217;re actively researching a product. The markets are Kenya, Nigeria, South Africa, Egypt, Morocco, Algeria and Tunisia. There&#8217;s also a localised M-PESA payment integration in Kenya to remove the foreign currency hurdle for local advertisers.

Suss Ads was founded in Nairobi in 2021 with the intention of the rollout being a bridge between the global AI platforms and the local commercial infrastructure. As consumers increasingly turn to conversational interfaces to compare services, look at universities, check out financial products and plan their travels, the limitations of interruptive advertising are becoming more and more apparent. Rather than being seen by users as they&#8217;re passively scrolling through social media or display ads, Suss Ads will appear in the conversation as the user is actively in the consideration phase of the purchase journey.

As per the rollout plan these ads will be clearly labelled and appear separately from the organic Chatgpt responses so that there is no possibility of advertising paying to influence or alter the platform’s answers and recommendations in any way. They are currently available to eligible adult users on the ChatGPT Free and Go plans and are being offered as an additional layer to the existing digital media mix rather than as a direct replacement.

A key element of the Kenyan launch is the integration of M-PESA for campaign funding. Getting access to international ad networks often involves the use of US dollars and international credit cards which creates a whole host of admin and cash flow issues for the local businesses. Suss Ads removes that barrier by allowing businesses to plan their budgets and pay for their ad spend via mobile money. It&#8217;s a massive enabler for SMES who are looking to trial AI driven customer acquisition in conjunction with a well established local partner.

Beyond payment processing the company offers end to end campaign management covering creative, media and performance. For the multi-national brands operating across North, West, East and Southern Africa the expansion also enables the delivery of coordinated cross border campaigns through the regional partner.

The conversational AI advertising is an extension of Suss Ads’ wider technology stack which over the past 5 years has evolved from traditional digital media buying through to programmatic, rich media, video, connected TV and digital out of home. The stack is built around a number of specialist units which take the consumer from the first point of discovery to the point of conversion. Suss Media is responsible for multi-channel distribution, Suss Studio for the development of interactive creative, Suss Intelligence for the data driven reporting and Suss CX for the automated customer conversations across WhatsApp, Telegram and social.

Suss Ads operates across Kenya, Tanzania and the UAE and works with clients in telecoms, financial services, consumer goods, education, entertainment and technology. The model is to combine global AI advertising inventory with local mobile money infrastructure and local campaign support to help African businesses adapt to the changing way consumers are discovering products and to reach high-intent audiences at the point of decision.
