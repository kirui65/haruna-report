AFCON 2027: Kenya Confirms All Host Venues, Training Facilities Will Be Completed By December

WhatsAppFacebookX

The Kenyan government has assured that every sports facility earmarked for the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations (AFCON) will be completed by the end of December.

Sports Principal Secretary Elijah Mwangi confirmed that ongoing construction and renovation projects across both primary match stadiums and training grounds remain firmly on schedule for year-end handover.

Speaking on Thursday at the Moi International Sports Centre, Kasarani, following an inspection tour and a briefing with contractors, Mwangi expressed satisfaction with the pace of work. He noted that key upgrades at Kasarani include pitch preparation, skybox construction, relocation of the Venue Operations Centre, and the installation of electronic ticketing turnstiles.

&#8220;We are confident that the December deadline we have set will be met. With the resources we have, the contractor is capable of doing extensive mobilization of equipment and personnel to meet the end-of-year deadline,&#8221; Mwangi said.

&#8220;The progress registered, coupled with intensified mobilisation of personnel, equipment and resources, gives us confidence that our December deadline is achievable and that the facilities will meet all requisite CAF standards,&#8221; Mwangi explained.

&#8220;We are working closely with stakeholders to expedite critical works, streamline logistics and accelerate the delivery of essential equipment to safeguard our completion timelines. Kenya remains firmly on course to deliver a world-class tournament that will showcase our infrastructure capabilities, sporting excellence and exceptional hospitality while cementing our position as a premier destination for international sporting events.&#8221;
