William Ruto Secretly Travels To Congo Amid Strained Kenya-DRC Relations

President William Ruto has travelled to Oyo, in the Republic of Congo, to attend a high-level four-party meeting convened by Congolese President Denis Sassou Nguesso.

The Congo government announced on Sunday, November 21, that Ruto had arrived in Oyo to participate in the gathering, which was described as a high-level diplomatic engagement initiated by Nguesso.

According to Congolese media reports, the meeting is also expected to bring together Democratic Republic of Congo President Félix Tshisekedi and Togolese Prime Minister Faure Gnassingbé, though no official confirmation of the full attendee list had been issued at the time of publication.

Ruto is reported to have left Kenya through Kisumu International Airport on Saturday after concluding a series of engagements with residents during a tour of Western Kenya .

The departure attracted attention given the low-profile nature of the trip and the diplomatic sensitivities surrounding Kenya-DRC relations.

The meeting takes place against the backdrop of a difficult period in relations between Nairobi and Kinshasa. Tensions first escalated in December 2023 when the Alliance Fleuve Congo (AFC), a political-military coalition linked to the M23 rebel movement, held a launch event in Nairobi.

The DRC government objected strongly, accusing Kenya of providing a platform to a group it regards as hostile. Kenya maintained that it had no legal basis to prevent freedom of political expression.

The relationship deteriorated further in October 2025 when Nairobi hosted a political meeting attended by Congolese opposition figures, among them former DRC President Joseph Kabila, drawing fresh criticism from Kinshasa.

The specific agenda of the Oyo meeting has not been officially disclosed, but its timing and composition suggest it is aimed at addressing regional security and diplomatic tensions involving the DRC.
