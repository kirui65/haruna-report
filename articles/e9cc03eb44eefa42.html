Former Oga Obinna employee Dappa Man appears to be stepping fully into his own lane, giving fans a first look at his brand-new studio in a TikTok video posted on November 19, 2026.

The behind-the-scenes clip, filmed in what looks like a home studio or apartment workspace, captures Dappa Man in a series of candid creative moments.

He is seen relaxing on an orange couch, editing photos on a computer monitor, scribbling notes, and working with a collaborator using a measuring tape while someone else sits nearby on a chair.

Each scene carries a recurring red "DTS 👌" caption, giving the whole video a lighthearted, work-in-progress energy.
