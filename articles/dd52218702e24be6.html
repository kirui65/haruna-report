Siaya launches youth AI Hub to promote digital skills, innovation and civic participation

WhatsAppFacebookX

Leaders, youth ambassadors and technology officials on Saturday called on young people in Siaya County to harness artificial intelligence, digital skills and civic participation to solve local and global problems, during the launch of the Siaya Artificial Intelligence Youth Agency (SAIYA) in Siaya town.

​Speaking at the launch, Principal Secretary for Health Services and SAIYA Patron Dr. Ouma Oluga said the hub is meant to give youth a platform to turn ideas into real-world impact rather than serving as a place for handouts.

He noted that Kenya has high technology adoption but needs to move from merely adopting innovations to generating original solutions on the global stage.

​Oluga pointed to recent engagements with global AI firms, including Anthropic, as well as Kenya’s AI Secretariat and a draft AI strategy running from 2026 to 2030, noting that AI can improve healthcare outcomes, patient experiences and other sectors.

However, he stressed that innovation must be paired with strong leadership, integrity and governance, urging young people to register for national identity cards and voter cards so communities have agency in decision-making.

​Kenya Film Classification Board (KFCB) Chief Executive Officer Nelly Muluka reminded the youth that AI is an enabler that carries both opportunities and risks.

While encouraging young creators to produce audiovisual and digital content using available internet and mobile tools, she urged them to create responsible work and avoid explicit, violent or harmful material, pledging mentorship, coaching and support through KFCB and partner networks.

​National Employment Authority (NEA) Director General Mrs. Edith Okoki said AI is rapidly reshaping business operations, service delivery and job creation, making digital skills and adaptability vital for competitiveness.

She urged Siaya youth to use the centre to build practical skills that lead to employment, while also encouraging voter registration so the county’s voice is felt nationally.

​Representing the ICT Authority in the Nyanza region, Engineer Wasonga Ogalo said AI is already a technology of today rather than tomorrow, transforming how people work, learn and deliver services while touching both economic transformation and national security.

He noted that the authority plans to support youth capacity development and improve digital infrastructure in the area, including consistent internet access and proper fibre connectivity.

​Youth ambassadors also spoke on the shift the initiative aims to bring to young people. Ambassador Oweo Andabav from Ugenya sub-county said youth groups sometimes face lucrative offers to disrupt events, adding that SAIYA and his role as an ambassador are helping shift mindsets toward constructive transformation.

​Ward ambassador Florence Lweya from Gem welcomed the focus on leadership and professionalism for young women navigating leadership spaces, pledging that ambassadors would mobilize peers across sub-counties and wards.

Fellow youth ambassador Sharon Adhiambo described the launch as more than opening a building, urging youth to turn ideas in technology, creative arts, entrepreneurship and agriculture into income and judge the centre’s success by the lives it transforms.
