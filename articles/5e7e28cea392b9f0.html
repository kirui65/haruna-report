Dubai Teaching Jobs for Foreigners: Qualifications Needed, Visas and Salaries You Can Expect

WhatsAppFacebookX

Dubai&#039;s private education sector remains one of the most financially attractive destinations for foreign teachers, with tax-free monthly salaries stretching from approximately AED 8,000 (roughly KSh 296,000) for newly qualified staff to more than AED 19,000 (approximately KSh 703,000) for experienced secondary school specialists.

Beyond the base pay, most contracts include furnished accommodation, annual return flights, and medical insurance.

The regulatory body governing private schools in the emirate, the Knowledge and Human Development Authority (KHDA), sets the minimum qualifications for each teaching category.

Nursery teachers working with children aged 45 days to 35 months must hold a Level 3 QF Emirates qualification in early childhood education.

For all other roles, including pre-primary, primary, and secondary subject teaching, a Level 6 bachelor&#039;s degree is the floor.

Subject specialists teaching learners aged 6 to 18 must hold a degree relevant to their area, alongside at least one year of recent classroom experience or six months of supervised practice.

Every teacher in a Dubai private school must hold a KHDA teaching permit. Applications are submitted by the employing school, not the individual, through the KHDA Educator Management System.

The authority typically takes between 5 and 15 working days to review submissions.

Foreign qualifications must be attested by the UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs and the applicant&#039;s home country embassy in the UAE.

The Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research must separately issue an equivalency certificate.

These attestation costs generally fall between AED 1,500 and AED 3,000, and the burden sits with the teacher. KHDA licensing fees, by contrast, are covered by the school.

Permits are institution-specific. A teacher who changes schools must have the original permit cancelled and a fresh application submitted.

Those considering a mid-contract move should check notice period obligations carefully, as violations can trigger a 90-day delay before a new appointment notice is issued.

Schools must complete safer recruitment checks before hiring, including at least two professional references and police clearances from any country where the applicant has lived in the past five to ten years. Under Dubai&#039;s 2026 safeguarding framework, staff are barred from using personal devices, private contact details, or personal social media accounts to communicate with students; all contact must go through institutionally approved channels.

On pay, there is no KHDA-mandated salary scale. Packages vary by curriculum, with British and IB curriculum secondary maths teachers earning between AED 8,500 and AED 19,000 per month depending on experience, while primary teachers typically see salaries in the AED 12,600 to AED 13,800 range.

End-of-service gratuity is calculated on basic salary only: 21 days&#039; basic pay for each of the first five years, then 30 days per year beyond that. A teacher on AED 10,000 basic who completes two years receives approximately AED 14,000. Kenyan applicants should verify that their contract places a reasonable portion of total compensation under "basic salary" rather than allowances, as the split directly affects gratuity.

Degree attestation and equivalency processing can take 8 to 12 weeks, so candidates planning a move should begin paperwork well in advance of their intended departure date
