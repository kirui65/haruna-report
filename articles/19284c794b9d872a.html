Farida Idris, a Kenyan businesswoman and strategic communications specialist, has been named among Kenya&#039;s 100 Most Influential Muslims at the seventh edition of the annual recognition initiative held in Mombasa.

Farida, who leads FID & Co. Africa as founder and chief strategist, was honoured for her work in entrepreneurship, leadership, strategic communications and women&#039;s empowerment.

She told TUKO.co.ke that before establishing her own firm, she built nearly two decades of experience within the media and business development sectors.

In 2026, she launched the Suhba Series, a platform dedicated to conversations around faith, leadership, entrepreneurship, financial independence and personal growth among women.

Speaking at the ceremony, Idris made a case for celebrating contributors while they are still present to receive it.

Looking ahead, she said her focus will be on deepening strategic partnerships for both FID & Co. Africa and the Suhba Series, with a clear priority on creating spaces that elevate women&#039;s voices.

In the same breath, former Nation Media Group journalist Mohamed Ahmed received recognition under the Business & Management category at the annual awards.

Ahmed, now Head of Sales at Mombasa-based Megna Homes, spent six years covering security and political affairs along Kenya&#039;s Coast Region.

The Muslim Media organised the awards programme, which honoured individuals across fields including leadership, law, media and community service.
