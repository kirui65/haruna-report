Kalonzo opens Machakos campaign with rule of law pledge

WhatsAppFacebookX

The Wiper leader draws hundreds to Wamunyu as his 2027 State House bid moves from declaration to doorstep.

Wiper Party leader Kalonzo Musyoka drew a warm reception in Mwala Constituency, Machakos County, at the weekend, addressing residents at Wamunyu and attending Sunday worship at AIC Mbaikini as part of an intensifying grassroots campaign ahead of the August 2027 General Election.

Speaking to residents, Kalonzo framed his presidential bid around constitutional governance and institutional accountability. &#8220;We want a nation where the constitution is supreme, where institutions serve the people, where justice is accessible to all, and where no Kenyan is above the law,&#8221; he said, closing his address with the declaration: &#8220;It is liberation time.&#8221;

He was joined by Democratic Party leader Justin Muturi, Machakos Senator Agnes Kavindu, Women Representative Joyce Kamene, Deputy Governor Francis Mwangangi, Members of Parliament Julius Mawathe and Makali Mulu, and Wiper Party Foundation officials — a delegation signalling the organisational depth the former Vice-President is assembling in Ukambani ahead of the race.

For Kalonzo, the Mwala visit is a reminder that the road to State House runs through constituency churches, community gatherings, and the patient work of earning trust one ward at a time.
