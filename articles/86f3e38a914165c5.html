KURA Declares 10-Day Closure of Nairobi Road, Provides 3 Alternative Routes

The Kenya Urban Roads Authority (KURA) has announced a 10-day partial closure of a section of Gitanga Road in Kawangware, Nairobi, beginning Thursday, November 26, 2026.

According to a public notice issued by KURA, the affected stretch runs between kilometre 0+520 and kilometre 0+620.

The closure will remain in force from 6:00 AM on November 26 until 6:00 PM on December 5, 2026, to allow workers to safely construct a cross culvert intended to strengthen drainage capacity along the road.

KURA noted that although vehicular access through the section will be restricted, a pedestrian walkway will remain open throughout the construction period to ensure the continuous movement of foot traffic.

KURA has published a colour-coded diversion plan to guide motorists around the construction zone. Drivers heading into Kawangware are directed to use Naivasha Road, which connects to a diversion route leading back towards Gitanga Road.

Vehicles travelling towards Nairobi or seeking local access should take Salim Road, connecting through Macharia Road before rejoining Gitanga Road in the direction of the city centre.

Stricter conditions apply to public service vehicles and heavy goods vehicles weighing more than 7.5 tonnes.

These categories must use the Naivasha Road route to bypass the construction zone entirely and are not permitted to use the lighter diversion routes designated for private cars.

Traffic marshals and flagmen will be stationed at key intersections to assist with vehicle flow, and KURA is urging all motorists to follow the instructions of traffic police deployed at the site.

The authority has flagged that the affected section overlaps with a busy commercial zone near the Kawangware Open-Air Market and adjacent roadside traders, making extra vigilance necessary for road users passing through the area.

Apart from the Kawangware road, KURA also temporarily closed to Mlolongo-Kamulu Quarry Road and the alternative routes available to motorists.

The authority has also extended the Eldoret Road closure until 30 December, blaming construction delays linked to a Nairobi River bridge project.
