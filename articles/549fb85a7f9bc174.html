US pact advances Kenya's nuclear energy ambitions

WhatsAppFacebookX

Between 16 and 20 November, Justus Wabuyabo, Chief Executive Officer of the Nuclear Power and Energy Agency (NuPEA), led a multi-agency delegation to Washington, D.C.

The team met at the Harry S. Truman Building to start formal negotiations on a Section 123 Agreement between Kenya and the United States. This bilateral framework governs the transfer of civil nuclear material, equipment and technical expertise under the US Atomic Energy Act.

Signing a Section 123 Agreement is a legal prerequisite for any country seeking to acquire American nuclear reactor technology or fuel services. Without the pact, US companies cannot legally export major nuclear components to Kenyan energy developers.

NuPEA is the state corporation mandated to promote and implement Kenya's nuclear electricity programme. The agency coordinates feasibility studies, site selection and regulatory frameworks required before physical plant construction can begin.

Negotiating with Washington aligns with Kenya's long-term infrastructure planning to diversify its national electricity grid. While the country relies heavily on geothermal, hydro and wind resources, planners have earmarked nuclear generation to provide stable baseload power for industrial growth.

The Washington talks involved officials from multiple Kenyan government institutions. Technical discussions during such negotiations typically cover non-proliferation commitments, physical security standards and environmental safeguards.

Wabuyabo noted that the November meetings marked the formal start of these treaty discussions with American counterparts. Both governments must review and ratify the final text before commercial technology transfers can proceed.

Kenya has spent over a decade developing institutional capacity through partnerships with the International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA). Training local engineers, drafting radiation protection laws and assessing coastal and inland sites remain ongoing priorities for NuPEA.

The original commentary detailing the Washington delegation was authored by Justus Wabuyabo and published by Business Daily Africa on 23 November 2026.
