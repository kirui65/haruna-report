Nairobi health revenue triples to KSh 2.6B as digital reforms and SHA transform county hospitals

WhatsAppFacebookX

News summary

Nairobi County has tripled revenue from its public health facilities from KSh 800 million to KSh 2.6 billion following digital reforms and improved reimbursements under the Social Health Authority, Governor Johnson Sakaja announced yesterday, as the county sets its sights on KSh 4 billion in health facility revenue before the end of the current financial year.
