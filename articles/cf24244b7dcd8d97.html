A clip from Faith Evangelistic Ministry&#039;s 38th Anniversary Sunday Service has taken social media by storm, showing Tanzanian delegates dropping to their knees as Rev. Teresia Wairimu addressed the congregation about Charlene Ruto&#039;s marriage to Tanzanian entrepreneur Isaya Yunge.

Rhema Revivers shared the video on TikTok and Instagram on November 15. It was filmed at FEM Family Church in Karen, Nairobi, where a Tanzanian delegation travelled to attend the anniversary celebrations.

In the footage, Rev Wairimu walks down a red-carpeted aisle in a long, flowing purple robe, microphone in hand, addressing a packed auditorium.

As she speaks, a group of men and women lining the aisle, dressed in suits, white dresses and traditional attire, go down on their knees. The rest of the congregation is seen standing and clapping.

Wairimu described Isaya as a handsome young man who had married Charlene, whom she affectionately called her daughter.

She told the congregation that through the union, Kenya had gifted Tanzania with a preacher, and expressed both love and gratitude towards the Tanzanian visitors, noting that the family had grown to embrace Isaya despite early uncertainty about his intentions.

The sermon came just over a week after Charlene Ruto , daughter of President William Ruto and First Lady Rachel Ruto, wed Isaya Yunge in a white wedding at Malaika Beach Resort in Mwanza, Tanzania, on November 6.

Both President William Ruto and First Lady Rachel Ruto walked her down the aisle. The white wedding followed their traditional Kalenjin koito ceremony, held on 8 August 2026 at the Ruto family&#039;s Intonna Heritage Farm in Kilgoris, Narok County .
