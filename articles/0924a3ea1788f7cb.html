A video of Deputy President Kithure Kindiki being stopped from accessing the podium alongside President William Ruto and First Lady Rachel Ruto has gone viral online, sparking a wave of reactions from Kenyans.

The footage emerged from Tuesday&#039;s 17th anniversary celebrations of the Joyful Women Organisation at State House, Nairobi.

Ruto shared about the event on social media, writing that he had "presided over the 17th anniversary celebrations of the Joyful Women Organisation at State House Nairobi" in the company of Rachel Ruto , who is the patron of the organisation, and Kindiki.

Kindiki arrived in a dark navy-blue suit with a matching jacket and trousers, a white dress shirt, and dark formal shoes.

Ruto opted for a purple suit jacket paired with dark navy trousers, a bright blue tie, and a white shirt, while Rachel wore a long green patterned outfit featuring white floral motifs, complemented by white shoes.

Video footage captured the trio walking side by side towards the stage as the crowd cheered.

However, just before they reached the podium, a female aide stepped in and intercepted Kindiki, directing him to his designated seat instead. Ruto and the first lady continued onto the stage, where the two danced together to a gospel song.

The video spread rapidly on social media, with many saying Kindiki&#039;s predecessor, Rigathi Gachagua , who was impeached and removed from office in October 2024, would speak about it.
