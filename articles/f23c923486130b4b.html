Manchester United Training Drone Footage: What Pochettino’s First Session Showed

WhatsAppFacebookX

Manchester United training drone footage captured part of Mauricio Pochettino’s first session with the squad at Carrington on Thursday, 19 November 2026. Around 12 minutes of video appeared online, showing small-group drills and the new head coach working with players. The footage prompted questions about training-ground privacy and how much Liverpool could learn before their Premier League meeting. Here is what the recording, the club’s official team news and the reported accounts establish.

Mauricio Pochettino’s first Manchester United training session took place as the club prepared to visit Liverpool at Anfield on Sunday, 22 November, with kick-off scheduled for 19:30 East Africa Time. The Argentine had arrived in Manchester on Wednesday after completing his final assignment with the United States national team. His first on-pitch work was filmed by an independent drone operator and later published on YouTube. United also released its own training update, confirming the involvement of two players whose availability had been in doubt.

The video of Pochettino’s first Man Utd training session shows football exercises rather than a confirmed tactical plan for Liverpool. Sky Sports reported that the 12-minute recording begins with rondos, in which players circulate possession while others attempt to intercept, before showing Pochettino guiding a smaller group through work on an adjoining pitch. The footage was recorded from outside the training complex and published online by a drone operator identified as CC Overview.

BBC Sport identified multiple groups of players involved in the session, including younger footballers and regular first-team names. Their presence confirms participation in that part of training, not a place in Sunday’s starting eleven.

Manchester United team news ahead of Liverpool received a more reliable update from the club itself: Harry Maguire and Patrick Dorgu trained at Carrington on Thursday. Maguire had been substituted with a back spasm during the 4-4 draw against Aston Villa, while Dorgu had not featured in Denmark’s recent international matches. Their participation is encouraging, but match readiness and selection remain decisions for the coaching staff.

The club also stated that Lisandro Martinez was available after suspension. It planned Pochettino’s first pre-match press conference for Friday, when a fuller assessment of the squad could be provided.

Questions about Mauricio Pochettino’s Manchester United tactics intensified because the footage shows a small group being coached separately from other players. Sky Sports described positional work and a group briefing, but cautioned that those involved should not be assumed to be the starting line-up. Early-session exercises can illustrate a coach’s methods without establishing his match plan.

Pochettino has previously favoured high-intensity work, pressing and collective movement, yet one filmed session cannot show whether those principles will be adopted immediately or how United will line up at Anfield. Any detailed formation claim would need stronger evidence, such as a confirmed team sheet or the manager’s own explanation.

The Carrington filming controversy centres on the publication of training images that the club had not itself released. Reports said the operator maintained that the drone was flown in unrestricted airspace, stayed within visual range and complied with the relevant requirements. Sky Sports also reported that signs near the complex prohibit drones and that Manchester United declined to comment on the incident.

Those accounts establish a dispute about access and privacy, not a legal determination. Nor is there evidence that Liverpool commissioned the recording or that the video changed either team’s preparations.

Liverpool vs Manchester United on 22 November 2026 is scheduled to be Pochettino’s first match as United head coach, only days after his appointment. United enter the trip amid pressure to improve their Premier League performances, while the manager has called for a brave approach to the Anfield encounter. The recording adds another story to a busy opening week for the Argentine, but his early results and team selections will offer better evidence of his approach than isolated training footage.
