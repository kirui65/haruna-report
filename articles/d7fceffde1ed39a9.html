Nairobi - Kenya Power has announced scheduled maintenance outages across four counties on Sunday, November 15, 2026, affecting residential estates, schools, businesses, and public facilities in Nairobi, Turkana, Machakos, Uasin Gishu, and Nyeri counties.

The notice was published on the utility&#039;s official social media channels on Saturday, November 14, 2026, giving customers roughly 24 hours&#039; advance notice before the interruptions begin.

In Nairobi, the power interruption will run from 12:00 PM to 8:00 PM, making it the longest single outage on the schedule.

KPLC announces areas in Mt Kenya, North Eastern regions to face power interruptions on November 14

Areas affected include Crystal Rivers, Sunset Boulevard, Greatwall Gardens (parts of 1, 3, 4 and 5), Welding Alloys, Riverpark Estate, Kims Business Park, Nova School, and neighbouring customers.

In the North Rift Region, customers across Lokichar, Kasuroi, and Kapese in Turkana County will experience a supply interruption from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

The affected localities include Lokichar Town, Nalemsekon, Kasuroi, Kamarese, Kapese, Arid Summit Hotel, and surrounding customers.

Residents in the Kamukunji area of Uasin Gishu County should expect a power cut from 9:30 AM to 4:30 PM. The outage covers Kamukunji Estate and adjacent customers.

In the Mt. Kenya Region, parts of Nyeri County covering the Mukurweini Boys and Gaturia areas will be without power from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Affected facilities include South Tetu Girls Water Pump, Mukurweini Boys school, Gaturia Market, and neighbouring customers.

Customers in the listed areas can [view Kenya Power&#039;s full maintenance schedule](https://shorturl.at/gbhap) to check for any updates or additional planned outages.
