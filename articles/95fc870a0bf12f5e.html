European Union Opens Applications for KSh 69 Million Cleaning Services Contracts in Kenya

The European Union Delegation to Kenya has invited cleaning service firms to apply for a framework contract to service its Nairobi offices, with the total estimated value of the arrangement standing at KSh 69 million (€460,000) over a period of up to 48 months.

The tender, published on 20 November 2026 on the EU&#039;s Tenders Electronic Daily platform under procedure identifier EEAS/DELKENN/2026/RP/0097, was issued by the European External Action Service (EEAS).

The contract covers cleaning services at the EU Delegation&#039;s offices located at Dunhill Towers, 19th Floor, 115 Waiyaki Way, Westlands, Nairobi.

The procurement is structured as a framework service agreement awarded on the basis of the best price-quality ratio, classified under CPV 90910000 for cleaning services.

No reopening of competition is envisaged once the contract is awarded.

The procedure is restricted, meaning it runs in two stages. In the first stage, interested firms submit a request to participate.

Only shortlisted candidates from that stage will receive an invitation to submit a full tender in the second stage.

Applications are open to any economic operator meeting the selection criteria set out in the procurement documents .

All submissions should be made electronically through the eSubmission system on the EU&#039;s Funding and Tenders Portal. Requests sent by email or letter were not accepted.

Each applicant is required to hold an EU Login account and to register in the Participant Register, through which organisations receive a nine-digit Participant Identification Code (PIC) used as their unique identifier across EU procurement processes.

Firms already registered are instructed to use their existing PICs rather than create new entries.

Interested firms can access full tender details and submit their requests to participate before the deadline on December 21, 2026 at 09:00 Africa/Nairobi time.

Any submission received after that point will be liable to be rejected, with the timestamp generated by eSubmission serving as proof of timely delivery.

Throughout the procedure, direct contact between applicants and the contracting authority is prohibited except in exceptional circumstances.

The EU Delegation also noted that the invitation to participate carried no binding commitment on its part, and the authority reserved the right to cancel the procedure at any point before contract signature without owing compensation to any candidate.

All costs related to preparing and submitting a request to participate are to be borne by the applying firms.
