Konza Technopolis Gets Customs Clearance Status to Ease Trade and Attract Investors

WhatsAppFacebookX

Konza Technopolis has been designated a Customs Area and allocated a customs code by the Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA), paving the way for faster movement of goods and simplified customs procedures for businesses operating within the smart city.

The designation marks a major step towards making Konza&#8217;s Special Economic Zone (SEZ) fully operational, particularly for companies involved in manufacturing, technology development and other businesses that depend on importing and exporting goods.

The development is expected to reduce customs-related delays and improve the ease of doing business, making Konza more attractive to local and international investors.

Technopolis Development Authority Chief Executive Officer John Paul Okwiri said the new status addresses a key requirement for businesses seeking to establish operations within the zone.

“The gazettement of Konza Technopolis as a Customs Area and the allocation of a customs code is a significant step towards making our Special Economic Zone fully operational. It will facilitate the movement of goods, simplify customs processes and create a more enabling environment for investors,” said Okwiri.

Konza Technopolis was gazetted as a Special Economic Zone in July 2019 as part of Kenya&#8217;s efforts to attract investment in technology, innovation and industrial development.

The latest approval strengthens the regulatory framework needed to support businesses operating within the smart city and advances Kenya&#8217;s ambition to become a knowledge-based economy.
