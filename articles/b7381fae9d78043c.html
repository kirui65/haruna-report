Kwale - A Kenya National Examinations Council (KNEC) supervisor is recovering at home after being abducted and severely beaten by suspected Form Four candidates in Kinango Sub-County, Kwale County , following his refusal to allow cheating during the ongoing Kenya Certificate of Secondary Education (KCSE) examinations.

The supervisor had been assigned to oversee exams at Vingurugani Secondary School when he intercepted unauthorised materials that a teacher had smuggled into the examination room.

He confiscated the papers and secured them as evidence, but the materials were subsequently stolen from his locker.

He was then summoned to the Kinango education offices, where a quality assurance official allegedly pressured him to cooperate with the teachers involved. On Monday, he was offered KSh 50,000 to look the other way on the malpractice, an offer he firmly declined.

The assault took place on the evening of Wednesday, November 11. After warning him the previous day that they would attack him upon his return, the students carried out their threat. The supervisor suffered severe cuts to his head and a fractured hand during the ordeal.

He told NTV that he feigned death in order to survive, and his attackers left him for dead after stealing the little money he had on him.

The attackers also made clear their motive and their anger at him specifically.

The supervisor has since filed a report at Kinango Police Station. Detectives have launched investigations to identify all individuals involved in the attack and to establish the full extent of the alleged conspiracy, which the supervisor suspects involves multiple parties.

Despite reporting the matter to authorities, he says he continues to live in fear and believes the network behind the scheme extends beyond the students who carried out the assault.
