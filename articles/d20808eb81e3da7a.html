Kenyans have caught a rare glimpse of the late President Mwai Kibaki&#039;s family home in Othaya, Nyeri County , after a group of TikTok content creators shared footage from their visit to the area.

The adventurers rode along a well-maintained tarmac road before passing the expansive compound, which sits in quiet, leafy surroundings typical of central Kenya&#039;s highlands.

From the roadside, the residence cuts an impressive figure. A large green and black gate with a roof-style design marks the main entrance, flanked by neat cabro paving and a security guard&#039;s chamber.

The boundary is lined with tall trees and a green fence, with patches of yellow flowers providing bursts of colour. Banana trees tower above the perimeter, nodding to the agricultural character of the region.

The TikTokers did not venture inside the compound, but the exterior alone left a strong impression, conveying a sense of calm and comfort befitting a man who spent the better part of his life at the centre of Kenyan public affairs.

Kibaki served as Kenya&#039;s third president from 2002 to 2013. Born in 1931 in Gatuyaini village in Othaya, he maintained a deep connection to the area throughout his political career, having also represented the constituency in Parliament for many years.

He passed away on April 21, 2022, at the age of 90. A state funeral was held in his honour, and on April 30 he was laid to rest at the Othaya home with full military honours.

He was buried beside his wife, the late former first lady Lucy Kibaki, who died in April 2016 and was also interred at the family home.

Since both leaders were laid to rest there, the compound has quietly become a place of reflection for Kenyans who pass through or travel to the area specifically to pay their respects.
