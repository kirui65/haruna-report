Kenyans in US await Sifuna’s arrival

WhatsAppFacebookX

Nairobi Senator and Linda Mwananchi Movement leader Edwin Sifuna during a Sunday service at Jesus Teaching Ministry in Nairobi on October 18, 2026.

As Edwin Sifuna lands in the United States over the Thanksgiving weekend, anticipation is building among Kenyan diaspora communities eager to assess one of the leading contenders in Kenya’s 2027 presidential race.

Beyond the political rallies and fundraising events, many see the visit as an opportunity to engage directly with an aspirant seeking to expand his national appeal and strengthen ties with an increasingly influential constituency abroad. Sifuna’s US trip carries significant political implications, according to Prof Kefa Otiso of Bowling Green State University,

“The diaspora’s most important contribution to these politicians is fundraising for their campaigns and influencing friends and family back home to support them,” says Prof Otiso.

Sifuna’s tour, like previous visits by Kalonzo Musyoka, David Maraga and Rigathi Gachagua, includes a significant fundraising component.

These visits feature paid events, meet-and-greet sessions, photo opportunities and fundraising drives through WhatsApp mobilisation networks. This has raised the stakes for candidates seeking the presidency in 2027.

On the significance of diaspora funding, Prof Otiso said,

“Diaspora funds have become crucial because the diaspora is capable of contributing substantial amounts of money. In Kenya, politicians dish out money to voters. In the diaspora, Kenyans contribute to candidates. This dynamic is what makes diaspora fundraising attractive to politicians of all stripes.”

Linda Mwananchi Movement party leader Edwin Sifuna addresses delegates during a meeting at the party offices.

Linda Mwananchi Movement (LMM)’s diaspora network has organised itself into state chapters across 50 US states. Sifuna’s itinerary begins with the opening of the LMM’s office in Hackettstown, New Jersey, on November 21, 2026. He will then attend a church service in Allentown, Pennsylvania, on November 22, 2026, followed by a business community dinner in Maryland on November 23, 2026.

He will then travel to Washington, DC, for an East Africa Politics Policy Meeting on November 24, 2026, as well as a dialogue with members of the Gen Z and 6.25 movements. Thanksgiving Day will centre on a dinner in Dallas, Texas, before he heads to the Pacific Northwest for a gala dinner.
