Must visit: National Police Service shares 423 venues for December recruitment

The National Police Service has released the full list of 423 venues where it will conduct its police constable recruitment exercise on Thursday, December 10, 2026, running from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.

The recruitment advertisement, signed by Inspector General Douglas Kanja Kirocho, states that the exercise is conducted under Articles 238, 243, 244, and 245 of the Constitution, alongside the National Police Service Act. Candidates who succeed will be enrolled for training as police constables.

To be eligible, candidates must be Kenyan citizens holding a valid national identity card and aged between 18 and 28 years.

The minimum academic requirement is a KCSE mean grade of D+, with at least a D+ in either English or Kiswahili.

Candidates must also be in good physical and medical condition, have no criminal record or pending charges, and meet minimum height requirements of 5 feet 8 inches for men and 5 feet 3 inches for women.

Female applicants must not be pregnant at the time of recruitment or during the full training period.

The 423 recruitment centres are distributed across every sub-county in all 47 counties.

Prospective candidates are required to download and complete the prescribed application form from one of four official websites: www.nationalpolice.go.ke, www.kenyapolice.go.ke, www.administrationpolice.go.ke, or www.dci.go.ke. Forms are also available at any Huduma Centre across the country.

On the day of recruitment, applicants must carry originals and copies of their academic certificates and testimonials, their Kenya National Identity Card, and their birth certificate.

Candidates reporting to the NYS Headquarters in Ruaraka must additionally present an NYS discharge certificate reflecting a conduct rating of at least "Good."

The National Police Service has emphasised that the recruitment exercise is entirely free of charge and open to all eligible Kenyans. It issued a firm warning that bribery, corruption, or submitting false information to influence the process constitutes a criminal offence.

Members of the public are encouraged to report any suspected malpractice at the nearest police station or through the toll-free lines 999, 911, 112, or via #FichuaKwaDCI on 0800 722 203. The service assured that all reports will be handled in strict confidence.
