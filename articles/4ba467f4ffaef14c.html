Zamara launches investment business and four unit trust funds

WhatsAppFacebookX

The launch comes at a time when Kenya’s investment landscape is evolving,

Nairobi, Kenya, 26 November 2026 – Zamara Group today launched Zamara Investments and four Unit Trust Funds, marking a significant milestone in its growth strategy and expansion into Kenya’s investment market.

Following regulatory approval by the Capital Markets Authority (CMA), the Group has introduced four Unit Trust Funds designed to cater to the diverse financial objectives, investment horizons and risk appetites of individuals, businesses and institutional investors.

The four funds comprise the Zamara Money Market Fund (KES), Zamara Money Market Fund (USD), Zamara Fixed Income Fund (KES) and Zamara Global Balanced Special Fund (USD).

The launch builds on the group’s more than three decades of experience in pensions, actuarial services and insurance broking, bringing together three fundamental pillars of financial wellbeing: wealth creation, financial protection and retirement security.

Speaking during the launch, Zamara Group Chief Executive Officer Sundeep Raichura said the expansion into investments represents a natural evolution of the Group’s business and its purpose of creating a financially secure and prosperous society.

“For over three decades, we have helped individuals, families and organisations navigate some of their most important financial decisions. Our entry into investments is a natural evolution of that journey.

“Financial security is not built through pensions or insurance alone. Individuals need opportunities to create wealth, protect what they have accumulated and prepare for the future. Bringing investments into our portfolio of financial solutions, helps us strengthen our ability to support our clients across their entire financial journey and deliver on our promise of Powering Prosperity.

Gideon Chokah, Head of Financing Securities Services (Kenya & East Africa) Standard Chartered Bank, representing Standard Chartered Bank, Trustee and Custodian of the Fund said, “A unit trust does more than bring investors together. It brings together their hopes, ambitions and resources and channels them towards productive opportunities that can improve lives, strengthen businesses and help build the economy.

By pooling savings and directing capital into productive investments, unit trusts contribute to deeper capital markets, support economic activity and help connect the aspirations of individual investors with the growth and development of the wider economy.”

The new investment business has already accumulated KSh 2.2 billion in Assets Under Management (AUM) since it began receiving investments in May 2026, reflecting early investor confidence in Zamara’s investment proposition and providing a strong foundation for its growth ambitions.

The launch comes at a time when Kenya’s investment landscape is evolving, with growing interest in professionally managed investment solutions and increasing demand for accessible and diversified avenues for wealth creation. According to the Capital Markets Authority&#8217;s quarterly report, assets held in collective investment schemes reached KSh 948.7 billion at the end of June 2026, an 11 percent rise from KSh 851.7 billion in March. More than four million Kenyans now hold unit trusts, and 98 percent of them are local retail investors.

The market is also maturing, with money market funds which held about 90 percent of industry assets in 2020, today hold roughly half, as investors move into fixed income and special funds in search of higher returns over longer horizons.

The funds will be available through MyZamara, the Group’s digital platform.
