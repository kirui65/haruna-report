Classic 105 radio presenter Maina Kageni has set tongues wagging after publicly rewarding his personal manager with a brand-new Mercedes-Benz E-Class estimated at KSh 4.5 million.

The moment was filmed and uploaded to Maina&#039;s Instagram on Monday, 24 November 2026, spreading rapidly across TikTok, Instagram and Facebook throughout the week.

The man on the receiving end of Maina&#039;s generosity is Melvin Andati, widely known online as Halfman Flex. He runs the Instagram account @halfman_flex, where he describes himself as "Half Man Half Amazing" and is associated with Halfman House and Halfman Lounge.

Melvin is no stranger to those who follow Maina closely. For years, he has been the quiet engine behind the radio star&#039;s daily life, managing his schedule, coordinating his movements, and regularly chauffeuring him around Nairobi. In countless behind-the-scenes clips and car reveal videos, it is Melvin behind the wheel.

In the video, Melvin is seated in the driver&#039;s seat of a maroon BMW X6, dressed in an olive-green "GAMEON" bomber jacket, a black Balmain Paris t-shirt and a gold chain, with sunglasses resting on his head. Maina, wearing a white hoodie, walks up to him, exchanges a few words, and then leads him out of the BMW.

Melvin&#039;s reaction said everything. He laughed in disbelief, circled the vehicle, ran his hands along the bodywork, peered inside the doors and kept repeating "thank you" before pulling Maina into a long embrace while onlookers filmed the moment.

The Mercedes-Benz E-Class Maina gifted to Melvin belongs to the W213 generation, produced between 2017 and 2023. In Kenya, units from this generation are listed between KSh 3.7 million and KSh 5.7 million, with foreign-used models typically around KSh 4.5 million. It is a popular choice among executives and celebrities who prefer understated luxury.
