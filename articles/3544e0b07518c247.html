German President Steinmeier to open major German-African Business Summit in Accra today

WhatsAppFacebookX

News summary

German President Frank-Walter Steinmeier is expected to officially open the sixth German-African Business Summit (GABS 2026) today, alongside President John Dramani Mahama. The three-day gathering, which began on Monday, November 23, is bringing together business executives, investors, policymakers and industry leaders from Germany and across Africa to explore opportunities for trade, investment and industrial cooperation.
