Pastor Ezekiel Odero, the founder of New Life Prayer Centre and Church in Mavueni, Kilifi County, left his congregation speechless after sharing a personal testimony about a wealthy woman who once pursued him, offered him money, and pushed to become his second wife.

The televangellist recounted the episode during a sermon, framing it as a lesson about temptation, faithfulness, and guarding the life God had built for him.

At the time the incident happened, Ezekiel was not yet a preacher. He was a keyboard player and choir teacher, still early in his walk of faith after getting saved in 2000 at Maximum Miracle Centre under Bishop Pius Muiru in Mombasa.
