Karua, Matiang&#8217;i and Nyoro take opposition message to Kirinyaga

The Kutus rally signals a sharpening alliance as Kenya&#8217;s emerging opposition bloc tests its reach in Mt Kenya heartland.

Martha Karua, former Cabinet Secretary Fred Matiang&#8217;i and Kiharu Member of Parliament Ndindi Nyoro on Sunday took their opposition campaign to Kirinyaga County, addressing residents in Kutus and Ngarubaini in what organisers described as an overwhelmingly attended series of public engagements under the #TuunganeTujikomboe banner.

Karua, speaking after the rally, framed the gathering in deliberately measured terms, pushing back against suggestions that the emerging alliance represented political opportunism. &#8220;Democracy is built on dialogue, engagement and the willingness to find common ground,&#8221; she said. &#8220;Political alliances and partnerships are part of that process, and there is no need to mistake discussion for division.&#8221;

The trio&#8217;s appearance together in Kirinyaga — a county that sits squarely within the Mt Kenya region whose political loyalty is now fiercely contested ahead of the 2027 General Election — carries significance beyond the warm receptions reported at both venues. It represents a visible effort to plant an opposition flag in territory that President William Ruto carried convincingly in 2022.

Karua was direct about the coalition&#8217;s intended focus. &#8220;Our attention must remain firmly on the people,&#8221; she said. &#8220;Kenyans want relief from the rising cost of living, opportunities for employment, better education and accessible healthcare. They want to live with dignity and have reason to believe in tomorrow.&#8221;

The road to 2027 runs through Mt Kenya. Yesterday, three of its most prominent voices served notice they intend to contest every inch of it.
