EALA MP Winnie Odinga has thrown her weight behind teenage newcomer Morgan Bahati after a video of her enthusiastically lip-syncing to his debut hit Zoza (Nikifika 18) spread rapidly online.

In the video, Winnie is seated in the driver&#039;s seat of a car, dressed in a plain white t-shirt and clear glasses, her seatbelt fastened and her hair pulled back.

Filmed from the dashboard in daylight, she mouths the lyrics, nods to the beat, points and grins her way through the fast Swahili dancehall-afrobeats track. It&#039;s an entirely unscripted moment — just Winnie, in her car, enjoying the song like countless other Kenyans have been doing since it dropped.
