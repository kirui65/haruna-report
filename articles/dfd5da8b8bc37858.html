"2 TAM": Linda Mwananchi office covered with graffiti ahead of Ruto's visit

A Linda Mwananchi Movement office in Kakamega County has been defaced by unknown individuals, with fresh political graffiti appearing on its walls ahead of President William Ruto’s visit to the area.

The building, located in Matungu Sub-county, had been painted in the movement’s distinctive white and blue colours, with its name and logo prominently displayed.

However, unknown individuals reportedly struck during the night, using black paint to cover the Linda Mwananchi branding and replace it with the bold inscription ‘2 TAM’.

The incident comes as Ruto prepares to visit the area as part of his ongoing development tour of Western Kenya , during which he is expected to launch a road project.

Images of the building showed black paint covering sections of the original branding, including the movement&#039;s name and logo.

The words ‘2 TAM’ were prominently written across the affected sections of the building.

The inscription appears to reference calls for Ruto to serve a second presidential term, although those responsible for the graffiti and their intentions had not been established by the time of publication.

It was also unclear whether the incident had been reported to the police or whether any suspects had been identified.

The incident comes as the president undertakes a development tour of the Western region, with several infrastructure projects and government initiatives forming part of his engagements.

During his expected visit to Matungu, Ruto is scheduled to launch a road project, although further details about the project were not immediately available.
