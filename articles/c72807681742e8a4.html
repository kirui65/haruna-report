Visa appoints Esther Waititu as Head of Southern & Eastern Africa

WhatsAppFacebookX

Visa has announced the appointment of Esther Waititu as Senior Vice President and Group Country Manager for Southern & Eastern Africa.

Based in Johannesburg, Esther will lead Visa&#8217;s presence across 24 markets in Southern and Eastern Africa, working with clients, partners, governments and Visa teams across the region to advance digital payments and advance the industry ecosystem. Esther joins Visa with more than two decades of experience across financial services and payments in Africa, with leadership experience spanning mobile money, corporate banking and retail banking.

Most recently, Esther served as Chief Financial Services Officer at Safaricom, where she led M-PESA’s business across payments, credit, savings, investments and insurance. She previously served as Director of Corporate Banking at KCB Bank Group and spent a decade in senior roles at Standard Bank Group, Standard Chartered and Commercial Bank of Africa, now NCBA.

“Southern and Eastern Africa is home to some of the world&#8217;s most dynamic and rapidly growing payments markets, and the opportunities ahead are significant. Esther brings deep industry expertise, a strong understanding of the region and a proven ability to build businesses and partnerships at scale. I am delighted to welcome her to Visa and look forward to working with her as we continue to grow our business and deepen our partnerships across the region,” said Tareq Muhmood, Regional President Visa CEMEA,

“I am excited to join Visa at an important moment for digital commerce across Africa. Southern and Eastern Africa is an incredibly diverse region, with enormous potential to expand access, enable businesses and create new ways for consumers and communities to participate in the digital economy. I look forward to working with Visa&#8217;s teams, clients and partners across the region to build on the strong foundations already in place and unlock the next phase of growth,” said Esther Waititu.
