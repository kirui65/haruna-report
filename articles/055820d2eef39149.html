Kenyans Saddened After KSh 6.5m BMW Z4 With KEA Plate Is Spotted at Salvage Yard

A luxury car that would ordinarily turn heads on Nairobi&#039;s roads has become a subject of online discussion after images emerged showing it parked at a salvage yard.

The vehicle, a BMW Z4 bearing a Kenyan KEA registration number, caught the attention of social media users after posts showing it at the yard circulated online.

The images were shared by several social media platforms, prompting reactions from Kenyans who expressed sadness at the sight of the high-end convertible.

The BMW Z4 is a luxury sports car known for its sleek design, two-seat configuration and convertible roof, making it a distinctive vehicle on Kenyan roads.

Recent listings for BMW Z4 models on Kenyan car marketplaces indicate that some versions can command prices in the region of KSh 6.5 million, depending on their model year, condition and specifications.

For many social media users, seeing such an expensive vehicle at a salvage yard was particularly striking, with some expressing disbelief that a car of its value could end up in such a location.

Many were concerned that a vehicle with that registration must be newly registered in Kenya, which means the owner didn&#039;t get more than six months to enjoy it

The images, however, did not provide an explanation of what had happened to the vehicle.

It was also not immediately clear where the salvage yard was located, how long the BMW had been there or whether it had sustained accident damage.

There was no confirmed information indicating whether the vehicle was an insurance write-off, awaiting repairs, undergoing assessment or being kept at the yard for another reason.

The circumstances surrounding the BMW&#039;s appearance therefore remain unclear, even as its photographs continue to attract attention online.
