Gender-based violence: a public health and human rights concern

WhatsAppFacebookX

News summary

Nairobi— Married at the age of 14, Mparakoni Leabune from Samburu County in northern Kenya was the third wife in a polygamous union. “My husband used to beat me a lot," she says. "That is how I got this leg injury," she says pointing to severe swelling on her left leg. Despite countless medical interventions, Leabune has endured a long and painful road to recovery. "My husband divorced me and now I live with my mother," she says.
