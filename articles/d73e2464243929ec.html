Good conduct document to be processed in three minutes

WhatsAppFacebookX

Residents of Mandera County can now obtain their Certificate of Good Conduct in less than three minutes following the launch of a biometric identification system at the Huduma Centre in Mandera town.

The new system, introduced by the Directorate of Criminal Investigations (DCI), is expected to reduce delays previously experienced by applicants, who sometimes waited for several weeks to receive their clearance certificates.

Speaking during the launch, Mandera East DCIO Hassan Yattani said the new technology would bring services closer to residents and improve the efficiency of the clearance process.

&#8220;Through this new system, residents can now obtain their Certificates of Good Conduct in less than three minutes, compared to the previous process, which could take more than two weeks,&#8221; said Hassan.

The DCI urged residents who require the certificates for employment and other official purposes to visit the Huduma Centre and take advantage of the improved service.

The services in Mandera are part of the Government’s nationwide strategy to decentralize police clearance services and bring them closer to the people.

The service, which was launched today, has already received positive feedback from applicants, with beneficiaries praising the faster processing of certificates and the efficiency of the new system.

Residents who received their certificates during the launch welcomed the new system, saying it would save them time and reduce the challenges associated with the previous application process.

A beneficiary Daud Ali said applicants previously experienced long waiting periods, sometimes extending to several months.

&#8220;We are grateful that we have finally received our certificates within just two minutes. Previously, obtaining them was a major challenge, and we appreciate the authorities for bringing this service closer to us,&#8221; Daud said.

Another beneficiary also welcomed the improvement, saying he had applied and received his certificate on the same day.

The biometric system is expected to make access to police clearance services faster and more convenient for residents of Mandera County.
