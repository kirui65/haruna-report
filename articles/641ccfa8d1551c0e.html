KCB Bank Announces Auction of 19 Vehicles, Prices Start at KSh 850,000

Kenya Commercial Bank (KCB) has opened bidding for 19 vehicles and pieces of equipment, with asking prices running from KSh 850,000 up to KSh 5.95 million.

The bank issued a public notice inviting prospective buyers to view and place bids through its official website, with the exercise set to close on Wednesday, December 2, 2026.

The most affordable item on the list is a 2019 FAW CA1073 truck, registered as KDA 446W, carrying a valuation of KSh 850,000. A 2018 Mitsubishi Canter FE71CB follows at KSh 1.53 million, and a 2022 Toyota Starlet is priced at KSh 1.8 million.

At the higher end of the price range sit several 2023 Mitsubishi Fuso Canter buses. The most expensive unit on the entire list, registered as KDS 404Q, is valued at KSh 5.95 million.

Additional vehicles in the upper bracket include a 2022 Isuzu FRR90 at KSh 4 million, a 2025 Isuzu NMR 85H truck at KSh 3.8 million, a 2020 Shacman M3000 SX3255 tipper at KSh 3.28 million, and a 2022 Isuzu NQR truck at KSh 3.18 million.

All units will be sold on an "as-is, where-is" basis, meaning bidders are expected to personally inspect each vehicle and satisfy themselves about its condition before submitting an offer.

Successful bidders will be required to pay a non-refundable bid fee of KSh 5,000 per vehicle.

KCB noted that financing can be arranged for existing account holders, subject to a credit appraisal.

The vehicles are currently held at storage facilities and bank branches spread across the country, covering locations such as Thika, Ruiru, Gilgil, Nairobi&#039;s Industrial Area, Kericho, Matuu, Kitale , Mombasa, Athi River and Bunyala.

Bids must be submitted through the bank&#039;s website before the Wednesday, December 2, 2026 deadline. Buyers seeking further details can reach KCB directly on 0798 471 848.

The catalogue features commercial trucks, SUVs, saloon cars and other vehicles stored in yards across several locations, including Nairobi, Mombasa, Kisumu, Eldoret , Nakuru, Meru and Kericho.

Prices range from KSh 170,000 for a 2015 Mazda Demio to KSh 6.34 million for a 2019 Toyota Land Cruiser Prado, while other listings include Isuzu trucks, Mitsubishi Fuso lorries, Toyota Hilux and Land Rover Discovery models.
