Bad day in office for Wetang’ula as he hangs on Farouk’s coat tails for political survival

WhatsAppFacebookX

National Assembly Speaker Moses Wetang&#8217;ula is practically fighting for political survival in the Kenyan political space.

Wetangula might previously have been a man of all seasons. But seemingly not for the coming one.

For more than one year now, matters have been turning topsy-turvy for the 69-year-old Senior Counsel.

The going is noticeably going very tough for the Ford Kenya Party Leader.

Both at national and regional levels, the former Sirisia MP is seemingly swimming against the political tides and is literally hanging on the coat-tails of President William Ruto&#8217;s personal assistant Farouk Kibet.

For more than one year now, Wetang&#8217;ula has been striding across the political terrains of Western, with Farouk in tow, advancing the case for Ruto&#8217;s re-election next year.

Within that period, Wetang&#8217;ula has been embroiled in court cases, a political rebellion from his own family, county and political party.

Last year, some members of the civil society took the self-proclaimed Papa Wa Roma to court, demanding his removal as the Ford Kenya Party Leader, arguing that as umpire of a multi-party Legislature, his position in the political outfit is untenable since it is prone to conflict of interest.

Early this year, Wetang&#8217;ula himself threatened to sue the Standard Media Group after one of its publication, The Standard, splashed an article in which it reported that most of the senior public servants from his Bukusu community and Bungoma and Trans-Nzoia counties are his blood relatives who owe their appointments directly to him..

For more than one year now, Wetang&#8217;ula has been campaigning, using parliamentarians from the Western region to do the spade work, to be appointed as Ruto&#8217;s running-mate, a move that appears to have floundered.

The political stars appear to have aligned against Wetang&#8217;ula and his party.

Slightly more than eight months to the next General-Election, Wetang&#8217;ula is noticeably in a very precarious position.

Nairobi Senator Edwin Sifuna who, like Wetang&#8217;ula , hails from Bungoma county, has sprung onto the national political arena and driven the Speaker to the back burner of regional politics.

Sifuna has attracted considerable national attention after he announced his departure from the popular Orange Democratic Movement (ODM) after being ejected as the party&#8217;s secretary-general, has since launched the Linda Mwananchi Movement (LMM) from where he is expected to run for Presidency.

The rise of Sifuna, coupled with previous persistent attacks from Trans-Nzoia Governor George Natembeya, have pushed Wetang&#8217;ula into a tight corner from where he is now fighting for both space and relevance in his own home turf.

Wetang&#8217;ula, through his Ford Kenya party mobilised more than 200, 000 votes for Ruto&#8217;s presidential candidature in 2022 from Bungoma County, the highest number in any of the five Mulembe Nation counties.

Wetang&#8217;ula&#8217;s critics argue that his refusal to relinquish the Ford Kenya leadership portrays him as a sympathizer of the Opposition, a claim that has been insinuated by Nandi Senator Samson Cherargei who early this year warned the Speaker not to dream of being supported again by UDA if he did not merge the outfit with UDA in the same manner that Prime Cabinet Secretary Musalia Mudavadi did in 2024 with his former party, the Amani National Congress (ANC).

The critics maintain that the failure by Wetang&#8217;ula&#8217;s party to field a candidate for the post of Bungoma governor in the 2027 polls elections, in addition to the fact that his young brother, Tim Wanyonyi who is the MP for Westlands on an ODM ticket is contesting for the same seat on Sifuna&#8217;s LMM exposes him as an usher of the Opposition into Bukusuland.

Ford Kenya has for the better part of this year been engulfed fierce internal feuds that have sparked an exodus of officials from the party.

The departure leaves the Speaker in a very vulnerable position. it is against this background that Wetangula has been left to cavort unendingly around Farouk Kibet
