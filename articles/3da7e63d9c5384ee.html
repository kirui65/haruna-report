He followed that post with two more candid admissions, writing:

The content creator had been fighting a rare parasitic eye infection called Acanthamoeba Keratitis, a notoriously difficult condition to diagnose.

He previously revealed that he endured more than 14 medical tests before doctors could identify what was attacking his eye.

His wife, Miss Wanjey, shed more light on the situation during a TikTok livestream on November 11, explaining that the amoeba had burrowed into the cornea, where it forms cysts and keeps multiplying, making treatment extremely complicated.

She also disclosed that medical teams had been treating the wrong condition for close to two months, giving the infection a dangerous window to advance. That delay is what ultimately caused his left eye to turn visibly white and robbed him of all sight in it.

Baba Talisha has confirmed that surgery is on the horizon, and he is now preparing to travel to India for specialist medical attention.

Throughout his health ordeal, Baba Talisha has used his platform to caution others. During a TikTok Live session, he urged Kenyans never to wash their eyes directly with tap water, warning that microscopic organisms present in the water could cause serious damage .

He also shared the same caution on Ghetto Radio, pressing the point that what appears to be ordinary water can harbour invisible risks.
