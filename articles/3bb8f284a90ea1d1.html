Health facility upgrades to expand access to quality healthcare

WhatsAppFacebookX

The Murang’a County Government is stepping up investment in health infrastructure, with new hospital units and dispensaries being opened as part of efforts to improve access to medical services and reduce congestion in existing facilities.

Governor Irungu Kang’ata on Sunday commissioned newly upgraded facilities at Kangema Level-4 Hospital in Muguru Ward, Kangema Sub County, including a male ward, newborn unit, X-ray room and a modern operating theatre.

The hospital’s maternity, postnatal, paediatric and female wards have also been renovated to improve the quality of care and provide patients with a more dignified environment.

Kang’ata said the investments were aimed at strengthening the capacity of public hospitals to handle more patients and provide a wider range of medical services closer to communities.

He noted that upgrading existing facilities, alongside constructing new ones, remained central to the county’s efforts to strengthen healthcare delivery.

The governor also officially opened a newly constructed social hall at the hospital, which will provide space for public participation meetings, community engagements and other forums.

Residents attending the event also benefited from a free medical camp offering general consultations, screening for chronic illnesses and mental health awareness services.

In a separate event, Governor Kang’ata joined residents of Ithanga at Mwania Mbogo in Gatanga Constituency to commission the newly constructed Mwania Mbogo Dispensary.

The facility, the governor said, is equipped with a pharmacy, consultation room, procedure room, laboratory, Maternal and Child Health unit and a room for family planning services. He explained that the dispensary is expected to bring essential primary healthcare services closer to residents, reducing the need to travel long distances to access medical attention.

The latest projects add to the county’s ongoing health infrastructure programme, which has seen investments in hospital expansions, maternity services, specialized units and the construction of new dispensaries.

Other ongoing and recently undertaken projects include the expansion of Murang’a County Referral Hospital through a new wards and outpatient complex, improvements to newborn care capacity and the development of additional theatre and maternity facilities in Level 4 hospitals.

The county has also expanded the use of digital health services, which supports electronic patient records, laboratory services, prescriptions and hospital billing, as well as telemedicine consultations.

Kang’ata noted that the county administration would continue investing in health infrastructure to decentralize services, strengthen primary healthcare and ensure residents receive quality medical attention without travelling long distances.
