Glovo expands investment in Kenya with two new facilities, targets 1,000 additional riders by year-end

WhatsAppFacebookX

Glovo, a leading technology platform operating across Europe, Central Asia and Africa, has opened two new facilities in Nairobi as part of its KSh10 billion investment commitment in Kenya, reinforcing its focus on creating meaningful employment and income-generating opportunities for young Kenyans.

The new Real Time Operations (RTO) office and Glovo Centre mark another step in the company&#8217;s expansion in Kenya, following the opening of its regional office in Nairobi in May 2026, where Glovo announced plans to invest an additional KSh 10 billion in the country by 2030. The facilities are designed to strengthen Glovo&#8217;s operations while providing young people with opportunities to build careers, develop practical skills and improve their financial wellbeing.

The facilities were officially launched during a visit to Kenya by Glovo Co-founder, Sacha Michaud, who engaged with employees, riders and other stakeholders on the company&#8217;s growth plans and continued investment in the market.

Speaking during the launch, Michaud said, &#8220;When we announced our investment commitment earlier this year, our ambition was not simply to grow Glovo&#8217;s presence in Kenya, but to create more opportunities for people to earn, learn and build their futures. These new facilities are part of making that commitment a reality. We want young Kenyans working within our operations to have opportunities to develop their skills and grow their careers, while ensuring riders have access to the training and financial knowledge that can help them make the most of their earnings and pursue their own ambitions.&#8221;

The new Real Time Operations office reflects Kenya&#8217;s growing role as a hub for Glovo&#8217;s operations. Initially established to support six markets, the Nairobi-based department has significantly expanded its mandate and now provides round-the-clock operational support across 21 markets.

The expansion has also increased the office&#8217;s capacity from an initial 100 workstations to accommodate 220 staff members, with plans to significantly increase capacity further as operations grow. This investment is expected to create additional employment opportunities for young Kenyans, particularly those looking to build careers in technology-enabled operations and customer support.

Beyond employment, the RTO office provides an environment where young professionals can gain practical experience, develop operational and problem-solving skills, and build careers within Glovo&#8217;s wider business.

Alongside the RTO office, Glovo officially opened its first Glovo Centre in Kenya, a dedicated facility designed to bring rider onboarding, training, development and support services under one roof.

The Centre will provide riders with access to practical training and skills development opportunities, supporting them in managing their work on the platform while equipping them with knowledge that can be useful beyond deliveries, including in pursuing entrepreneurship and other income-generating opportunities.

To mark the opening, Glovo partnered with Centonomy to deliver a financial literacy training session for 50 riders, equipping them with practical financial management skills to help them better manage their earnings, develop a savings culture and plan for their financial futures.

The occasion also marked the launch of a rider-led Savings and Credit Cooperative Society (SACCO), established by Glovo riders themselves to encourage saving, improve access to credit and strengthen their financial wellbeing. Supported by Glovo, the initiative provides riders with an opportunity to collectively build financial security and access resources that can support their personal and entrepreneurial ambitions.

Through the new Glovo Centre, the company is targeting the onboarding of an additional 1,000 riders by the end of 2026, building on the 2,765 riders already onboarded in the first ten months of the year. The figure represents Glovo&#8217;s highest annual rider onboarding since entering the Kenyan market in 2019, reflecting its continued efforts to expand income-generating opportunities through the platform.

Since launching in Kenya in 2019, Glovo has grown to connect more than 6,000 businesses with customers through its platform, while over 12,400 riders have had the opportunity to earn income through deliveries.

The opening of the two facilities builds on Glovo&#8217;s long-term investment plans for Kenya, with a focus on expanding opportunities for young men and women to participate in the digital economy. Through employment, skills development, financial literacy and rider support initiatives, the company aims to ensure that its continued growth creates opportunities for people to build sustainable livelihoods and pursue their longer-term career and business ambitions.
