Esri Eastern Africa Appoints Faith Nkatha Gitonga As CEO

WhatsAppFacebookX

Esri Eastern Africa has appointed Faith Nkatha Gitonga, a technology and digital transformation leader who most recently worked at the Tony Blair Institute for Global Change (TBI), as its new Chief Executive Officer. She takes over leadership of the regional business on December 1, 2026.

Gitonga joins from TBI, where she served as interim Kenya country director and head of technology and digital transformation for Africa Advisory until April 2026. In that role, she worked on technology-driven reform in the public sector, experience that is directly relevant as governments across the region turn to geospatial intelligence, location analytics and digital mapping to plan infrastructure, manage resources, deliver services and make strategic decisions.

Before TBI, Gitonga led Cellulant’s Kenyan operations as country manager from 2020 to 2023, running one of the country’s prominent fintech businesses. Earlier in her career, she headed Oracle’s digital transformation business for Kenya’s public sector and held senior strategy and banking roles at Sidian Bank and Chase Bank. In all, she brings more than 15 years of leadership experience across financial services, payments, technology and public-sector transformation.

She also brings governance experience from the boards of Capital A Investment Bank, TechnoServe, PAL Network and LVCT Health, spanning business, development and corporate governance.

Esri Eastern Africa distributes geographic information systems (GIS) technology, including the ArcGIS platform, alongside spatial analytics, mapping, consulting and training services. Headquartered in Nairobi, it serves customers in Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania and Ethiopia.
