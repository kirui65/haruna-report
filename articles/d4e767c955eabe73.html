Lulu Hassan Openly Snubs Esther Passaris at KICC, Kenyans Ask Questions

TV presenter and Mvita parliamentary hopeful Lulu Hassan sparked a wave of online chatter after footage from a Nairobi event appeared to show her deliberately skipping Nairobi Woman Rep Esther Passaris during a greeting round.

The incident took place on Thursday, November 26, at the Nairobi Muslim Women&#039;s Forum held at the Kenyatta International Convention Centre (KICC).

The gathering was hosted by Senator Hamida Kibwana and drew a crowd of well-known figures, among them Mining CS Hassan Joho , Mombasa Governor Abdulswamad Shariff Nassir, Senator Karen Nyamu and MP Jalang&#039;o.

Lulu arrived dressed to impress. She turned up in a long, flowing light pink gown with a subtle red and pink floral pattern, topped with a black sequined shawl draped over her shoulders.

A bright magenta gele headwrap completed the look, making her one of the more striking figures in the room.

After walking in, she made her way along the row of leaders, warmly embracing Joho and then Abdulswamad.

The moment things got interesting was when she reached Passaris.

Rather than stopping to greet her, Lulu moved on and instead hugged Nyamu and Jalang&#039;o, leaving Passaris visibly unacknowledged.

Footage from the venue spread quickly online, with viewers split between curiosity and amusement. Many were keen to understand the apparent tension between the two women.

In other news, Lulu and Rashid entertained viewers after disagreeing during a Citizen TV broadcast over whether married partners should explain their social media posts.

Their discussion centered on how song choices could send unintended messages, with Lulu arguing that spouses might question such posts while Rashid defended personal freedom online.
