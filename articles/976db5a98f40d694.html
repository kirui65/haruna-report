The Salaries and Remuneration Commission (SRC) is responsible for reviewing and determining the salary of a deputy county governor in Kenya. Article 230(4)(a) of the Constitution gives the SRC authority to set remuneration for state officers at both national and county levels.

The Third Remuneration and Benefits Review Cycle, covering financial years 2021/2022 to 2024/2025, underpins the current pay structure. The gross monthly salary is KES 652,742 , made up of a basic salary of KES 391,645, a house allowance of KES 100,000, an official commuter allowance, and a salary market adjustment of KES 161,097.

Kenya uses a progressive PAYE tax system with five bands: 10% on the first KES 24,000 of monthly taxable income, 25% on the next KES 8,333, 30% up to KES 500,000, 32.5% up to KES 800,000, and 35% above that.

The mandatory statutory deductions from the deputy governor&#039;s salary are NSSF , SHIF at 2.75% of gross pay, the Affordable Housing Levy at 1.5% of gross pay, and PAYE income tax. A personal relief of KES 2,400 per month is then applied to reduce the final PAYE bill.

Deputy county governors receive a monthly airtime allowance of up to KES 15,000. An annual leave allowance of KES 50,000 is also payable to both governors and deputy governors.

Governors and deputy governors are entitled to official vehicles with an engine capacity not exceeding 3,000cc. The county government is also obliged to provide an official residence with commensurate home utilities and attendants.

Annual medical coverage extends to the deputy governor, one spouse, and up to four dependent children below 25 years. The cover includes inpatient treatment of up to KES 10 million, outpatient cover of KES 300,000, maternity cover of KES 150,000, dental cover of KES 75,000, and optical cover of KES 75,000.

Deputy governors are eligible for a car loan of up to KES 5 million and a mortgage facility of up to KES 25 million.

Retirement benefits offer two options: a service gratuity equal to 31% of annual pensionable income for the period served, or participation in a pension scheme with employer contributions as determined by the SRC. Additionally, deputy governors receive group life and personal accident insurance coverage equivalent to three times their annual pensionable emoluments.

Any remuneration and benefits not specified in the SRC gazette notice are not payable unless subsequently set by the SRC.

Under the Constitution of Kenya, the county governor and the deputy county governor are the chief executive and deputy chief executive of the county, respectively.

When the county governor is absent, the deputy county governor acts as the county governor. Beyond that, the governor may assign the deputy governor any other responsibility or portfolio as a member of the county executive committee.

Constitutionally, the critical mandates of the deputy governor include deputising the governor, filling a vacancy in the governor&#039;s office, and promoting inclusivity in the county.
