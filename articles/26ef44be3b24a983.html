“Gaona 2”: YY puts his daughter’s name on KSh 7m Mercedes-Benzes

Kenyan comedian YY has found a truly extravagant way to show his daughter just how much she means to him, and it involves two Mercedes-Benzes and a pair of personalised number plates.

The entertainer, whose real name is Oliver Otieno, sparked a wave of admiration online after photos emerged showing his luxury cars bearing customised plates named after his daughter, Circe Gaona.

The first image, posted on his Instagram page @yycomedian, features little Circe standing beside a sleek grey Mercedes-Benz E-Class.

A second vehicle, a white Mercedes-Benz C-Class, carries the plate "GAONA 2," continuing the tribute. SPM Buzz confirmed the connection, noting that YY had named both high-end cars in honour of his daughter. The two vehicles are reported to be worth a combined KSh 7 million.

Circe was born in January 2022 to YY and his former partner, Marya Okoth. The couple parted ways in September 2024, but have kept things cordial for the sake of their child, maintaining an active co-parenting arrangement.

YY has long made it clear that fatherhood is central to his life. From school runs to quality time captured on social media, his bond with Circe has been a consistent presence in his public life. Having her name ride with him on every journey he takes is perhaps the most personal statement yet.

Fans flooded the comments section with praise, applauding YY for going the extra mile to honour his daughter in such a grand and costly fashion.
