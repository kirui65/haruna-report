Amnesty International Kenya Opportunities 2026: Technology and Human Rights Campaign Officer and Intern

WhatsAppFacebookX

Amnesty International Kenya is part of the global Amnesty International movement, which works to protect and promote human rights, freedom, dignity and justice for all people.

The organisation brings together activists, researchers, campaigners, volunteers and human rights defenders committed to challenging injustice, defending fundamental freedoms and holding governments and other powerful institutions accountable.

Through research, advocacy, public campaigns and community mobilisation, Amnesty International Kenya addresses human rights violations and promotes respect for international human rights standards.

As technology continues to transform societies, the organisation is strengthening its work on digital rights, online freedoms, privacy, accountability and the human rights implications of emerging technologies.

Amnesty International Kenya is inviting talented, motivated and passionate individuals to explore opportunities within its Technology and Human Rights Programme.

The organisation is currently advertising two opportunities for individuals interested in advancing human rights in the digital age.

These opportunities provide an opportunity to contribute to research, policy development, evidence-based advocacy and campaigns addressing the relationship between technology and human rights.

Position: Technology and Human Rights Campaign Officer Organisation: Amnesty International Kenya Programme Area: Technology and Human Rights Sector: Human Rights Advocacy and Digital Rights

The Technology and Human Rights Campaign Officer will be responsible for designing, coordinating and implementing evidence-based campaigns that promote and protect human rights in the digital environment.

The position focuses on ensuring that both government institutions and technology companies are held accountable for their human rights impacts.

The successful candidate will contribute to campaigns addressing digital rights violations, technological accountability and the protection of fundamental freedoms in an increasingly digital society.

The role centres on developing and implementing effective human rights campaigns through research, advocacy and stakeholder engagement.

The position is particularly relevant to professionals with experience in human rights campaigning, digital rights advocacy, policy influencing and technology-related research.

Position: Technology and Human Rights Intern Organisation: Amnesty International Kenya Programme Area: Technology and Human Rights Sector: Policy Research, Technology and Human Rights

Amnesty International Kenya is also offering an internship opportunity for individuals interested in developing practical experience in human rights research, policy analysis and technology-related advocacy.

The internship provides an opportunity to gain exposure to the research and advocacy processes used to investigate human rights challenges arising from technological developments.

This opportunity is suitable for individuals interested in human rights research, public policy, digital freedoms, technology governance and social justice.
