"Juu ya KSh 20”: Nairobi tout allegedly throws woman out of moving matatu

A Nairobi woman is fighting for her life after a matatu conductor allegedly shoved her from a moving vehicle along Haile Selassie Avenue, reportedly because she was KSh 20 short of the full fare.

The woman, said to be from Vihiga, was travelling when the conductor demanded KSh 100 for the journey. She only had KSh 80 and reportedly begged him to accept what she had.

According to sources, the tout refused and allegedly pushed her out of the vehicle while it was travelling at high speed.

She landed on the tarmac and sustained serious injuries, losing consciousness on impact. Sources indicate she was left in a coma, her condition described as critical.
