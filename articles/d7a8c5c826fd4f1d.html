CEMASTEA trains laboratory technicians as Kenya races to equip senior schools for STEM pathway

WhatsAppFacebookX

The training covers laboratory safety, safe disposal of laboratory waste, risk management, and the preparation of laboratory solutions, reagents and specimens. It also covers laboratory management, inventory preparation and management, and the improvisation of learning resources for schools that lack fully equipped laboratories. (Education News Kenya — https://educationnews.co.ke/news/cemastea-trains-laboratory-technicians-as-kenya-races-to-equip-senior-schools-for-stem-pathway)

CEMASTEA is a Pan-African institution that builds the capacity of STEM teachers and provides specialised teaching and learning resources. It is the headquarters of the SMASE-Africa Association and works with the Ministry of Education on STEM policy.
