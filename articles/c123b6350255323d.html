SportPesa League round eight weekend predictions: Match previews, team news, current form & h2h

There is plenty on the line at the top of the SportPesa Premier League standings as we gear up for a potentially explosive round eight this weekend.

Both AFC Leopards and Police FC have the chance to take charge of the SportPesa League with a win in their particular fixtures scheduled for Saturday afternoon.

Leopards kick off the weekend matches with an away match against newly promoted Migori at the Green Stadium in Awendo, while Police host APS Bomet later in the day.

KCB are currently the SportPesa League leaders with 14 points after seven games, one more than record league winners Gor Mahia in second.

Ingwe are third, level with Gor and Police follow closely with 12 points.

Murang&#8217;a climbed into fifth with 11 points after an impressive 2-0 away win against Bandari on Friday afternoon, inspired by SL top scorer Josphat Lopaga.

In the other Friday fixture, Mathare came back to rescue a valuable point in a 1-1 draw against Kakamega Homeboyz as they maintained their steady rise up the log.

Homeboyz are now winless in their last seven games and continue to lose momentum in 13th place with seven points.

With plenty of action lined up over the weekend, we have a look at the top games and offer our predicted outcome.

This is one of the most intriguing fixtures of the weekend, with unbeaten Leopards travelling to face a Migori Youth side that has impressed considerably in its debut top-flight campaign.

Leopards enter the match third with 13 points from seven matches, just one point behind leaders KCB. Migori are 11th with seven points from six matches.

Ingwe&#8217;s biggest strength has been their defence &#8211; conceding just one goal in seven league matches and have kept six clean sheets, although their attacking output remains a concern.

Migori have also been difficult to beat. They have won one, drawn four and lost just once, while they remain unbeaten at Green Awendo Stadium with one victory and two draws.

Despite their consistent form, four of Ingwe&#8217;s matches have resulted in barren draws and this could be a low-scoring encounter as well.

There is no previous competitive meeting between Migori Youth and AFC Leopards. This will therefore be the first competitive encounter between the two clubs.
