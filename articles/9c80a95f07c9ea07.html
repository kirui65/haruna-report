Suba North MP Millie Odhiambo turned up to Parliament on Wednesday, 18 November, and barely said a word before the internet had plenty to say about her.

A photo shared by the Parliament of Kenya captured the outspoken legislator walking confidently onto the Parliament grounds, flanked by a woman in a sharp grey suit who appeared to be scanning her surroundings with the kind of quiet alertness that screams security detail.

A uniformed officer was also visible near the gate in the background.

Millie herself was dressed in a grey pleated skirt, white top, beige trench coat, and knee-high brown boots. Within hours, the image had pulled in more than 2,000 likes and 325 comments.

The occasion was no ordinary sitting. MPs and Senators convened for a special joint session to hear President William Ruto deliver his fourth State of the Nation Address, constitutionally required under Article 132 and notably his last before the August 2027 General Election.

Ruto arrived at 2:10pm, inspected a Kenya Air Force guard of honour, and entered the chamber at 2:28pm, with Parliament Road having been sealed off since 11am.

In his address, the President touched on economic recovery, affordable housing, the SHA rollout, youth unemployment, and infrastructure.

He also announced that NSSF assets had grown from KSh 288 billion in 2022 to KSh 850 billion. After he wrapped up, legislators from the ruling coalition broke into chants of "tutam, tutam" in a show of support for his re-election campaign.
