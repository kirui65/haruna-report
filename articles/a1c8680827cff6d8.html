Kenya Met Predicts Dry Spell in Most Counties on Saturday, November 28, Lists Areas to Receive Rain

The Kenya Meteorological Service Authority has forecast largely dry weather across most parts of the country, with only a few counties expected to receive light rainfall and isolated thunderstorms on Saturday, November 28.

In its daily weather forecast issued on Friday, November 27, the department indicated that most counties would experience partly cloudy conditions at night, followed by sunny intervals in the morning and afternoon.

The outlook could concern farmers in areas relying on seasonal rainfall for crop production, particularly those whose agricultural activities depend on consistent precipitation.

However, the department did not issue a prolonged dry spell or drought warning, but indicated that rainfall would remain limited to a few locations.

According to the forecast, light showers and thunderstorms are expected in parts of the Coast, Eastern and Central regions.

In the Coast region, Mombasa, Kilifi, Tana River and Taita Taveta are among the counties likely to receive some rainfall. Kwale could also experience isolated light showers in the morning.

In the Eastern region, Marsabit, Meru, Tharaka-Nithi, Embu, Kitui, Machakos and Makueni are expected to receive varying amounts of light rainfall, with some areas likely to experience thunderstorms in the afternoon.

Murang&#039;a and Kiambu are also expected to receive light showers and thunderstorms in a few places during the afternoon.

Kakamega is forecast to experience isolated showers and thunderstorms at night, although sunny intervals are expected during the day.

The rest of the counties, including Nairobi, Uasin Gishu, Nakuru, Kisumu , Busia, Siaya, Kericho, Bomet and Trans Nzoia, are expected to experience mainly sunny intervals.

Besides the rainfall outlook, the department warned of strong southerly to southeasterly winds exceeding 25 knots (12.5 metres per second) in parts of Marsabit and Turkana counties.

Residents in the affected areas should take precautions against the potentially disruptive winds.

The forecast also showed significant temperature variations across the country. Mandera is expected to record one of the highest maximum temperatures at 36°C, alongside Tana River and Turkana, which will experience similarly hot conditions.

On the other hand, Nyandarua is expected to record a minimum temperature of 11°C, making it one of the coldest counties during the forecast period.

Uasin Gishu, Narok and Kericho are expected to record minimum temperatures of 12°C. The forecast is valid from 9pm on Friday, November 27, to 9pm on Saturday, November 28.
