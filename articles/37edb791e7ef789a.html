Portugal honour Cristiano Ronaldo with special number 7 shirt redesign

WhatsAppFacebookX

News summary

Portugal have introduced a special redesign of their iconic number 7 shirt as a permanent tribute to Cristiano Ronaldo, celebrating the extraordinary legacy of a man who transcended football and became the defining symbol of Portuguese sporting greatness across more than two decades of international service.
