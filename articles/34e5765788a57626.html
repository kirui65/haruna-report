Kenya, Zimbabwe strengthen bilateral ties through media cooperation

WhatsAppFacebookX

Kenya and Zimbabwe have strengthened bilateral cooperation in the media and information sector through the signing of a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between the Kenya News Agency (KNA) and the Zimbabwe Inter-African News Agency (NEW ZIANA).

The agreement was signed by Kenya’s Principal Secretary for Broadcasting and Telecommunications, Stephen Isaboke, and Zimbabwe’s Permanent Secretary for Information, Publicity and Broadcasting Services, Nick Ndabaningi Mangwana, at State House, Nairobi, during the State Visit of the President of the Republic of Zimbabwe to Kenya from 26th to 27th November 2026.

The signing formed part of broader bilateral engagements aimed at deepening cooperation across various sectors and formalising agreements of mutual strategic interest to the two nations.

The MoU establishes a framework for the exchange of news and multimedia content, professional capacity building, sharing of knowledge and technical expertise, and collaboration in journalism and information and communication technology (ICT).

The partnership seeks to strengthen institutional collaboration between the two national news agencies, enhance people-to-people relations and increase the visibility of African perspectives on regional and global media platforms.

PS Isaboke underscored the importance of intra-African media cooperation in enabling the continent to tell its own stories, shape its narratives and showcase its development, innovation, cultural diversity and economic opportunities.

The agreement marks a significant step in advancing Kenya–Zimbabwe bilateral relations, strengthening Africa’s information ecosystem and promoting the broader aspirations of African integration.
