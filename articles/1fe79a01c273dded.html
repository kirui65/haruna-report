Kenya Power Announces Wednesday Blackouts in Eight Counties

WhatsAppFacebookX

Kenya Power has announced scheduled electricity interruptions in eight counties across the country on Wednesday, November 25, as part of planned maintenance works.

In a notice issued on Tuesday, November 24, the company said the outages would affect selected areas in Nairobi, Machakos, Trans Nzoia, Uasin Gishu, Kiambu, Kisii, Migori and Kwale counties.

In Nairobi County, the interruption will affect parts of Kitsuru, including Tate Close, Kitsuru Road, Forward Close and neighbouring areas.

In Ruaraka, the affected locations will include Garden City Mall, KBL Corporate Offices, Cabro City, GSU, Qwetu Hostels and adjacent customers.

In Machakos County, the planned outage will affect Wote and surrounding areas, including parts of Kathonzweni Market, Mulili, Mbuvo, Kiangini, Kalii, Kitise, Kithuki, Kalawa, Miangeni, Miseke, Nthia, Thavu, Mavindini Kavingo, Tamu Tamu, Mau Eli, Kathamboni, Yemulwa and Yikiuki.

In Trans Nzoia County, the interruption will affect Chepchoina, Molem, Kimondo, Soko Mjinga, GSU, Suam Orchard, Suam Border and neighbouring areas.

Meanwhile, in Uasin Gishu County, Munyaka, Hawaii and adjacent areas will experience the scheduled power interruption.

In Kiambu County, the affected areas will include Kimende, Soko Mjinga and Escarpment, covering Mathore, Kongo Thiria, Rukuma, Maingi, Magina, Kinale, Kirasha, Kwa Edward and Sulmac.

In Kisii County, the interruption will affect Nyabururu, Embassy, Nyamataro, Nyakoe, Mosocho, Matieko, Rugaquarry, Siara, Keore, Kioge, Irianyi, Riotero, Rera, Nyando, Quarry, Sombogo Factory, Gesoni, Gesabakwa, Nyagesai, Kiareni, Gesangora, Marani Market and Rioma.

In Migori County, the affected areas will include Sony Sugar Company, the entire Awendo Town, Ranen, Kokuro, Dede, Nyakuru, Ombasa, Chamgi Wadu, Kanyimach, Siany, Rabuor, Saka, Aora Jope, Ngóngá, Rinya, Manyatta and Komolorume.

In Kwale County, the planned outage will affect Tiwi Beach, Tiwi KD, Amani Tiwi, Ras Kadini, BAT, Jacks Place, Twiga Lodge, Marijani, Clifftop, Hillpark, Tiwi Maweni, Old Kwale Road, Mnyenzi, Chikola, Tiwi Baptist, Bixa Plant, Chai Primary School and Tiwi Water Boreholes C, E and 8.

Kenya Power indicated that the interruptions in Kiambu, Kisii, Migori and Kwale counties would run from 9:00 am to 5:00 pm.

Customers in the affected areas have been advised to plan accordingly as the company undertakes the scheduled maintenance works.
