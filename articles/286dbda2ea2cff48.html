Citizen TV journalist Stephen Letoo has entered the heated conversation around Royal Media Services&#039; (RMS) decision to p ut a price tag on its YouTube livestreams , and he was not holding back.

RMS recently introduced a KSh 350 monthly subscription fee for viewers watching Citizen TV, Inooro TV and Ramogi TV live on YouTube.

The move drew swift backlash from viewers , many of whom pointed out that all three stations remain freely available via television decoders, making the online charge feel like an unnecessary extra.

Competing broadcasters were quick to seize the moment. NTV shared a cheeky Facebook poster welcoming anyone considering the switch, reminding viewers that its livestream remains completely free.

KTN News also joined in, posting a lighthearted reminder that its coverage costs nothing on YouTube.

State broadcaster KBC Channel 1 added its own dose of humour to the moment, jokingly welcoming back its "prodigal sons" and pointing viewers towards its own free livestream.

The online chatter appeared to translate into real audience movement.
