President Ruto begins 10-day development tour of Western Kenya

WhatsAppFacebookX

President William Ruto has commenced his 10-day development tour of Western Kenya, with visits to Busia, Kakamega, Vihiga and Bungoma counties as his administration seeks to strengthen its presence in the region ahead of the 2027 General Election.

The tour follows a week-long visit to the region in March and comes after Ruto met Western Kenya leaders on Tuesday, November 17, to review ongoing projects and discuss new development priorities.

The meeting, led by National Assembly Speaker Moses Wetang&#8217;ula, brought together governors, MPs and senior government officials from the four counties. Discussions focused on healthcare, road infrastructure, affordable housing, industrial development and education.

Ruto highlighted reforms in the sugar sector, saying the revival of struggling public factories had improved farmers&#8217; earnings and ensured workers received regular salaries.

Prime Cabinet Secretary Musalia Mudavadi and Wetang&#8217;ula have been at the forefront of efforts to rally political support for Ruto in Western Kenya, where other regional leaders have aligned themselves with the opposition.

Mudavadi said the government remained committed to ensuring Western Kenya benefits from national development programmes and that all communities are included in the country&#8217;s development agenda.
