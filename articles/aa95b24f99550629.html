Geothermal Company Announces New Tenders for Kenyans to Apply, Minimum KSh 100k Needed

The Geothermal Development Company (GDC) has floated 12 new tenders covering a broad range of goods and services, with the lowest entry requirement set at KSh 100,000 in tender security .

The tenders are spread across GDC&#039;s three operational regions: its Nairobi headquarters, the Central Rift area centred on the Menengai Geothermal Project in Nakuru , and the North Rift around the Baringo-Silali Geothermal Project.

Submission deadlines run from December 1 to December 10, 2026, and all bids must be lodged electronically through the government&#039;s e-GP portal at egpkenya.go.ke.

Three of the 12 tenders are for security services contracts running two years, one each for the Nairobi Office and Suswa Geothermal Field (KSh 500,000 tender security), the Central Rift (KSh 1,000,000), and the North Rift (KSh 1,000,000). Bidders may apply for only one of the three security contracts.

The remaining tenders cover supplies and services required for geothermal drilling and power generation operations.

The smallest tender security, KSh 100,000, applies to the supply of electrical cables for Menengai.

Other requirements include KSh 150,000 for 8-inch pipeline accessories, KSh 300,000 for a high-capacity trailer, KSh 390,000 for 1mm thick black pond liners, KSh 400,000 for crane hire services, and KSh 500,000 each for two separate drilling foam tenders.

A tender for 3MVA generator batteries is set aside for women-owned enterprises registered under the Access to Government Procurement Opportunities (AGPO) programme.

Instead of a cash security deposit, qualifying bidders need only submit a tender securing declaration form.

The three-year framework agreement for hotel, conference, and outside catering services carries no tender security requirement at all.

The first deadlines fall on December 1, 2026, at 2:30 p.m., covering crane hire and pond liner supply tenders. Four tenders close on December 7, including the Menengai electrical cables tender at 10:30 a.m. and the two drilling foam tenders at 2:30 p.m.

The hospitality framework agreement has the latest closing date, December 10 at 12:00 p.m.

Prospective bidders for the three security tenders must attend mandatory site visits scheduled for November 23 to 25, 2026.

For the Nairobi and Suswa contract, bidders assemble at Kawi House in South C at 8:00 a.m. on November 23 before proceeding to the Suswa Geothermal Field.

The Central Rift visit takes place at the Menengai project on November 24, with bidders gathering at the GDC Polo Center Office on Kenyatta Avenue in Nakuru at 9:00 a.m.

The North Rift visit follows on November 25 at the Baringo-Silali project, with assembly at the GDC Kapkerwa Office along the Marigat-Loruk Road at 8:30 a.m.

Bidders who attended the mandatory site visit for the previous security tender, reference GDC/891/0066/2025-26, are exempt from attending again, provided they attach their earlier site visit certificate.

In other news, the Teachers Service Commission (TSC) has o rganised sensitisation meetings for businesses owned by youth, women and persons with disabilities across eight regions, covering 29 counties between November 18 and 27, 2026.

The sessions aim to help suppliers understand the Access to Government Procurement Opportunities (AGPO) framework, tender compliance requirements, submission procedures and procurement opportunities available through the Commission.

Meetings will be held at designated venues, including the TSC headquarters in Nairobi, NEP Girls in Garissa, Coast Girls in Mombasa and other schools across the regions, with all sessions starting at 10am.

Interested businesses must register by emailing their firm name, postal address, tender name, telephone number and email address to ddprocurement@tsc.go.ke before attending.
